What is stored

This site is made and run by one person, Mohamad Khorbatli. Here is everything it keeps about you, and what you can do about it. It is short on purpose.

هذا الموقع يديره شخص واحد. في ما يلي كل ما يُحفَظ عنك، وما يمكنك فعله به.

You need a username and a password. Nothing else.

Everything else on the sign-up form is optional and the site works the same without it. If you leave a field empty, nothing is stored in it.

If you tell me about yourself, it is used to suggest courses

Your country, education, work, language level and interests are used for one thing: deciding which courses are worth suggesting to you. They are not sold, not shared, and not sent to any advertiser. There are no advertising or tracking scripts anywhere on this site.

I do not ask for your phone number, your address, or your date of birth. If you want to tell me something else, there is a free-text box, and what goes in it is up to you.

What the site records while you are signed in

Today, it records when you sign in and out, and when you change your own details. That is all.

As each course is connected to accounts, it will also record which lessons you open and finish and how far through you got, which questions you answered and whether the answer was right, which words you looked up, what you searched for, and which pages you visited here. This page will say so when it happens rather than afterwards.

It is there so the site can put you back where you stopped, mark your work, and eventually suggest what to do next. It is not shared with anyone.

While you are signed out, nothing you do reaches me at all.

ما دمت غير مسجَّل الدخول، لا يصلني شيء مما تفعله.

Your password

It is stored in a scrambled form that cannot be turned back into your password. I cannot read it, and neither can anyone who ever got hold of the database. That is also why nobody can tell you what your password was — only help you set a new one.

Email is optional, and this is the catch

If you do not give an email address, your password can never be reset. There is no other way to prove the account is yours. Not by asking me, not by any other route. If you give an address and confirm it, you can reset your password by email; an address you never confirmed does not count, because if it was mistyped the reset would go to a stranger.

إن لم تُدخل بريداً، لا يمكن إعادة تعيين كلمة المرور أبداً.

While the trial is running, no email is being sent yet. That means an address you give cannot be confirmed yet, so for now no password can be reset by email at all — give me a ring and I will reset it for you. Your address is stored and will be confirmable as soon as sending is switched on, and this paragraph will go.

لا يُرسَل بريد بعد خلال الفترة التجريبية، فلا يمكن حالياً إعادة تعيين كلمة المرور بالبريد — اتصل بي وسأعيد تعيينها.

Where it is kept

On a database I rent in the United States, from a company called Render. The site's own server reaches it over a private network, so nothing a visitor does touches it directly. There is also an administrative connection that I use myself, protected by a password. Traffic between your browser and the site is encrypted.

Some things stay in your own browser and never reach me at all: your place in a lesson, which answers you have revealed, and the key for the assistant if you use it.

You can take it all, and you can delete it

  • Download it. One button on your account page gives you a file with everything — your profile, your progress, and your activity.
  • Delete it. One button removes your profile and your progress permanently. Two things stay: an empty record with no name, no email and no password, and the activity list above with that record's number against it. Neither holds anything that points back to you, and they stay so the overall totals for the site do not break. If you would rather the activity went too, phone me and I will remove it.
  • Change it. Every optional field can be edited or emptied at any time.

Children

This site is written for adults and for older students. I do not knowingly keep accounts for children under 13. If you believe a child has created one, tell me and I will remove it.

If this changes

If I ever store something new, this page changes first and says so at the top. I will not start selling or sharing your information; if that ever became the plan, the honest thing would be to tell you and let you delete your account before it happened.
