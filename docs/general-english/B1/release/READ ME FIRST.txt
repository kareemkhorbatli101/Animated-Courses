English for Daily Life - B1
==========================

2 volume(s), 1 unit(s) written, 41 figures a unit.

  EFDL-B1.1-LookingBack                Volume 1, Looking Back
  EFDL-B1.2-MakingYourselfClear        Volume 2, Making Yourself Clear
  ...-AnswerKey-...                    the same key, printable on its own
  units/                               each unit on its own, DOCX and PDF
  covers/                              the covers at 300 DPI
  reports/                             the check report for each volume

Each book is one file: the units in order, then the answer key, with
the front and back covers as the first and last pages. DOCX is the
editable source; the PDF is what it prints as.

Every unit carries 41 figures, every one of them a teaching device
the text refers to. The unit opener and both covers fill a page.
