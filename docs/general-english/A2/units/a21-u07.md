**Part 2: Grammar Focus Box**

*Figure 7.12 · Two things, or one out of many.*

| | **Form** | **Use** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Short word** | + -er · + -est | one or two beats | cheap → cheaper → the cheapest |
| **Long word** | more · the most | three beats or more | expensive → more expensive |
| **Irregular** | good → better → the best | a short list | bad → worse → the worst |
| **than / in** | -er **than** · the -est **in** | which goes where | warmer **than** · the best **in** the street |

**Remember:** *than* after a comparative; *the* before a superlative.

> **Watch out!** ✗ *It is more cheaper.* → ✓ *It is cheaper.* (one is enough). ✗ *It is the most cheap shop.* → ✓ *It is the cheapest shop.* (*cheap* is short).

**Part 8 · Global Story — The Street That Repairs Everything**

***[PLUS] Plus track · pre-read / discuss***

*Figure 7.35 · A broken thing, and the two roads out of it.*

Before you read: what happens to a broken thing in your town?

> **Gloss:** jua kali = an open-air workshop in Kenya, from the words for hot sun · informal = outside the official system · skill = something you can do well because you learned it · motor = the part that makes a machine go

> In Nairobi there is a part of the city where almost nothing is thrown away. People call it jua kali, which means hot sun, because the workshops are in the open air. A man takes the motor out of a dead fridge and puts it into a working one. A woman cuts a new sole for a shoe out of an old tyre. That shoe then lasts longer than the one it came from. There are thousands of these workshops and almost none of them has an office or a sign. Visitors from richer countries often call it poor, and part of it is. But the skill in those streets is real and hard to find. Most of Europe lost it in two generations. The cheapest thing is still the thing you already own; what Nairobi kept is the knowledge of how to keep it.

> Mr Okonkwo’s coat is older than Dani. It was expensive when he bought it, and he says that was the point. A woman two streets away relined it twice, and she no longer takes new work.
