## Unit 3 · A Place of My Own

**3A** · Four people looking for a flat.

> 1 — S: I want somewhere near the bus garage. My flat now is too far away.
> 2 — F: I have got a flat already, but it is not big enough for my books.
> 3 — L: I am looking for two rooms. I have just moved back from abroad.
> 4 — M: I am not looking. I live in flat 12 and I look after the others.

**3B** · What the landlord said.

> V: Flat 11 is free now. It is furnished and the rent is six hundred.
> V: Flat 4 is free from June. It has not got a garage. Four hundred and fifty.
> V: Flat 9 is free from August. Eight hundred and twenty, and it is very bright.

**3C** · A second flat from above.

> M: This one is a studio, so there are three rooms in all.
> M: The bed is in the studio, near the window.
> M: Next to the bathroom there is a storage room.
> M: There is no separate bedroom. That is why the rent is lower.

**3D** · Four more things to hear.

> V: Please close the window. · The door does not lock properly. ·
>   There is one chair in the kitchen. · The desk is too big for the room.
