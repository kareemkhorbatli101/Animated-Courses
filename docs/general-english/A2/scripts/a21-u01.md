## Unit 1 · Back on Hill Road

**3A** · Four voices on the street.

> 1 — N: Lili! You're back! Come in, sit down. I'll make you a coffee.
> 2 — S: I drive the twelve every day. Nothing changed while you were away.
> 3 — F: I was at the clinic all year. We have a new doctor now.
> 4 — B: The shop is the same. I still open at eight and close at six.

**3B** · What Lili did in Spain.

> L: I went to Madrid last June and I started a course.
> L: In October I worked in a hotel. I was at the desk every evening.
> L: Then in March I moved to the coast and I taught English.
> L: And this June I came home.

**3C** · The week she came back.

> L: On Wednesday I packed. It took all day.
> L: On Thursday I flew. The plane left at seven in the morning.
> L: And on Friday I arrived here.
> N: I was at the cafe all week, except Thursday. Omar was at school every day.

**3D** · Four words you will hear.

> V: The plane left at seven. · My suitcase was very heavy. ·
>   I lost my phone at the airport. · Rosa has my key.
