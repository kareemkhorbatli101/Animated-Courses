## Unit 9 · Feeling Better

**3A** · Four people at the clinic.

> 1 — O: My throat is sore, and I cough all night. I feel weak.
> 2 — R: I fell on the stairs last week. My ankle is swollen now.
> 3 — M: I cut my hand at work. The nurse put a bandage on it.
> 4 — L: Nothing hurts. I came for the vaccine, and I booked it last week.

**3B** · The clinic list on Thursday.

> N: Ten past nine, O. Hassan. A sore throat and a cough since Monday.
> N: Half past nine, R. Alvarez. A swollen ankle after a fall on the stairs.
> N: Ten o'clock, M. Nowak, in room three. An itchy rash on his arm.

**3C** · The card the nurse fills in.

> F: R. Alvarez. The ankle, yes. It is swollen, and it hurts when you walk.
> F: Your temperature is thirty-seven point one. That is normal.
> F: So, you should rest. No walking for two days. Ring me if it gets worse.

**3D** · Four things in the pharmacy.

> V: The letter from the clinic came this morning. · One vitamin tablet a day. ·
>   A bottle of water, cold from the fridge. · A cup of tea and then bed.
