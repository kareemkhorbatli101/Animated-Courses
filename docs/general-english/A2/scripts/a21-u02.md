## Unit 2 · Every Day and Right Now

**3A** · Four voices at the cafe.

> 1 — B: I am at the counter. I am counting the money before we close.
> 2 — L: I am clearing the tables. I normally do this at the end of a shift.
> 3 — R: I am waiting for my coffee. I come here every Tuesday morning.
> 4 — S: I am not working now. I am having my break before the next bus.

**3B** · What the supervisor wrote.

> M: At six, Nadia started the bread. She does that every morning.
> M: At half past nine, Lili cleaned the tables.
> M: At one o'clock, Ben counted the money.
> M: And at eight in the evening I locked the door myself.

**3C** · The hours they worked.

> M: Last week Nadia worked eight hours on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday.
> M: She did not work on Thursday.
> M: Ben worked six hours on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.
> M: Lili worked six hours on Monday and six on Thursday. Twelve in all.

**3D** · Four more things to hear.

> V: Can I have a cup of tea? · The bread is still warm. ·
>   I found a coin under the table. · Marek is writing on the board.
