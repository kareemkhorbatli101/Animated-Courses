## Unit 8 · At Work

**3A** · Four people about their work.

> 1 — B: In the shop I must count the money every evening before I lock up.
> 2 — R: I do not work now. I get my pension, so I do not have to go anywhere.
> 3 — L: I am new. I must wear a badge, and I should ask before I decide.
> 4 — O: At school we must not use a phone in the classroom. That is the rule.

**3B** · The interview times.

> V: At half past nine we are seeing A. Silva, for the assistant job, in room two.
> V: At a quarter past ten, L. Chen, also for assistant, also room two.
> V: And at eleven, J. Kowalski, for the driver job. That one is in room four.

**3C** · The rules on the board.

> M: Everyone must wear a badge. Nobody must smoke inside. Everyone should
>   arrive a few minutes early.
> M: In the kitchen you must wash your hands, you must not use a phone, and
>   you should tie your hair back.
> M: Drivers must carry a licence, must not use a phone, and should check the
>   oil before the first journey.

**3D** · Four more things to hear.
