## Unit 5 · What Happened

**3A** · Four witnesses.

> 1 — B: I was standing at the shop door. I heard a shout from the stairs.
> 2 — L: I was coming down behind her. I saw Rosa fall.
> 3 — O: I was outside with my ball. I saw the ambulance arrive.
> 4 — N: I was in the cafe all morning. I am sorry, I saw nothing.

**3B** · What each witness said.

> V: Ben was at the shop door and he heard a shout.
> V: Lili was on the stairs and she saw Rosa fall.
> V: Omar was outside and he saw the ambulance.
> V: Nadia was in the cafe. She saw nothing at all.

**3C** · The times that morning.

> M: At twenty to nine Rosa was in the shop.
> M: At five to nine she was on the stairs. That is when it happened.
> M: At five past nine she was sitting down, and the ambulance arrived.
> M: I was on the phone then. By twenty past she was on her way to hospital.

**3D** · Four more things to hear.

> V: The box was too heavy for her. · A bottle broke on the step. ·
>   We put a chair at the bottom of the stairs. · Her bag fell with her.
