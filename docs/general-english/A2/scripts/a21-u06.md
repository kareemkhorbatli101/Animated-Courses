## Unit 6 · Getting There

**3A** · Four passengers.

> 1 — B: I never travel far. I walk to the shop in four minutes.
> 2 — F: I take the train to the city once a month, for a meeting.
> 3 — M: I have to drive. I carry tools, so I cannot use the bus.
> 4 — N: I come on the 12 every morning. Sam always says good morning.

**3B** · What the announcement said.

> V: The nine fifteen to the coast leaves from platform two. It is on time.
> V: The nine forty to the city leaves from platform one. It is ten minutes late.
> V: The ten oh five to the airport is cancelled. Take the bus from outside.

**3C** · The week of travel.

> S: I drive the 12 on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.
> L: I walk on Monday and Wednesday, I take the bus on Tuesday,
>   and on Thursday I go by train.
> O: I cycle to school every day except Wednesday. On Wednesday I stay at home.

**3D** · Four more things to hear.

> V: My suitcase is too heavy for the shelf. · Show your passport at the gate. ·
>   One ticket to the coast, please. · I left my bag on the train.
