## Unit 4 · How Much, How Many

**3A** · Four people at the bank.

> 1 — R: I am here for my pension. It arrives on the third every month.
> 2 — O: My mother gave me a little money for my birthday. I want to save it.
> 3 — B: I pay the shop money in on Fridays. It is part of my job.
> 4 — F: I have not got much time. I only want a new card.

**3B** · What they paid in May.

> V: In May, Lili paid six hundred for rent, two hundred and ten for food,
>   and seventy-two for travel.
> V: Ben paid four hundred and eighty for rent, a hundred and eighty for food,
>   and forty for travel.
> V: Rosa paid nothing for rent. She paid a hundred and forty for food and
>   eighteen for travel.

**3C** · The prices in the shop.

> B: A small coffee is one eighty, a medium is two twenty, a large is two sixty.
> B: Bread is one ten for a small loaf and one sixty for a medium. We have not
>   got a large one.
> B: Juice is two pounds, two forty, and two ninety for the large.

**3D** · Four more things to hear.

> V: There is not much milk left. · I only want one egg. ·
>   An apple is forty pence. · Two tickets to Greenfield, please.
