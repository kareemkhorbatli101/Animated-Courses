## Unit 10 · The Way I Am

**3A** · Four people describe a friend.

> 1 — N: My friend talks to everybody. She is never quiet for a minute.
> 2 — B: Mine is curious. He asks about everything, and he remembers it.
> 3 — O: My friend is shy. He listens more than he speaks, and I like that.
> 4 — M: My friend works slowly, but he checks every job twice. He is reliable.

**3B** · The club list on Saturday.

> V: Ten o'clock, L. Chen, room one. She is shy, so sit with her at first.
> V: Half past eleven, O. Hassan, room two. Very talkative, and always cheerful.
> V: Two o'clock, M. Nowak, room one. Sensible, and he never forgets the money.

**3C** · The note the landlord writes.

> L: The new tenant is L. Chen. She is quiet and tidy, and she is no trouble.
> L: She is out most evenings. She works late, so you hardly see her.
> L: One thing to know: she plays the guitar on Sunday. Not loudly, but she does.

**3D** · Four things in her room.

> V: She reads every evening before she sleeps. · She plays at parties, and
>   she is good. · She trains on Saturday with the club. · She waters it every
>   day, and it is the only green thing here.
