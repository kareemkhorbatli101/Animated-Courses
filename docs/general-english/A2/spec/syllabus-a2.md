# EFDL A2 — syllabus

Twenty units, forty grammar points, twenty functional sets, twenty phonology
points. Sourced from **American Headway Level 2**'s A2 band, because EFDL A1
already covers the whole of *Starter* and essentially all of *Level 1*.

It stops short of B1 on purpose: no past perfect, no reported speech, no
second conditional, no passive beyond the simple present.

## A2.1 — *Getting On*

| Unit | Title | Grammar | Phonology | Real-World File |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Back on Hill Road | tense review: be, present simple, past simple, will | contractions in speech | a registration form |
| 2 | Every Day and Right Now | present simple vs present continuous; state verbs | -s and -ing endings | a work rota |
| 3 | A Place of My Own | have got; too and enough | weak *of*, weak *a* | a rental advert |
| 4 | How Much, How Many | much, many, a lot of, a few, a little | stress in long numbers | a bank statement |
| 5 | What Happened | past simple vs past continuous; when and while | *was/were* weak forms | an incident report |
| 6 | Getting There | prepositions of movement; have to, don't have to | linking across words | a travel timetable |
| 7 | Making Plans | will vs going to vs present continuous for arrangements | *gonna*, *going to* | a calendar invitation |
| 8 | At Work | must, mustn't, should | word stress in jobs | a job advert |
| 9 | Feeling Better | should and shouldn't for advice | sentence stress | a pharmacy label |
| 10 | The Way I Am | adverbs of manner; frequency; not as … as | the schwa in adverbs | a personality quiz |
