# Picture File

Every figure in the course: its number, the part it belongs to, the job it does,
its caption, and alt text for a reader who cannot see it.

The four jobs are **data** (the figure carries the information the task needs),
**contrast** (it shows one form against another), **lexis** (it shows what a word
means) and **document** (it reproduces a thing from the world: a form, a receipt,
a notice). There is no fifth job, and no figure is decoration.

**440 figures · 20 units**
