# Phrase File

The things you say, grouped by what you are doing. Every set comes from the
Part 7 Real-World File of its unit, so the phrases here are the phrases the
course practises.

---

## 1 · Meeting someone

Hello. · Hi. · Good morning. · Good afternoon. · Good evening.
What's your name? — My name is Nadia.
How do you spell it? — N-A-D-I-A.
Where are you from? — I'm from Egypt.
Nice to meet you. · Goodbye. · Bye. · See you tomorrow.

## 2 · In the class

What's this in English? — It's a pen.
How do you say this in English?
Can you say it again, please?
I don't understand. · Sorry, I don't know.
Open your book. · Close the door, please.

## 3 · Family and people

Who's this? — This is my brother.
How old is he? — He's twelve.
Are you married? — Yes, I am. · No, I'm not.
This is Marek's mother. · These are their children.

## 4 · Where you live

Where do you live? — I live at 14 Hill Road.
What's your address? · What's your phone number?
How many rooms are there? — There are three.
Is there a lift? — Yes, there is.

## 5 · Times and days

What time is it? — It's half past eight.
What time do you start? — At nine o'clock.
When are you free? — On Friday.
See you at seven.

## 6 · Jobs

What do you do? — I'm a nurse.
Where do you work? — At the clinic.
What time does she finish? — At six.
Does he work on Saturday? — No, he doesn't.

## 7 · Food and drink

I'm hungry. · I'm thirsty.
What would you like? — Some bread, please.
Is there any milk? — No, there isn't any.
I like tea. I don't like coffee.
The bill, please.

## 8 · In the town

Excuse me, where is the bank?
Go straight on. · Turn left. · Turn right. · Cross the road.
It's opposite the school. · It's next to the cafe.
Is it far? — No, it's near.

## 9 · What you can do

Can you swim? — Yes, I can.
Can you help me, please? — Of course.
I can't drive. · I can speak two languages.

## 10 · Last weekend

Where were you? — I was at home.
How was it? — It was great.
It was terrible. · It was a very good day.

## 11 · Yesterday

What did you do yesterday? — I worked.
I walked to the park. · She cooked dinner.
I watched a film. · We listened to music.

## 12 · Last year

Where did you go? — I went to Spain.
What did you see? — I saw the sea.
Did you have a good time? — Yes, I did.
I didn't go to the beach.

## 13 · Right now

What are you doing? — I'm cooking.
She's waiting outside. · They're working now.
Are you listening? — Yes, I am.

## 14 · Next week

What are you going to do? — I'm going to travel.
When are you going to fly? — Tomorrow.
I'm going to pack tonight.
Have a good trip!

## 15 · Comparing two things

Which one is cheaper? — The green one.
This coat is bigger than that one.
Which do you want? — The red one, please.
This book is more difficult.

## 16 · Free time

What do you like doing? — I like swimming.
Do you like music? — Yes, I love it.
Do you hate waiting? — Yes, I do.
There's a club on Monday. Everyone can come.

## 17 · Buying and paying

I'd like a coffee, please.
Would you like anything else? — No, thanks.
How much is it? — That's eight pounds.
How would you like to pay? — By card, please.
Have you got a bigger one?
Here you are. · Your change is two pounds. · Thank you. · You're welcome.

## 18 · The best in town

Which is the best cafe? — The one on Hill Road.
What's the oldest building here?
Which street is the quietest?
It's the most beautiful place I know.

## 19 · How you feel

How are you? — Not very well.
What's the matter? — I've got a headache.
Have you got a temperature? — No, I haven't.
You should go to bed. · You shouldn't go to work.
Get well soon. · I hope you're better soon.

## 20 · Saying goodbye

Good luck! — Thank you!
I will miss you. — I will miss you too.
Keep in touch. — I promise.
See you soon! · See you in June!
All the best. · Have a wonderful time.
