# Grammar File

One page for each grammar point, in the order the course teaches it. The
wording is A1 wording. Eight terms are used and no others: **verb**, **noun**,
**question**, **answer**, **short form**, **past**, **now** and **next**.

---

## Unit 1 · am, is, are

| Person | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I **am** Nadia. |
| you, we, they | are | You **are** here. |
| he, she, it | is | She **is** a student. |

Short forms: I'm · you're · he's · she's · we're · they're.

**my, your, his, her** go in front of a noun: **my** name, **her** bag.

---

## Unit 2 · Questions and short answers

Turn the sentence round to ask:

| Sentence | Question |
|---|---|
| You are a teacher. | **Are** you a teacher? |
| She is here. | **Is** she here? |

Answer short: Yes, I **am**. · No, I'm **not**. · Yes, she **is**. · No, she **isn't**.

**a** before a sound like b, c, d: a bag. **an** before a, e, i, o, u: an apple.

**this** is near. **that** is far.

---

## Unit 3 · Possessive 's and plurals

Add **'s** to a name to show it belongs to them: Marek**'s** mother.

Add **s** to make more than one: one bag, two bag**s**.
Some words change: one child, two **children** · one man, two **men** ·
one woman, two **women**.

**our** and **their** go in front of a noun, like my and your.

---

## Unit 4 · there is, there are

| One thing | More than one |
|---|---|
| **There is** a bed. | **There are** two beds. |

Question: **Is there** a kitchen? · **How many** rooms are there?

**in**, **on**, **under** say where: **in** the kitchen, **on** the table,
**under** the bed.

---

## Unit 5 · Present simple · I, you, we, they

Use it for every day:

I **work**. You **live** here. We **start** at eight. They **finish** at five.

Question: **Do** you work here? Answer: Yes, I **do**. · No, I **don't**.

Times: at seven **o'clock** · **half** past eight · a **quarter** past nine.

---

## Unit 6 · Present simple · he, she, it

Add **s** to the verb:

He work**s**. She live**s** here. It start**s** at eight.

Some add **es**: he go**es**, she watch**es**, he finish**es**.

Question: **Does** she work here? Answer: Yes, she **does**. · No, she
**doesn't**. The **s** goes on **does**, not on the verb after it.

---

## Unit 7 · some, any · countable and uncountable

Use **some** in a sentence: There is **some** bread.
Use **any** in a question and after not: Is there **any** milk? ·
There isn't **any** rice.

Some words you can count: one egg, two eggs.
Some words you cannot: bread, milk, rice, water, sugar. They take **is**, not
**are**: The milk **is** cold.

**like**: I **like** tea. She **likes** coffee.

---

## Unit 8 · Where things are · imperatives

**next to**, **opposite**, **between**, **behind**, **in front of**,
**near**, **far from**.

To tell someone what to do, use the verb alone:
**Go** straight on. **Turn** left. **Cross** the road. **Don't** turn right.

---

## Unit 9 · can, can't

| Person | Verb |
|---|---|
| I, you, he, she, we, they | can |

I **can** swim. She **can** drive. They **can't** cook.
**can** never adds an s, and the verb after it never changes.

Question: **Can** you swim? Answer: Yes, I **can**. · No, I **can't**.

---

## Unit 10 · was, were

The past of am and is is **was**. The past of are is **were**.

| Person | Past |
|---|---|
| I, he, she, it | was |
| you, we, they | were |

I **was** at home. They **were** at the party.
Question: **Were** you there? · No, I **wasn't**.

Past time words: **yesterday** · last **week** · two days **ago**.

---

## Unit 11 · Past simple · regular verbs

Add **ed** to the verb:

work → work**ed** · play → play**ed** · listen → listen**ed**

If the verb ends in **e**, add **d**: live → live**d**.
If it ends in **y** after a sound like b or r, the y becomes **ied**:
carry → carr**ied**.
Some double the last letter: stop → sto**pped**.

The past is the same for every person: I worked, she worked, they worked.

---

## Unit 12 · Past simple · irregular verbs · did

Some verbs do not add ed. They become a new word. The list is in the back of
this book.

go → **went** · have → **had** · see → **saw** · eat → **ate**

Question: **Did** you go? Answer: Yes, I **did**. · No, I **didn't**.
After **did** and **didn't**, the verb goes back to its first form:
Did you **go**? · I didn't **go**.

---

## Unit 13 · Present continuous

For what is happening **now**:

| Person | Verb | Verb + ing |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | work**ing** |
| you, we, they | are | work**ing** |
| he, she, it | is | work**ing** |

She **is cooking** now. · They **are waiting**.

Every day is the present simple. Now is the present continuous:
She **cooks** every day. She **is cooking** now.

---

## Unit 14 · going to

For what you plan:

I **am going to** fly. · She **is going to** pack. · They **are going to**
travel.

Question: **Are** you **going to** travel? Answer: Yes, I **am**.

Next time words: **tomorrow** · **tonight** · **next** week · **soon** ·
**later**.

---

## Unit 15 · Comparatives

Two things, one measure. Add **er** to a short word:

old → old**er** · cheap → cheap**er** · big → big**ger** · busy → bus**ier**

Long words take **more** in front: **more** expensive · **more** difficult.

Some are new words: good → **better** · bad → **worse**.

Use **than** between the two things: My bag is bigger **than** yours.

---

## Unit 16 · like, love, hate + ing

After **like**, **love** and **hate**, the verb takes **ing**:

I love danc**ing**. · She likes read**ing**. · They hate wait**ing**.

Spelling: dance → danc**ing** (the e goes) · swim → swi**mming** (the m
doubles).

Question: **Do** you like dancing? · **What do** you like doing?

---

## Unit 17 · would like

**would like** is a polite way to say want:

I **would like** a coffee. · **Would** you **like** a cake?

Short form: **I'd** like · **he'd** like · **we'd** like.
The verb after would like never changes, and there is no s on **would**.

**like** is always true. **would like** is right now:
I like coffee. (every day) · I'd like a coffee. (now)

---

## Unit 18 · Superlatives

Three things or more. Add **est** and put **the** in front:

the old**est** · the cheap**est** · the long**est** · the bus**iest**

Long words take **most**: the **most** beautiful.

Some are new words: good → the **best** · bad → the **worst**.

Two things take **er** and **than**. All the things take **the** and **est**.

---

## Unit 19 · have got · should

| Person | Verb |
|---|---|
| I, you, we, they | have got |
| he, she, it | has got |

I **have got** a cold. · She **has got** a headache.
Short forms: I**'ve** got · she**'s** got.
No: I **haven't** got a temperature.
Question: **Have** you got a cold? · Yes, I **have**.

For advice use **should**: You **should** go to bed. · You **shouldn't** go
to work. The verb after should never changes.

---

## Unit 20 · The three times, together

| Time | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| last year | past | I **was** at school. I **worked** here. |
| now | present | I **am** at work. |
| next year | will | I **will be** in Spain. I **will live** there. |

**will** is the same for every person, and the verb after it never changes.

Question: **Will** you come back? Answer: Yes, I **will**.
