# Audio File

Every listening script in the course, to the word. Eighty scripts, four in
each of the twenty units.

**How to use this book without the recordings.** Read the script aloud at a
natural pace. Read it twice. Do not read the answers out, and do not slow
down past the point where the sentence stops sounding like English — a
beginner who only ever hears speech at half speed learns to understand speech
at half speed. Where a script marks two voices, two people should read it.

Speaker letters are **N** Nadia, **S** Sam, **L** Lili, **M** Marek,
**F** Fatima, **B** Ben, **R** Rosa, **O** Omar, and **V** for a voice who is
not one of the eight.

## Unit 1 · Hello

**3A** · Four voices.

> 1 — B: Hello. My name is Ben. I work in the shop.
> 2 — R: Hello. I'm Rosa. I live at number fourteen.
> 3 — O: Hi! I'm Omar. I'm nine.
> 4 — M: Good evening. My name is Marek. I look after the flats.

**3B** · The class list.

> F: Good evening, everyone. Lili Chen — you're from China, yes?
> L: Yes, from China.
> F: Omar Haddad. Omar is from Egypt.
> F: Ana Silva — Brazil. Is that right, Ana?
> V: Yes, Brazil.
> F: And Jan Kowalski, from Poland. Thank you.

**3C** · Eight countries.

> F: Where is everyone from? Lili is from China. Hannah is from Japan.
> F: Ana is from Brazil, and Jan is from Poland.
> F: Carmen is from Spain. And Chidi — Chidi is from Nigeria.
> F: That's six countries in one class.

**3D** · Spelling names.

> 1 — L: My name is Lili. L-I-L-I.
> 2 — M: Marek. That's M-A-R-E-K.
> 3 — R: Rosa. R-O-S-A.
