# EFDL A1.1 · the ten units

The arc follows the sequence American Headway Starter uses — be, then
possessives and family, then there is/are, then present simple split across
two units (I/you/we/they before he/she/it), then food, then the town, then
can, then the past of be — rearranged so that every unit stands on Hill Road
and uses the cast the learner already knows.

| | Title | Grammar | Lexis | Function |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hello | am / is / are · my, your, his, her | greetings, countries, jobs, 1–10 | greet, introduce, spell |
| 2 | In the Class | questions and short answers · isn't, aren't · a/an · this/that | classroom things, 11–20 | ask what things are |
| 3 | Who's This? | possessive 's · plural nouns · our, their | family, 20–100 | talk about people |
| 4 | Where I Live | there is / there are · how many · in, on, under | rooms, furniture | describe a home |
| 5 | Every Day | present simple I/you/we/they · time · days | routines, o'clock, half past | say what you do |
| 6 | She Works Here | present simple he/she/it · does | jobs, workplaces, often/never | ask about others |
| 7 | Food and Drink | some / any · countable and uncountable · like | food, drink, prices | order and pay |
| 8 | In the Town | prepositions of place · imperatives | places in a town | ask the way |
| 9 | I Can Do That | can / can't | activities, sports | say what you can do |
| 10 | Last Weekend | was / were · past time words | the weekend, feelings | say where you were |

Every unit: 38 exercises in nine parts, 22 figures, at most 42 new words,
ceilings only.
