# 17 · Detailed Contents — the A2 and B1 books

Five books · 50 units · 530 guided hours · cumulative vocabulary 700 → 2,850.

Each unit is given twice: once as **the system** (what it teaches) and once as **the unit** (what
the learner does with it). The system columns are generated from `data/spine.json` and
`data/structures.tsv`; the Outcome, File and Decision columns are authored here, and for A2.1,
A2.2, B1.2 and B1.3 they appear for the first time.

**Conventions.** G1 is the new grammar target, G2 the returning one. L1 is the topic lexical set,
L2 the system set. The File rotates **W S C W S C W S C W** (`15-rotation-maps.md` §2); no format
appears twice in a book, and no format appears in two consecutive books. The reading genre follows
the rotation matrix in `15` §3.

## How these five books hold together

**The grammar arc.** A2.1 builds the past and the first conditional; A2.2 adds the reported and the
hypothetical; B1.1 stops introducing tenses and starts *contrasting* them — narrative tenses
contrasted, future forms four ways, conditionals 0/1/2 together. B1.2 is the hypothetical book:
third, mixed, future perfect, *wish*. B1.3 leaves the verb phrase almost entirely and works on
**clause and discourse** — participles, concession, cleft, passive reporting, inversion. That shift
at B1.3 is the one that decides whether a learner can go on to B2.

**Returning structures, measured.** The passive is introduced at A2.1 U7 as a form, returns at
B1.1 U6 as procedure and at B1.2 U6 as something done *to* you. Relative clauses: A2.1 U4 (defining),
A2.2 U8 (non-defining), B1.1 U8 (reduced), B1.3 U1 (*of which*), B1.3 U2 (participle). Five
encounters, five different jobs. `tools/grammar_db.py` enforces that no return comes closer than
three units to the previous encounter at B level.

**The File across five books.** 50 units carry 20 Work Files, 15 Study and 15 Culture, drawn from
all 36 formats, no format twice in a book and none in two consecutive books. The Work Files move
from *the meeting* and *the review* at A2 to *the handover*, *the safety brief* and *the client
problem* at B1.3 — the same formats, at a level where the learner has to carry the consequence.

**The Decisions.** Twenty-five of the fifty are about money, ten about who gets to decide, eight
about telling the truth at a cost, and seven about a person who was not in the room when the
decision was made. None has a right answer. Each one ends with a Reveal that says what happened,
and asks whether the reasoning was sound even when the outcome was not.
