> **FIGURE `fig_a21_u09_p03_v09b` · V9 Grammar Visual · ladder**

### 3C · Know

| | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **too** | *too* + adjective (= more than you want) | *It's **too expensive**.* |
| **too much / too many** | + noun | ***too much** data · **too many** cameras* |
| **enough** | adjective + *enough* , *enough* + noun | *big **enough** · **enough** money* |
| **not enough** | less than you need | *We don't have **enough** money.* |
| **so** | *so* + adjective **or** adverb | *It's **so** simple. · He spoke **so** quickly.* |
| **such** | *such* + (a) + adjective + noun | *It's **such** a simple system.* |

> **too is a complaint. so is a feeling.**
> *It's too simple* = it does not do what I need.
> *It's so simple* = and I am pleased about that.

> **Adverbs of manner.** Most add **-ly**: *quick → quickly*, *careful → carefully*.
> Some do not change: *fast → fast*, *hard → hard*. One is irregular: *good → **well***.

> **Watch out.** ✗ *so a simple system* ✓ *such a simple system* ✓ *so simple*
> ✗ *He speaks very good.* ✓ *He speaks very well.*

## Part 11 · The Decision ◆ **C · THE PERFORMANCE**

# Free wifi, nine cameras

A company will install wifi across the whole market, free, for five years. In return it puts up
nine cameras and keeps what they record. Nobody at the company has been able to say clearly what
the cameras count beyond "footfall".

- **Accept.** Free wifi is worth about 2,800 a year to the traders. Nine cameras in a public
  market where there are already four.
- **Refuse.** Pay for wifi, or do without. The market has done without for thirty years.
- **Accept with conditions.** Signed answers to the three 2011 questions, a copy of the data,
  and the right to end it with three months' notice.

### 11A · The evidence

1. **The contract extract** from Part 5E — and the clause in smaller type.
2. **The chart** — what the cameras can count, and what each thing is worth to the company.
3. **The voice.** The salesman, twenty seconds: *"Honestly, I don't know what else it counts. I
   sell them. There's a technical team and I've asked twice and they send me a diagram I don't
   understand either."*

### 11B · Talk about it

In threes. Everybody says one. Use the frame.

> *I think the market should ______ , because ______ .*

**Words you can use:** *privacy · data · track · record · too much · enough · who can see · free*

### 11C · Decide

Write **two sentences**. Choose, and say why.

> I think the market should ______________________ ,
> because ______________________ .

### 11D · Model decision

> Accept with conditions, and make the conditions the three questions from 2011. The committee
> asked them fifteen years ago and never got an answer, and the only thing that has changed is
> that somebody now wants something badly enough to answer. If the company will not put the
> answers in writing, that is itself the answer, and it costs nothing to find out.

### 11E · The Reveal

> **What happened.** The committee asked for the three answers in writing. The company answered
> two of them in four days and did not answer the third.
>
> The third was *who else can see it*. The reply, when it came six weeks later, named two
> partner companies that nobody had heard of, and one of them is a company that sets prices for
> supermarkets.
>
> The market refused. Three months later the same company offered the deal again, without the
> cameras, for a fee of 400 a month, which the market is now paying.

**The question that was never answered in 2011 was the one that mattered. And the wifi now costs
money.**

> **Was it the right decision?** Say why — and say what 400 a month is buying.
