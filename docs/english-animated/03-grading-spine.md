# 03 · The Grading Spine

Every number in this document is a **writer's brief and a QA gate**. A unit that misses a band is
rejected and rewritten. This is what "graded" means operationally; without it, "A2" and "B1" are
decorations on a cover.

---

## 1 · Vocabulary

"Active" = taught, practised in ≥3 parts, glossed, recycled in a later unit, and tested.

| Band | Single words | Collocations & chunks | Phrasal / multi-word verbs | Fixed expressions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1–A2 | 70% | 20% | 5% | 5% |

**Recycling law.** Every active item must reappear **≥4 times after the unit that teaches it**,
at least once in a *different unit* and at least once in a *different skill*. Tracked in the
series lexical database; audited per book.

**Authenticity ramp.** A1–A2: written to level. B1: adapted authentic — real source, simplified
lexis, original structure and voice preserved. B2: lightly edited authentic (cuts only). C1–C2:
unedited authentic, including texts that are *difficult because they are badly written*, which is
a real-world reading skill nobody teaches.

**Genre quota per book (minimum).** From B1 every book contains at least one of: a chart or data
set read as a text · a legal/administrative document · a personal/first-person piece · a text the
learner is invited to disagree with · a text written by a non-native user of English.

**Unit audio, all parts** counts every recorded minute in the unit, not only Part 2: the two
listening texts, the Vocabulary Lab audio, the Speaking models, the Pronunciation Lab, the Decision
evidence and the Part 12 review. Per book this gives **1.3 h at A1.1 rising to 4.3 h at C2.2**,
including the Milestone Long Listens — the figure quoted in `01-series-architecture.md` §4.

**Scripting ramp.** A1–A2 fully scripted and performed. B1 scripted from a real recording,
re-performed with hesitation restored. B2 semi-scripted: speakers briefed, not scripted. C1–C2
genuinely unscripted, lightly edited for length only.

**Decoding clinic targets** (Part 2B), by level: A1 word boundaries and numbers → A2 weak forms
and contractions → B1 elision, assimilation, linking → B2 reduced clusters, discourse marker
swallowing → C1 fast-speech chunking across clause boundaries → C2 comprehension under
competing-signal conditions.

course rather than a better-looking one.

## 9 · Gate summary

A unit is returned to the writer if any of these fails:

1. Active-item count outside band ±10%.
2. Reading or listening length outside band.
3. Off-list vocabulary above tolerance in a graded text.
4. Mean sentence length outside band ±15%.
5. Fewer than the minimum evaluate/create tasks.
6. One language point owning more than 4 parts.
7. Any figure not cited by a task.
8. Recycling quota unmet in Part 12.
9. Any task type repeated in identical form within the unit.
10. Writing output below band.
