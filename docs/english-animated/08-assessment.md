# 08 · Assessment, Progression and Certification

The corpus assesses by self-report: one can-do tick list per unit, no diagnostic, no progress test,
no portfolio, no exit test, no exam mapping (`00-diagnosis.md` §3.5). English Animated runs six
layers, each with a different job and a different owner.

---

## 1 · The six layers

| Layer | Frequency | Owner | Purpose | Stakes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Placement** | Once, on entry | Platform | Put the learner in the right book | None |
| **2 · Book Diagnostic** | Start of each book | Teacher | Find what is already known; route | None |
| **3 · Unit Can-Do + Evidence** | Every unit, Part 12 | Learner | Metacognition; make progress visible | None |
| **4 · Milestone Check** | After Units 5 and 10 | Teacher | Integrated performance; route remediation | Low |
| **5 · Book Exit** | End of each book | Teacher / centre | Confirm readiness for the next book | Medium |
| **6 · Portfolio** | Continuous | Learner + teacher | Hold the evidence; carry it forward | Varies |

---

## 2 · Layer 1 — Placement

Adaptive, 25–35 minutes, on the platform.

- **Grammar and lexis**, 20–30 adaptive items, item difficulty calibrated to the series' own spine.
- **Listening**, 4 extracts of rising speed and complexity.
- **Reading**, 3 texts of rising length and density.
- **Writing**, one 100-word open response, machine-scored against a trained rubric and flagged for
  human review at band boundaries.
- **Speaking**, one 60-second recorded response, scored for fluency, range and intelligibility.

**Output:** a recommended book, a confidence band, and an explicit *second-choice* recommendation
with the reason. A placement test that returns one number without a reason cannot be argued with,
and should be.

---

## 3 · Layer 2 — Book Diagnostic

30 items at the start of each book, mapped to that book's ten units. The learner receives a **map,
not a score**:

> *Units 1, 4 and 7 will be new to you. Unit 3 you mostly have — use the Fast path. Unit 9 is your
> weakest area; the Workbook has 6 extra pages for it.*

The Teacher's Edition prints the routing table that converts a diagnostic profile into a lesson
plan for the whole class.

---

## 4 · Layer 3 — Unit Can-Do with Evidence

The corpus's tick list, made honest. Each can-do is **paired with the artefact that proves it**:

| I can… | My evidence | Date | Confident? |
|---|---|---|---|
| chair a short meeting and summarise what was agreed | Part 9 recording, 14 Mar | | ◯ ◑ ● |
| write minutes a colleague can act on without asking me | Part 8 draft 2 | | ◯ ◑ ● |
| explain a technical point to a non-specialist | Part 10 relay task | | ◯ ◑ ● |

Plus the **Part 0 / Part 12 replay**: the learner's 30-second answer to the Unit Question, recorded
at the start and re-recorded at the end, played back-to-back. Audible evidence of change inside a
single unit. This is the strongest motivational device in the architecture and it costs nothing.

---

## 5 · Layer 4 — Milestone Check

After Units 5 and 10, a 50-minute integrated check. No single-skill sections.

| Section | Task | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Integrated reading & writing | Read two short texts, write a response that uses both | 25% |
| Integrated listening & speaking | Listen, take notes, relay to a partner, answer a question | 25% |
| Language in use | Recycled items from all five units, mixed formats | 25% |
| Mediation | Simplify, summarise or bridge, to a named audience | 25% |

**Output is a routing profile, not a grade.** Every score band maps to specific pages: Workbook
sections, platform drills, extension tasks, or a one-to-one tutorial trigger.

---

## 6 · Layer 5 — Book Exit Test

90 minutes, five papers, aligned to the formats the learner will meet in public exams without
pretending to be one.

| Paper | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Reading & Use of English | 30 min | 3 texts, 40 items, mixed formats |
| Listening | 20 min | 4 recordings, 25 items, one at the book's top speed |
| Writing | 25 min | 2 tasks: one controlled genre, one open |
| Speaking | 10 min/pair | Interview · long turn · collaborative task · discussion |
| Mediation | 15 min | One source, two audiences |

**Pass = 60% overall with no paper below 45%.** The floor exists because a learner who cannot
listen has not finished the book, whatever their reading score.

Writing and speaking are scored on a **5-band analytic rubric** published to learners from day one:
*task achievement · coherence & organisation · range · accuracy · interaction/delivery*. A learner
who has not seen the rubric cannot aim at it.

---

## 7 · Layer 6 — Portfolio

Three anchor pieces per book, chosen by the learner from their own work, redrafted to their best:

1. **A spoken artefact** — a presentation, a chaired discussion, a negotiation.
2. **A written artefact** — the best genre piece, with all drafts attached.
3. **A mediated artefact** — something explained, simplified or bridged for a named audience.

Each carries a **50-word reflection**: what was hard, what changed between drafts, what the learner
would do differently. By C2 the portfolio holds 42 pieces spanning eight years — a record no exam
certificate can substitute for, and the thing a learner actually shows an employer.

---

## 8 · External exam mapping

The series does not teach to an exam. It maps to them so that a learner, or a centre, can see
exactly where they stand.

| Book | Cambridge | IELTS | TOEFL iBT | Linguaskill | Aptis | Trinity GESE/ISE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1.1–A1.2 | — / Starters–Movers | — | — | 100–119 | A1 | GESE 1–2 |
| A2.1–A2.2 | **A2 Key (KET)** | — | — | 120–139 | A2 | GESE 3–4 / ISE 0 |
| B1.1–B1.3 | **B1 Preliminary (PET)** | 4.0–5.0 | 42–71 | 140–159 | B1 | GESE 5–6 / ISE I |
| B2.1–B2.3 | **B2 First (FCE)** | 5.5–6.5 | 72–94 | 160–179 | B2 | GESE 7–9 / ISE II |
| C1.1–C1.2 | **C1 Advanced (CAE)** | 7.0–8.0 | 95–114 | 180–199 | C1 | GESE 10–11 / ISE III |
| C2.1–C2.2 | **C2 Proficiency (CPE)** | 8.5–9.0 | 115–120 | 200+ | C2 | GESE 12 / ISE IV |

**Exam Bridge sections** appear in the final book of each level (A1.2, A2.2, B1.3, B2.3, C1.2,
C2.2): five pages decoding the exam's task types, its rubrics, and its timing — taught as the
*Exam Decode* Study File format (`05-topic-matrix.md` §6), so that exam skill is a transferable
literacy rather than a cram.

## 9 · Teacher-facing analytics

From the platform, per class and per learner:

- **Can-do coverage heat map** — which can-dos have evidence attached, which are claimed without it.
- **Recycling performance** — how learners score on *recycled* items versus *new* ones. The single
  best early-warning signal for retention failure, and the metric no coursebook platform reports.
- **Productive-skills trend** — rubric bands over time, per criterion, so a teacher can see that
  range is rising while accuracy is flat.
- **Time-on-part** — which parts over- or under-run against the planned timings, fed back into the
  next edition.

---

## 10 · What is deliberately not assessed

- **Accent.** Intelligibility is assessed; accent is not. This is stated to learners explicitly.
- **Cultural conformity.** No rubric rewards agreeing with a text's position.
- **Speed of acquisition.** The routing architecture assumes different learners need different
  numbers of encounters, and says so.
- **Participation as a proxy for learning.** Quiet learners produce artefacts too; the portfolio,
  not the classroom, is the record.
