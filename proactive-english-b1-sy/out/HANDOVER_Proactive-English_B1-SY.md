## 6 · Register rules, and the one that inverts

- **The past perfect is at ZERO until Unit 3.** A2 shipped zero in all four volumes; Units 1
  and 2 of B1 hold that line so that **Unit 3 owns it**. Unit 1's first draft had **12** in the
  Student's Book and **10** in the key — it is the single likeliest drift at B1 and the sweep
  must run on every unit until Unit 3, after which it inverts to a by-hand check that the form
  is *present and correct* where the unit teaches it.
- **Reported speech keeps A2's no-backshift rule until Unit 10**, which teaches the full system
  and names the A2 rule it replaces.
- **Hedging** is introduced in 1.5 — _tends to · can be · a bit · I'd say · at first_ — one per
  sentence, and the test is whether the information survived.
- **Phrasal verbs** are a declared set per unit, 8–10, never repeated. Unit 1: _come across as ·
  get on with · fit in · settle in · take to somebody · bring something up · turn up · keep up with_.

## 8 · QA faults found in Unit 1 and fixed — watch for these again

1. A comprehension question (1.1 **b2**) asked why a character "stops and starts again" — he does
   not; the behaviour belonged to a different character. **Test every comprehension question
   against the track, not against the plan for the track.**
2. A Reading Part 5 item had **two** correct options (*if* and *whether*). Distractors must be
   visibly wrong, especially in the first unit of a level.
3. Two unit-check listening items were not supported by their own script — the key named
   something the extract did not say. **Read each extract alone and answer the item from it.**
4. Twenty-two past-perfect occurrences across the pair (see §6).
5. Eighteen Note boxes in the key against a house range of 7–10 (see §7).
