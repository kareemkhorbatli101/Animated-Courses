## 7 · Register rules

- **The past perfect is at ZERO until Unit 3.** From Unit 3 the sweep inverts to a by-hand check
  that the form is *present and correct* where the unit teaches it.
- **Reported speech keeps A2's no-backshift rule until Unit 10.** Decisions in minutes are **not**
  backshifted (10.6). **Unit 23 reopens the subject from the other end** — not the backshift but
  the **choice of verb**, and the house rule stated there is the one to carry into B2: _write_
  said _unless you can say out loud why you have chosen something else._
- **Hedging** introduced in 1.5, one per sentence. **Unit 22.3 adds the approximators** —
  _about_, _roughly_, _between X and Y_, _on average_, _out of_ — which are not hedges: they say
  how a figure was arrived at.
- **Phrasal verbs:** a declared set of 8–10 per unit, never repeated.
- **The two commas of Unit 11 are meaning, not punctuation.** The sweep for it is by eye.
- **Gaps inside italics render as literal underscores**, because `lib.js`'s italic regex excludes
  `_` inside a span. This is the as-built behaviour of **every unit of both books** (`_I drive ___
  160 kilometres a day._`), it is consistent, and `lib.js` is frozen, so **do not "fix" it**.

- **The page-count levers.** Measure per page
  (`pdftotext -f N -l N file.pdf - | grep -c '[A-Za-z]'`) and read the spread:
  - a page under about twenty counted lines usually means a `cantSplit` box **jumped**;
  - **a page with only 2–5 counted lines is one of two different things** — a genuinely blank
    page (a cycle's content ended exactly at a page boundary and the next `pb()` pushed past it),
    or a correct `lines(N)` ruled writing block, which the line counter cannot see. **Always read
    the preceding page before trimming**; a Unit 21 "blank" turned out to be the ruled block for
    a Writing Part 1 answer.
  - Trimming early in a cycle does **not** collapse the tail. Take 12–15 counted lines out of the
    cycle whose tail page is smallest, and expect to need a **second, deeper pass** (another
    10–14 lines) where a page is genuinely blank.

## 9 · Faults found and fixed — watch for these again

1. **`'),` instead of `',`** at the end of an array element or table row. The most frequent defect
   in the series: `SyntaxError: Unexpected token ')'`. Sweep for lines ending `_'),`.
2. **Apostrophes in fix scripts.** The JS files store `\'`. In Python use a **raw triple-quoted**
   needle (`r"""…Rasha\'s…"""`) and assert `count == 1` and `old != new` for **every** pair before
   writing anything; a single no-op pair aborts the whole write and leaves the file untouched,
   which is the behaviour you want.
3. **Exam items with more than one correct answer.** Found in Units 1, 7, 17, 22, 23 and both end
   tests. Every distractor must be visibly wrong, and a **half-correct** distractor counts as a
   fault (Unit 23's first draft offered _nineteen; reportedly_ where only the second half was
   wrong). Check every option against the stem, by hand, after the build.
4. **Unit-check listening items not supported by their own script.** Read each extract alone and
   answer the item from it with the rest of the page covered. Unit 17 had two.
5. **Answer-letter patterning.** Reorder **options**, never answers. Three of a letter in five, or
   two adjacent identical answers in a six-item cloze, is a redraft.
6. **Comprehension questions written against the plan for a track rather than the track.** Unit
   23's "what three things does he say he is not claiming" asked for three where the model has two.
7. **Register drift:** past perfect before Unit 3; Americanisms; policy words.
8. **Continuity drift inside the books.** Found and fixed: *Bassel Khoury* for *Bassel Tahhan*;
   *lorries* for *buses* in Tareq's thread; **nine `F. Haddad` bylines for `F. Kayyali`** across
   five Book 2 keys; and a new figure given the name of an existing one (an Aleppo foreman called
   *Issam Haddad*, who is the handset distributor's man — renamed **Nabil Zahra**). **Grep the
   cast's surnames, and each character's vehicle, job and premises, against §5 at the end of every
   unit pair, and grep any new name against the whole tree before using it.**
9. **The house `**Practise:**` count.** Six per unit, one per Language box in x.1–x.6. Unit 17's
   first draft had two.
10. **Note-box count in the keys.** Ten per key in Book 2. Count in the **source**, not the PDF.
