@INSTR Read about root cause logic.
_80
@NUM 100
1. Read: "Root cause logic explains the relationship between an action and the result expected from it. It shows how an action is supposed to remove a cause. For example, if we record the hours at the end of each shift, the cost of each job becomes accurate. If the cost is accurate, the price covers what the job really took. If prices cover their costs, the margin holds. If the margin holds, the business can pay its staff and reinvest. Root cause logic helps managers understand the path from one action to the long-term result."
