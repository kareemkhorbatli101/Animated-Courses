@EX Part 4: Reading About Influence in a Plant
@INSTR Read about where influence lies inside a plant.
_80
@NUM 100
1. Read: "Understanding where influence lies is essential for effective quality advocacy. An influence analysis examines who holds the authority to decide, who shapes those decisions, who would gain from the change, who gains from the present arrangement, and how influence is spread among the parties. Different parties hold different sources of influence—formal authority (the plant director), money (the owners), knowledge (the laboratory and the auditors), numbers (the shift teams), collective voice (the quality committee). Effective advocacy identifies where influence lies, builds support among those who can be persuaded, brings the shift teams together (influence through numbers), and creates new influence (evidence, customer expectation, a shared committee) where little existed before."
