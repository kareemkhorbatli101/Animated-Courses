1. Read: "Understanding where influence lies is essential for effective advocacy. An influence analysis examines who holds the authority to decide, who shapes those decisions, who would gain from the change, who gains from the present arrangement, and how influence is spread among the parties. Different parties hold different sources of influence—formal authority (the ministry), money (the large producers), knowledge (the lawyers and the accountants), numbers (the small studios), collective voice (the association). Effective advocacy identifies where influence lies, builds support among those who can be persuaded, brings the small studios together (influence through numbers), and creates new influence (evidence, press attention, a shared committee) where little existed before."
