@EX 2B:  Grammar Focus Box
@INSTR Complete the table below using the rules provided.
@GRID
First Conditional | Second Conditional
 |
Structure: If + present simple, will + infinitive. Example: "If the country improves its legal framework, investors will commit." / "If you submit the application, we will fast-track it." | Structure: If + past simple, would + infinitive. Example: "If we restructured the package, we would attract more interest." / "If you opened a dedicated office, processing time would drop."
@CHOICES
a)  Used for hypothetical, imagined, or unlikely situations. The speaker is describing something that is not happening now or is not expected to happen.
b)  Used for real, probable, or likely situations and their results. The speaker believes the condition could actually happen.

@INSTR Read the profile of the National Development and Investment Forum. Pay attention to the purpose, the sectors covered, the participating delegations, and the outcomes.
@PANEL
The National Development and Investment Forum
The National Development and Investment Forum is an annual international event organised by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in partnership with the Investment Promotion Agency and the Ministry of Finance. The Forum brings together foreign investors, development banks, international organisations, and government officials to explore investment opportunities across the country's priority development sectors.
The 2025 Forum, held in Addis Ababa over two days, attracted delegations from 24 countries, including Germany, France, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Italy, and India. A total of 87 companies and financial institutions participated, alongside representatives from the World Bank, the African Development Bank, the Islamic Development Bank, and seven UN agencies.
The Forum focused on five priority investment sectors: infrastructure and transportation, energy (conventional and renewable), agriculture and food security, housing and urban development, and telecommunications and digital infrastructure.
During the Forum, three letters of intent were signed: one between Richter Infrastructure GmbH and the Investment Promotion Agency for a road construction feasibility study in the eastern corridor; one between the Gulf Development Fund and the Ministry of Agriculture for an agricultural joint venture study in the Awash Valley; and one between a South Korean telecommunications consortium and the Ministry of Communications for a digital infrastructure framework agreement.
The Forum also featured a panel discussion on investment law reform, a series of bilateral meetings between ministry officials and company representatives, and a cultural evening hosted by the Protocol Department showcasing the country's heritage. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs has confirmed that the 2026 Forum will expand to three days and will include an investors' site visit programme to priority development zones.
