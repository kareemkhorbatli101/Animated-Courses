Profile 1: Pham Thi Mai
Pham Thi Mai is a Protocol Officer at the Vietnamese Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Hanoi. She has worked at the ministry for four years. Her role involves managing the arrival and reception of visiting diplomats, organizing official ceremonies, and ensuring that all diplomatic formalities are correctly observed. Currently, she is preparing the detailed logistics for a three-day official visit by the UN Special Rapporteur, including the arrival ceremony, a series of bilateral meetings, and a formal working dinner. Mai regularly coordinates with embassy staff from multiple countries to confirm schedules and protocol requirements before each visit.
Profile 2: Nguyen Van Duc
Nguyen Van Duc works as a Consular Affairs Officer at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. He has been with the ministry for six years and is responsible for managing the processing of diplomatic visas, travel documents, and consular notifications for foreign nationals visiting or residing in Viet Nam. Duc's section also provides support to Vietnamese citizens abroad who need documentation assistance when returning to the country. Currently, he is reviewing and updating the ministry's diplomatic visa processing procedures to reduce waiting times for applicants. Duc frequently liaises with foreign embassies in Hanoi to coordinate document verification processes.
Profile 3: Tran Thu Ha
Tran Thu Ha is a Press Attaché at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. She has been in this role for two years. Her main responsibilities include managing media accreditation for journalists covering diplomatic events, drafting official press statements, and organizing press conferences at the ministry. Ha is currently preparing the full media program for an upcoming regional investment conference in Hanoi — including the press pool rotation, photography access protocols, and simultaneous translation arrangements for international media. She collaborates closely with the ministry's spokespersons and with foreign press offices to coordinate coverage.
Profile 4: Le Quang Huy
Le Quang Huy is an Economic Affairs Specialist at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. He has worked at the ministry for three years, focusing on trade diplomacy and international investment facilitation. His responsibilities include preparing briefing documents for the foreign minister ahead of bilateral economic meetings, analyzing trade policy developments in partner countries, and representing Viet Nam at international economic conferences as part of the diplomatic delegation. Currently, Huy is coordinating Viet Nam's participation in a major regional investment forum and is working with the German and French embassies on bilateral economic cooperation frameworks.
