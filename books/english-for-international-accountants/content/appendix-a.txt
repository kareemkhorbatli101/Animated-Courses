@H2 Unit 8: The Accounting Cycle
@H2 Passage Title: Closing the Books
@LINE The accounting cycle is the heartbeat of the finance department. It is a systematic process that repeats every month, ensuring that the financial health of the business is accurately captured. At NovaCorp, the cycle is strictly adhered to in every country where the group operates. The process begins with the identification of transactions. Throughout the month, source documents like invoices and receipts are collected and analyzed. These transactions are then journalized. The data is entered into the system, creating a chronological record of every financial event.
@LINE Once the transactions are recorded, they are posted to the General Ledger. This is where the accounts are classified. At the end of the month, the unadjusted trial balance is generated. This document lists all the account balances to ensure that total debits equal total credits. However, the work is not finished yet. The accounts must be adjusted to reflect accrual accounting principles. Adjusting entries are made for items like depreciation, prepaid expenses, and accrued revenue. These entries are prepared by the senior accountant to ensure that expenses are matched to the revenue they helped generate.
@LINE After the adjustments are posted, the adjusted trial balance is produced. From this document, the financial statements are derived. The Income Statement is prepared first, followed by the Statement of Retained Earnings and the Balance Sheet. These reports are reviewed by the Controller for accuracy, and the local figures are translated into the reporting currency before they are consolidated. Finally, the closing entries are made. The temporary accounts—revenue, expenses, and dividends—are closed to Retained Earnings. This resets their balances to zero, ready for the next accounting period to begin.
@LINE The post-closing trial balance is then generated to verify that the ledger is ready for the new month. The entire process is conducted with strict adherence to deadlines. The books are usually closed by the fifth business day of the following month, in every ledger. If a material error is found after closing, the books must be reopened, or a prior period adjustment must be made. At NovaCorp, accuracy is prioritized over speed, but the cycle must always be completed efficiently.
