@BLABEL Analysis

@TASK 2B. Grammar Focus Box: Future Forms
@TABLEB Future Form | Use | Keyword Example | Example (Accounts Context)
WILL | Predictions, sudden decisions, offers/promises. | ~probably, I think, I promise.~ | I *think* we *will* *clear* this *debt* by Friday.
GOING TO | Prior plan or intention, a future result based on present evidence. | ~intends to, planning to.~ | We *are going to* *issue* a credit note to the client.
PRESENT CONTINUOUS | Fixed, definite arrangement, often with a time/date. | ~tomorrow, next week, on Monday.~ | The *payment run is happening* on Tuesday.

@LABEL Explanation | In rapid speech, will often reduces to /l/ ('ll), and going to reduces to gonna.

They think it'll be paid by the end of the month. (It will be paid)
We are not gonna extend the terms. (We are not going to extend)
