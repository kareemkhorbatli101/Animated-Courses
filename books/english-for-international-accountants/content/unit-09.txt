@SCRIPT

@BLABEL Analysis
@BULLET
Which verbs are used to form the Present Perfect Simple in the text? (HAVE/HAS + _________).

@TASK 2B. Grammar Focus Box: Present Perfect Simple
@TABLE Form | Positive Example | Negative Example | Question Example
*HAVE* / *HAS* + *P.P.* (Past Participle) | I *have* *worked* *in* *audit* *since* 2019. | He *hasn't* *finished* *the* *report* *yet*. | *Have* *you* *ever* *used* *Xero*?
