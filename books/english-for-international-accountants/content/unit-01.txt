@PART 1 | 1 | VOCABULARY – The Language of Bookkeeping
@TASK 1A. Defining the Basics
@LINE Match the accounting terms to their definitions.
@TABLE Term | Definition
1. Invoice | a. A book or digital file where accounts and transactions are recorded.
2. Receipt | b. An entry that increases an asset or expense account.
3. Ledger | c. A document sent by a seller to a buyer requesting payment.
4. Transaction | d. Something valuable that a business owns (e.g., cash, computer).
5. Asset | e. An entry that increases a liability or equity account.
6. Liability | f. Any business event that has a monetary impact (e.g., buying paper).
7. Debit | g. A written acknowledgment that a specified article or sum of money has been received.
8. Credit | h. A debt or financial obligation the company owes (e.g., unpaid bills).

@PART 2 | 1 | GRAMMAR – Present Simple vs. Present Continuous
@TASK 2A. Grammar Context: Routine vs. Right Now
@LINE Read the dialogue between a Senior Accountant (Marta, in Kraków) and a Junior (Daniel, in Nairobi).
@SCRIPT
Marta: "Daniel, do you check the bank statements every morning?"
Daniel: "Yes, I do. I usually download them at 9:00 AM my time. But right now, I am fixing a printer error, so I am a bit late."
Marta: "Okay. What is Priya doing?"
Daniel: "She is meeting with a supplier on a video call. They are discussing the payment terms."
Marta: "I see. We generally hold supplier calls on Tuesdays, but this week is busy."
@BLABEL Analysis
@BULLET
Underline verbs describing *habits/routines* (Present Simple).
Circle verbs describing *actions happening now* (Present Continuous).

@TASK 2B. Grammar Focus Box
@TABLEB Tense | Function | Time Words | Example
Present Simple | Routines, facts, permanent situations, schedules. | ~usually, always, daily, every month, often~ | "I *post* invoices every Friday." // "The office *opens* at 8 AM."
Present Continuous | Actions happening *now*, temporary situations, current projects. | ~currently, at the moment, right now, today, this week~ | "I *am reconciling* the bank." // "We *are using* new software this month."
@LABEL Note on Stative Verbs | Verbs of thinking/owning (know, owe, own, cost, understand) are almost always Simple, even if happening now.
@LINE Correct: "We owe the supplier EUR 500."
@LINE Incorrect: "We are owing the supplier EUR 500."
