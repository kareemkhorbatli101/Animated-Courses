@PART 2 | 10 | GRAMMAR – Reported Speech
@TASK 2A. Grammar Context: Relaying a Decision
@LABEL Context | An accountant is telling a colleague about a meeting with the Group CFO.
@SCRIPT
Accountant: "The Group CFO said that they were not going to approve the full budget increase. She added that we had to find savings in the marketing department. She also asked us if we could submit a revised plan by the end of the week."
@BLABEL Analysis
@BULLET
What *is* *the* *main* *reporting* *verb* *used* *to* *introduce* *the* *reported* *speech*?
How did the verb tenses change from Direct Speech (e.g., ~"We are not going..."~) to Reported Speech (e.g., ~"...they were not going..."~)?
What words (e.g., if, that) are used to connect the reporting verb to the reported clause?

@TASK 2B. Grammar Focus Box: Reported Speech Tense Shifts
@TABLE Direct Speech | Reported Speech (Reporting Verb in Past) | Example
Present Simple (~is~) | Past Simple (~was~) | "The *deadline* *is* *Friday*." -> *He* *said* *the* *deadline* *was* *Friday*.
Present Continuous (~am/is/are doing~) | Past Continuous (~was/were doing~) | "We *are* *reviewing* *the* *figures*." -> *They* *said* *they* *were* *reviewing* *the* *figures*.
Future (~will~) | Conditional (~would~) | "We *will* *hire* *next* *month*." -> *She* *said* *they* *would* *hire* *the* *following* *month*.
Past Simple (~did~) | Past Perfect (~had done~) | "We *cut* *costs* *in* Q1." -> *He* *reported* *that* *they* *had* *cut* *costs* *in* Q1.
