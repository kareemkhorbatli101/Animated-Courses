@TASK 2B. Grammar Focus Box: Past Simple vs. Past Continuous
@TABLEB Tense | Form | Function | Time Marker | Example (Accounting Context)
Past Simple | Verb + -ed / Irregular | Completed action at a specific past time. | Yesterday, last week, in 2022 | "I *found* the error yesterday."
Past Continuous | Was/Were + Verb + -ing | Ongoing action interrupted by another, or action in progress at a specific time. | While, when, at 2 PM yesterday | "I *was posting* the entries when the manager *called*."
