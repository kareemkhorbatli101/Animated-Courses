@TASK 2B. Grammar Focus Box: Comparatives and Superlatives
@TABLE Type | Form | Use | Example (Accounts Context)
Comparative (Short Adj.) | Adj + -er + than | Comparing two items (A > B). | The rent is higher than the salary cost.
Comparative (Long Adj.) | More / Less + Adj + than | Comparing two items (A > B or A < B). | This is less significant than last month's variance.
Superlative (Short Adj.) | The + Adj + -est | Comparing three or more items (A is the highest). | The marketing cost is the largest expense.
Superlative (Long Adj.) | The + most / least + Adj | Comparing three or more items (A is the most X). | This is the most important trend we have seen.
