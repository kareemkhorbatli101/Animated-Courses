@BLABEL Analysis
@BULLET

Which tense of the *Passive Voice* is used most often here (Present Simple Passive)?

@TASK 2B. Grammar Focus Box: The Passive Voice (Present and Past)
@TABLEB Tense | Form | Use | Example (Accounts Context)
Present Simple Passive | is / are + P.P. (Past Participle) | Describing a *routine* *process* or *fact*. | The sales *are* *posted* *daily*.
Past Simple Passive | was / were + P.P. | Describing an *action* *completed* *in* the *past*. | The *report* *was* *prepared* *last* Friday.
Passive with Modal Verbs | Modal + be + P.P. | Describing *necessity* or *possibility* *in* a *process*. | The *accounts* *must* *be* *closed* *by* *noon*.

@TASK 5D. Reading Passage 3: Defining Adjusting Entries
@LINE Read the definition of adjusting entries.
@LINE Adjusting entries are necessary at the end of an accounting period to ensure that the revenue recognition principle and the expense recognition principle are followed. These entries are usually made for items like depreciation, accrued expenses, and unearned revenue. The entries are not supported by separate source documents; they are derived from internal calculations. Without them, the financial statements would be materially misstated.
