@EX Part 4: Reading - Humanitarian Decision-Making
@INSTR Read the text and answer questions about humanitarian choices.
_80
@PANEL
Humanitarian organizations face difficult decisions when resources are limited. In any crisis, needs exceed available resources. Organizations must prioritize assistance based on medical urgency and vulnerability. Life-threatening situations receive priority over chronic conditions. Vulnerable groups like children, elderly, and disabled persons receive priority. Geographic areas with highest need are served first. Cost-effectiveness is considered but never overrides ethical principles. Humanitarian organizations maintain transparency by explaining their prioritization decisions to affected communities and donors. This transparency builds trust and understanding.

@EX Part 4: Text Comprehension - Humanitarian Ethics
@INSTR Read about humanitarian ethics and choose the best answer.
_80
@PASSAGE
Humanitarian action is guided by core principles: humanity, impartiality, neutrality, and independence. Humanity means respecting human dignity and reducing suffering. Impartiality requires treating all people equally regardless of nationality, ethnicity, or religion. Neutrality means not taking sides in conflicts while providing assistance to all affected people. Independence means that humanitarian organizations make decisions based on needs, not political pressure. These principles guide all humanitarian decisions and ensure ethical practice.

@PANEL
Kenya hosts one of the world's largest refugee populations. More than half a million refugees live in camps at Dadaab and Kakuma, many of them for decades. Nairobi is also the regional headquarters for United Nations agencies serving the whole of East Africa. Humanitarian access across the region is complicated by distance, drought, and insecurity. The Kenya Red Cross Society operates across the country alongside international organizations. Drought and flooding affect millions of people in the arid north. Humanitarian workers demonstrate extraordinary patience and commitment. Their work includes emergency response, medical care, and community support. International recognition of Kenyan humanitarian workers acknowledges their skill and dedication. Their resilience and compassion inspire support from the global community.
