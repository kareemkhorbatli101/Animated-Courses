@EX Part 4: Reading About Power Analysis in Advocacy
@INSTR Read about power analysis in advocacy context.
_80
@NUM 100
1. Read: "Understanding power dynamics is essential for effective advocacy. Power analysis examines who holds decision-making authority, who influences decisions, who would benefit from change, who benefits from current system, and how power is distributed among stakeholders. Different stakeholders have different power sources—formal authority (government), financial resources (wealthy individuals), knowledge (experts), numbers (communities), collective voice (unions). Effective advocacy identifies where power lies, builds coalitions with power holders who can be influenced, mobilizes communities (power through numbers), and creates new power (coalition, evidence, public pressure) where little existed before."
