@EX Part 4: Reading About Power Analysis in Advocacy
@INSTR Read about power analysis in the advocacy context.
_80
@NUM 100
1. Read: "Understanding power is essential for effective advocacy. Power analysis examines who holds decision-making authority, who influences decisions, who would gain from change, who gains from the present arrangement, and how power is distributed among stakeholders. Different stakeholders have different sources of power—formal authority (the municipality), money (developers), knowledge (engineers and surveyors), numbers (residents), collective voice (professional bodies). Effective advocacy identifies where power lies, builds coalitions with power holders who can be influenced, mobilizes residents (power through numbers), and creates new power (coalition, evidence, public attention) where little existed before."
