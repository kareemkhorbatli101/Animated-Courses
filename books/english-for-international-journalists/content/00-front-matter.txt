@BIGLABEL WELCOME | About this course
@P
English for Journalists · International Edition is a ten-unit English course for journalists who work across borders. You learn English through the work of Atlas Wire, an invented independent news agency with a world desk in Geneva and correspondents in many countries, and through one continuous story: the reopening of the Kalina crossing — from the morning the barrier went up to the front page that told the year.
The course climbs from CEFR A2 to lower B1. Each unit teaches one new piece of grammar and one part of an international journalist's work — from the words of the world desk, to interviews through an interpreter, to attribution, to a full front page.

@H How to use this book
@NUM
Each unit has a Warm-Up and eleven parts: Words, Grammar, a Skill, Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking, Ethics, Digital, Review, and an Assignment.
Every part builds up step by step: you see a model, notice how it works, practise it, and then produce your own.
Grammar is taught half as controlled practice and half in your own words. Every part mixes exercises you can check with open tasks to discuss and create.
The Ethics part in every unit builds the Code: be accurate, be honest, be fair, be independent, and be kind.
The Answer Key at the end of each unit gives the answers to the closed exercises. Open tasks (discussion, role-play, and your own writing) are marked.
