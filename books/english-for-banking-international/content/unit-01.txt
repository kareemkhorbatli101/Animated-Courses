@PART Part 2 · Grammar — Present Simple vs Present Continuous | Pre-class input

@TASK 2A | Notice the grammar
@INSTR Read the short dialogue. Underline the verbs. Which show a routine, and which show something happening now?
@SCRIPT

Mei: Nice to meet you. I’m training in the loan office this week.

@NUM
work — routine or now? ____
am training — routine or now? ____
do / help — routine or now? ____
am learning — routine or now? ____
@GF
@GRAMMAR
 | Form | Use | Example
Present simple | I/you work; he works | routines, facts | I work at the bank every day.
Present continuous | am/is/are + ‑ing | now, this week (temporary) | I am training this week.
@REMEMBER usually, every day, always → present simple. now, this week, at the moment → present continuous.
@WATCH
✗ He work here. → ✓ He works here. (add ‑s for he/she/it)
✗ I working now. → ✓ I am working now. (don’t drop am/is/are)

@PART Part 8 · Global Case Study — Onboarding at a Global Bank | Practitioner track · read then discuss
@INSTR Before you read: how long should a good “first week” be at a bank? What should a new person learn first?
@READ A Two-Week Start | About 210 words · a made-up example based on real practice
A large international bank believes that a good start helps new staff stay and do well. So the bank gives every new employee a two-week induction, and it uses the same programme in every country where it works. In the first week, new staff do not work at the counter alone. Instead, they shadow an experienced colleague and watch how the work is done. Each new person also gets a “buddy”. A buddy is a friendly colleague who answers small questions and helps the new person feel welcome. New staff say the buddy system makes the first days much easier.
In the second week, new staff take short online lessons about safety, service, and the bank’s rules. Because the bank works in several countries, they also learn which rules are the same everywhere and which ones are local. They practise the computer system with support. Nobody works alone until they are ready. New employees are on “probation” for three months. During this time, the manager gives regular feedback. If a new person needs more help, the manager gives it early. The bank says this careful start is worth the time: staff who feel supported learn faster and give better service.
@GLOSS
shadow — watch and follow an experienced colleague to learn
buddy — a friendly colleague who helps a new person
induction — the training period at the start of a job
probation — the first months, when your work is checked

Harbour Bank is opening a new branch in Nairobi. The branch manager, Amina Yusuf, arrives early. The branch is clean and ready, but the team is very new.
