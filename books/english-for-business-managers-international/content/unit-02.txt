@EX Part 1: Reading - Management Career Path
@INSTR Read the text and answer the questions.
_80
@PANEL
Many people start their management careers as trainees. Trainees work during their studies for a small payment, but they gain important experience. After training for one or two years, graduates may become supervisors or assistant managers. Junior managers receive training to improve their planning and their reporting. Some work on the factory floor as production supervisors, running a shift. Others work in finance as assistant accountants or in sales as account officers. The most experienced staff may become general managers who answer to the owners and train other managers. Working in management offers opportunities to learn, to grow, and to build something that lasts.

@EX Part 4: Reading - Management Structure
@INSTR Read the text and answer the questions.
_80
@PANEL
Most companies have a hierarchical structure. At the top is the General Manager who agrees the plan with the owners and answers for the result. Below the general manager are department heads who run specific areas such as production, finance, sales and purchasing. Each department head supervises supervisors and officers who carry out the daily work. Internal auditors check whether the rules were followed, and report to the owners directly.
Companies also employ support staff like storekeepers, drivers, and office administrators. While these roles may seem less visible, they are essential. For example, a driver makes sure an order reaches the customer on the promised day. A storekeeper makes sure the material is there when the shift starts. Every role is important for the company to function effectively.
Recent years have seen changes in company structures. Smaller firms operate more flexibly, with fewer management layers. This allows decisions to be made faster. However, keeping records complete and cash under control remain challenges even with simpler structures.

@PART 7 | Part 7: Culture
@EX Part 7: Business Sector in Türkiye and the Region
@INSTR Read about business management in Türkiye and the Middle East region, then answer questions.
_80
@PANEL
The business sector in Türkiye has changed significantly in response to the need to move from low-cost assembly to branded manufacturing. Large manufacturers, exporters, and small family workshops all employ managers. These managers serve both the domestic market and customers abroad. Working in Türkiye's companies requires persistence because competition, currency movement and input costs are all relentless.
In neighbouring countries like Bulgaria and Georgia, companies employ managers who sell for both local sale and export. The work is challenging but essential. These businesses demonstrate how dedicated professionals keep working in hard markets. Turkish managers are known in the region for their resourcefulness and persistence.
Many Turkish professionals who studied abroad chose to return to run companies at home. They sacrifice higher salaries in wealthy countries to employ people in their own towns. This shows deep commitment to the country and to the reputation of what it makes. Their choices inspire others to consider management careers.
