Rizal's section also provides support to Indonesian citizens abroad who need documentation assistance when returning to the country. Currently, he is reviewing and updating the ministry's diplomatic visa processing procedures to reduce waiting times for applicants. Rizal frequently liaises with foreign embassies in Jakarta to coordinate document verification processes.

She collaborates closely with the ministry's spokespersons and with foreign press offices to coordinate coverage.
Profile 4: Budi Pratama
Budi Pratama is an Economic Affairs Specialist at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. He has worked at the ministry for five years, focusing on trade diplomacy and international investment facilitation. His responsibilities include preparing briefing documents for the foreign minister ahead of bilateral economic meetings, analyzing trade policy developments in partner countries, and representing Indonesia at international economic conferences as part of the diplomatic delegation. Currently, Budi is coordinating Indonesia's participation in a major regional investment forum and is working with the German and French embassies on bilateral economic cooperation frameworks.
