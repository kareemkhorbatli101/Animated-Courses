@PANEL
Following a change of government in late 2024, the ministry's international engagement entered a new phase. Within the first twelve months, diplomatic contacts were established or upgraded with more than twenty countries, including partners from Europe, Latin America, and Asia. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs has been reorganized to reflect these new relationships, with dedicated liaison offices established for European, American, and Asian affairs respectively.
A series of bilateral cooperation frameworks have been signed in areas including transport infrastructure, educational exchange, trade facilitation, and cultural cooperation. These agreements were negotiated over a period of six to eighteen months and were signed at ceremonies in Lima attended by both foreign ministers. Implementation is being overseen by joint technical committees, which are required to report on progress every quarter. The frameworks are designed to be transparent and accountable both to the public and to international partners.
The returning-citizens program has been formally established and is being administered by the Consular Affairs Section in partnership with the International Organization for Migration. By mid-2025, more than forty thousand applications had been received from nationals living in Spain, Italy, Japan, Chile, and the United States. Priority is given to applications from skilled professionals whose expertise is needed in priority sectors, including medicine, engineering, education, and public administration. The program is being expanded to include legal assistance for citizens whose documents were lost while abroad.
International investment conferences have been held in Seoul, Madrid, and Tokyo, at which funding pledges totaling several billion dollars were made. A multilateral coordination committee was established to ensure that pledges are converted into implemented projects rather than remaining as declarations. The ministry's economic affairs team has been strengthened, and a new investment facilitation unit has been created within the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to serve as a single point of contact for international investors. The ministry's engagement is recognized regionally as one of the most significant diplomatic developments of the mid-2020s.
