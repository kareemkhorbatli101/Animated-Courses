Unit 7 — Monthly Performance

Before you start

Variance analysis, comparing actuals vs. budget, sales figures, and trend reporting.

The grammar
Adjectives (Comparatives and Superlatives) to express magnitude and difference.
What you will be able to do
Explaining that costs are higher than last month, identifying the most expensive expense category, comparing sales periods, and reporting performance trends.
Who you will meet
Aoife, Director, Junior, Speaker, Student B, Supervisor
How long
The whole unit is about 2.9 hours of work if you write every answer. Most people do it over several sittings — it saves your place, and every screen stands on its own.
What you need
Nothing. It runs in this browser, offline once the recordings have loaded, and there is no account.

By the end of this unit

  • I can identify favorable and adverse variances.
  • I can use Comparative adjectives (better/worse/more/less) to compare two financial figures.
  • I can use Superlative adjectives (best/worst/most/least) to identify the highest or lowest item.
  • I can explain why actual costs are higher than budget figures.
  • I can write a clear summary report using comparison language.
