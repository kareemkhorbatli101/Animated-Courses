Unit 5 — Accounts Payable and Receivable

Before you start

Scheduling payments, chasing debts, managing collections, and cash flow planning across currencies.

The grammar
Future Forms (will, going to, present continuous for arrangements).
What you will be able to do
Discussing when payments will be made, scheduling calls with debtors, planning the weekly payment run.
Who you will meet
Client, Colleague 1, Colleague 2, Collector, Controller, Manager
How long
The whole unit is about 2.9 hours of work if you write every answer. Most people do it over several sittings — it saves your place, and every screen stands on its own.
What you need
Nothing. It runs in this browser, offline once the recordings have loaded, and there is no account.

By the end of this unit

  • I can define and explain Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable.
  • I can discuss when invoices are due and schedule payment runs.
  • I can use Will to make predictions and offers.
  • I can use Going To for plans and intentions.
  • I can use the Present Continuous to discuss fixed professional arrangements.
