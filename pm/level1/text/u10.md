# Unit 10 · Nothing Changes Without a Request
%%SUB A baseline, a request form, and five words that change a project without one%%
%%GOALS
Ask for and give permission
Say what a baseline and a change request are
Say what a guidance standard does not prescribe
%%

Agree what you are building. Write it down. Call that the baseline. After that, nothing changes
without somebody asking and somebody answering. The hard part is not the form. The hard part is the
change that arrives in five words at the end of a telephone call.

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

Change control fails in the same way almost everywhere, and it is not because people are careless.
The rule is right — nothing changes without a record — and then the record is made so expensive
that a person on site has to choose between helping the project and following the process. Everybody
chooses the project, every time, and the changes still happen; they just stop being visible. The
cost arrives years later, when a maintenance team cannot find a valve and spends a week establishing
whether the drawing or the building is correct, and then checks everything you ever send them. The
repair is not more discipline. It is a cheaper record: four lines, two minutes, answered the same
day, with the two-page form kept for the two changes a year that genuinely need a board. Make the
honest path the easy one and the dishonest path disappears by itself.

**Discuss:** What makes the honest path expensive at your company?
