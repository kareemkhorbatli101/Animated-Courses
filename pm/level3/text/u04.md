### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

What gets a certificate gets measured, everywhere. A company can hold three hundred records about
concrete and two about how it runs projects, and nobody involved is negligent: one of those things
can be audited by an outside body and the other cannot, and the files grow where the attention is.
The guidance that asks for both is explicitly not a certification standard, which means the gap it
describes is the one gap nobody will ever be rewarded for closing. The repair has to be small enough
to survive that: eleven lines on one page, each written in the passive so it is about the work and
not about a person, with an empty box where something did not happen. The empty boxes are the whole
value, and the first month there will be a lot of them.

**Discuss:** What does your company believe about how it works that nothing records?
