# Unit 12 · One Page of Method Decisions
%%SUB Eleven documents, thirty-four things taken, sixty-one left, and a signature under the word tailored%%
%%GOALS

Eleven methods in eleven units. This one puts them on a single page: which document, which edition,
what we adopted, what we left, who decided, and what the document itself says it does not cover. One
standard in this unit asks for exactly that page, and it is the only honest way to use any of them.

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

Eleven units of methods come down to one page, and the page is not about the methods at all. Every
document in this book was written by people who knew it would be adopted in part, and the better ones
say so: they offer tailored conformance, they expect adjustment, they list what they do not cover. The
single document that refuses to be tailored is also the shortest, and it is the one most often claimed
by organizations holding half of it. What separates a professional method statement from a brochure
is not the number of elements adopted but four columns: what was taken, what was left, why, and who
decided. The fourth column is the one that gets dropped, and dropping it has a predictable cost —
within about eighteen months an omission with no author has become a rule with defenders, and the
reason it exists has left the building. Write the name. It is the cheapest line on the page and the
only one that is still useful in two years.

**Discuss:** Who decided what your organization leaves out?
