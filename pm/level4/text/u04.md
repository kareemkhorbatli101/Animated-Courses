leave elements out and you lose the benefit, and may render the whole thing useless. That single

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

A name does work, which is why people resist giving it up. Teams everywhere hold three of five
events, keep the word on the wall, and are not being dishonest: each missing piece was dropped for a
reason that made sense on the day, usually because it produced no deliverable and nobody noticed its
absence. The retrospective goes first, in month two, because there is *nothing to discuss* — in the
one month of a project when there is almost nothing except things to discuss, none of them
comfortable. What makes this document unusual is that it refuses the tailoring every other method in
this book invites. That refusal is a design claim and not arrogance: each part is there to make a
particular failure visible, and removing the part removes the visibility rather than the failure.
The honest response is not to defend the name. It is to describe the practice accurately, which is
usually quite respectable.
