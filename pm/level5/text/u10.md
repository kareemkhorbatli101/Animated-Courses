# Unit 10 · Line of Sight
%%SUB Thirty-one actions, four that reach the top, and a benefit of 84,000 a year nobody records%%
%%GOALS
Describe a chain of derivation
Trace an action to an objective and an objective to a plan

A standard published in 2014 describes how value is realized from assets, and the idea at its center
is alignment: what you do to an asset should be traceable to what the organization is trying to
achieve. Two years after handover, four of our thirty-one actions reach the top, and the benefit that
justified the dearer pump is measured by nobody.

> **Grammar Note** — *Flows from* and *is aligned with* are not the same claim. *Flows from* says one

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

A chain of plans breaks silently in the middle, and the work continues perfectly well on either side
of the break, which is why it can go unnoticed for years. People do sensible things in support of
objectives they have never read, and the documents stay tidy because each one was written carefully
by somebody looking at the level immediately above or below. The second habit is more costly. A
benefit that justified a capital decision disappears unless somebody writes it into an objective and
reports it, and the person who would benefit from measuring it — the one who argued for the dearer
option — has usually left the project by the time it could be measured. So the file ends up holding a
business case with no outcome, and at the next decision the honest reading of the file is *we spent
eight hundred thousand extra and nobody knows what happened*. Measuring it two years late is
awkward, which is exactly why it is worth doing.

**Discuss:** Which benefit at your organization justified a decision and is measured by nobody?

### 🗣 Group ranking

*In fours.* Four of thirty-one, two broken links, eighty-four thousand a year. Rank these, best to
worst. You must agree.

a. Give every benefit in a business case an objective and an owner at handover
b. Rewrite the objective so it measures what the organizational plan targets
c. Replace the cycle with condition-based intervention
d. Sort the twenty-two untraceable actions into handrails and habit
e. Report the metered energy figure every year, even when nobody asks

Report your order, and say which one would have prevented the silence.
