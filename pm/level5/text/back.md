# Grammar reference

Every structure in this book, in unit order. The second column is the mistake it prevents, which is
the column worth reading twice.

## Starting Point · Four questions to ask any number

| | |
|---|---|
| **A number of what, exactly?** | design, land, connection charges, spares, client staff, tax — a price without a boundary is not a price |
| **How accurate is it, and who says so?** | a single figure with no range is silent about its own accuracy, and silence reads as confidence |
| **Which year's money, over what period?** | three million today and three million in year twelve are different amounts |
| **Who is obliged by it, and from when?** | a number in a report obliges nobody; a number in a contract obliges somebody, usually within a stated number of days |

## Unit 1 · Ranges and accuracy

| | |
|---|---|
| ***plus … minus* · *between … and* · *of the order of* · *no better than*** | a figure with no range invites a template with one cell to delete it |
| **class 5 = 0–2 % defined; class 3 = 10–40 %** | the class is set by how much is designed, not by how long somebody spent |
| **accuracy is not precision** | 11.843 million at concept stage is a lie told in three decimal places |
| **12 issued with a range of 6 to 24; outcome 16.1** | the estimate was correct and the budget was wrong, and they were the same number |

## Unit 2 · Proportional change

| | |
|---|---|
| ***for every doubling* · *falls by* · *falls to* · *each successive* · *by a factor of*** | *falls by 85 percent* leaves fifteen; *falls to 85 percent* leaves eighty-five |
| **the rate applies per doubling, not per unit** | units 16 to 32 is one doubling and contains sixteen units, which is why the curve flattens |
| **material does not learn** | forty-six units need forty-six units' worth of steel, whatever the labor does |
| **8,280 hours against about 4,400** | and an eleven-week interruption put the outcome at 5,150, because the curve assumes continuity |

## Unit 3 · Long horizons

| | |
|---|---|
| ***over a period of* · *by year …* · *in present-value terms* · *at a real discount rate of*** | a saving with no period and no rate cannot be checked by anybody |
| **real rates go with today's money; nominal rates with inflated figures** | mixing them always flatters the option with the lower capital cost |
| **3 years says A; 30 years says B; they cross at year 12** | the period and the rate are policy, and they decide the answer before the arithmetic starts |
| **life-cycle cost is not whole-life cost** | emissions, income and land sit outside the narrower term |

## Unit 4 · Appraisal conditionals

| | |
|---|---|
| ***were it to fall by* · *if … were* · *on the assumption that* · *the switching value is*** | *the result is sensitive to X* has no direction and no size, and is forgotten in a week |
| **FNPV −6.2 with ENPV +14.8** | a negative financial result with a positive economic one is the ordinary case for public works |
| **the switching value of the weakest input was −23 %** | and the input was a figure transferred from a study in another country |
| **a survey at 40,000 against a capital cost of 14 million** | refused for timetable reasons, which is a decision and not a deadline |

## Unit 5 · Time-bar obligations

| | |
|---|---|
| ***within … days of* · *no later than* · *the period runs from* · *failing which*** | a time bar needs a length, a trigger and a consequence, and loses its force without any one |
| **the period runs from awareness, not from instruction** | the clock starts in a foreman's handwriting, and the obligation belongs to somebody else |
| **28 days printed; 21 days agreed** | the particular conditions prevail, and the site instruction pack was copied from another job |
| **knowledge is not notice** | the engineer standing in the excavation protects no entitlement |

## Unit 6 · The contract present tense

| | |
|---|---|
| **the contractor *notifies*; the project manager *replies within* two weeks** | the present simple is an obligation expressed as a fact, and is read by newcomers as advice |
| ***could*, not *will*** | the threshold is possibility; waiting for probability converts a weekly duty into a quarterly one |
| **a warning is worth the float that exists on the day it is given** | eleven weeks of float and three options in week 3; none and one in week 14 |
| **no accepted programme for eleven weeks** | three compensation events assessed against nothing, and chasing the document was nobody's job |

## Unit 7 · Scope limitation

| | |
|---|---|
| ***covers … but not* · *says nothing about* · *is silent on* · *as far as … is concerned*** | three letters settle carriage, cost and risk, and are silent on title, payment, law and remedies |
| **a rule, a named place, and the edition year** | without the year, identical letters carry different insurance obligations |
| **a sea rule cannot name an inland site** | risk passed on board, 1,900 km before the damage |
| **186 uninsured on a road leg** | and the supplier complied with the order exactly |

## Unit 8 · Weighting and evaluation

| | |
|---|---|
| ***weighted at* · *accounts for* · *carries … points* · *on a weighting of*** | a weighting means nothing without the spread of the thing it weights |
| **22 points of quality at 10 percent move a total by 2.2** | against 5.4 for a six percent price difference |
| **a criterion everybody passes is page count** | twenty dead points reduced the effective quality weighting from ten percent to eight |
| **31 passes, 0 stops; reversing at 70/30** | the weighting chose the winner, and the weighting came from a pipe order |

## Unit 9 · Requirement and recommendation

| | |
|---|---|
| ***shall* · *should* · *may* · *must be able to demonstrate* · *failing which*** | copying a *should* into a contract does not promote it; the sentence arrives with its weakness attached |
| **four parts: obligation, threshold, proof, consequence** | leave out the proof and you are told it is met; leave out the consequence and you are told it will be |
| **2 of 11 suppliers could comply** | an unachievable requirement is waived at the first refusal, and a requirement waived once is gone |
| **60 percent now, 90 percent from month 18** | a dated threshold tells a supplier it is worth replacing a machine |

## Unit 10 · Chains of derivation

| | |
|---|---|
| ***flows from* · *is derived from* · *in support of* · *which in turn* · *traceable to*** | *is aligned with* says two documents do not contradict; *flows from* says one was written out of the other |
| **a condition grade is not an interruption** | the objective measured metal and the plan measured whether water arrived |
| **4 of 31 actions reached the top** | and one of the twenty-two untraceable actions was a handrail that should be fitted this month |
| **38 percent energy saving, about 84 a year** | the benefit that justified the capital decision, measured by nobody for two years |

## Unit 11 · Evaluative judgment

| | |
|---|---|
| ***on the criterion of* · *judged against* · *to the extent that* · *cannot be assessed because*** | a score outlives its reasoning by about five years; a reasoned refusal does not |
| **six criteria since 2019; coherence was the addition** | five of them look at you and only coherence looks sideways |
| **two schemes, one aquifer, fourteen months apart** | two correct procedures produced a question that belonged to nobody |
| **applied in order, with equal effort** | which the guidance warns against, and four of six paragraphs were worth little |

## Unit 12 · Provision and contingency

| | |
|---|---|
| **three tests: present obligation from a past event · probable outflow · reliable estimate** | the contingency fails the first, and almost everybody guesses the third |
| **recognized is not disclosed** | an item can be certain to matter and correctly appear only in a note |
| **no provision for future operating losses** | the accounts state a position at a date; the risk register states a view of the future |
| **contingency 1.90 · provision 0.34 · gap 1.56** | two honest documents, one shared word, three years |

# The British layer

This book is written in American English, and the series carries a switchable British layer: a fixed
list of words is respelled at build time. The list lives with the series rather than in this book.

What the layer does not touch:

1. **The date.** Write the month in letters where the sentence is prose, and the international format
   where it is data.
2. **A quoted document keeps its own spelling.** Every standard, contract form, guide and paper
   quoted here is quoted as published.
3. **A document number keeps its published form.** ISO 15686-5:2017, ISO 10845-1:2020, ISO 20400:2017,
   ISO 55000:2014 and IAS 37 are citations, not words.
4. **Money has no symbol anywhere in this series.** Every figure is in whatever unit your own project
   uses, which is also why this book names no currency and no jurisdiction.
5. **A name is never respelled.** Wright, Boehm, Shewhart, Deming, Cooper and Kotter are spelled as
   published.

**Programme** keeps that spelling throughout, as in Books 1 to 4: it is the term ISO 21506:2018
defines, and the series uses it as that standard's defined term.

# Sources

Twelve source bodies carry the twelve reading texts, one per unit, and the Starting Point carries
none. Every one is a published standard, contract form, guide, paper or reporting standard, cited with
its edition or date, and every reading text prints what the publisher or the author says the document
does **not** cover. No source appears in any other book of this series or in its four sister series,
and no figure is reproduced from any of them: every chart, form and table in this book was drawn for
it, with invented numbers.

|---|---|---|

| 2 | a paper on the factors affecting the cost of airplanes, reporting the learning curve | **Journal of the Aeronautical Sciences, February 1936** |
| 3 | a standard on life-cycle costing for buildings and constructed assets | **ISO 15686-5:2017**, second edition, replacing 2008 |

**The honesty rule of this series, in this book's own words.** Every reading text names its source,
its edition and its date, and then prints the limits the source states about itself. In this book the
limits do most of the teaching, and the pattern that emerges by Unit 12 is the whole point of the
book: **in every one of the eleven stories the document was honest about itself and somebody used it
for the adjacent purpose.**

- **Unit 1.** A practice that classifies estimates by how much is defined, contains no cost data, and
  cannot stop a class 5 figure becoming a budget — which is the only failure that occurred.
- **Unit 2.** An empirical relationship from 1930s airframe manufacture, about direct labor and not
  material, that presumes continuity of production — and an eleven-week interruption proved it.
- **Unit 3.** A standard that supplies neither the period of analysis nor the discount rate, which
  are the two numbers that decided the answer.
- **Unit 4.** A guide whose rates are benchmarks, whose shadow prices need national data it does not
  supply, and which says plainly that cost-benefit analysis is one input to a decision and not the
  decision.
- **Unit 5.** A standard form intended to be amended by particular conditions, whose printed
  twenty-eight days was not our twenty-one — and our own site pack carried the printed figure.
- **Unit 6.** A contract that assumes active administration and cannot produce an accepted programme
  by itself; its early warning clause creates a route and not a motive.
- **Unit 7.** Three letters that settle carriage, cost and risk and are silent on ownership, payment,
  governing law and remedies — all four of which are what people argue about.
- **Unit 8.** A standard that supplies the evaluation method and leaves the weighting and the criteria
  to the employer, and cannot make a criterion discriminate.
- **Unit 9.** Guidance that says of itself that it is not a management system standard and is not
  intended for certification, written in recommendations — which we pasted into a contract.
- **Unit 10.** An overview and terminology standard with no requirements in it, which describes what
  alignment is and leaves you to discover that you have none.
- **Unit 11.** Criteria rather than indicators, with no weighting and no measurement method, whose own
  guidance warns against using them as a checklist — which is what we did.
- **Unit 12.** A financial reporting standard that regulates a statement of position and not a plan,
  and whose word *probable* does not mean what a risk register means by it.

# A note on everything else in this book

Every person, team, company, project, number and incident in this book is invented. Lindhurst does
not exist; neither do the Calder valley scheme, the forty-six kiosks, the three transformers, the two
pump options, the eleven plant hire firms, the aquifer, the 2027 subsidy review or any of the people
who appear in the dialogues. The documents are real and cited; the stories around them are written to
teach the language.

**Nothing in this book is legal, financial, contractual, tax or accounting advice, and it is not a
substitute for reading the document you are working under.** Several of the documents discussed here
are copyright works sold by the bodies that publish them, and some are contract forms whose precise
wording is the whole of their effect; this book describes what they contain only in order to teach the
English used to discuss them, and prints what each one says it does not cover. Where a sentence sounds
like a rule — *within twenty-one days of becoming aware*, *not less than ninety percent recycled
content*, *failing which the delivery is rejected* — that is one invented company's internal practice
or a paraphrase for teaching, printed because a concrete number teaches better than an abstraction.
**Your clause is the one in your contract. Read it.**

# Closing

You began this book able to say what something costs. You end it able to say **what the figure is a
number of**, **what range it carries and at what level of definition**, **which year's money it is in
and over what period**, **who is obliged by it and from what date**, **what the price does not
include**, **which weighting decided the award**, **which test an item fails**, and **what each of
your numbers is not**.

Eleven documents, and one habit: **write the fourth column.** The number, the document, the owner,
and what it is not.

Six sentences to take with you.

From Unit 1: **a single figure is silent about its own accuracy, and silence reads as confidence.**

From Unit 3: **the period and the rate decide the answer before the arithmetic starts.**

From Unit 5: **the clock starts in somebody else's handwriting.**

From Unit 6: **a warning is worth the float that exists on the day you give it.**

From Unit 9: **sixty percent enforced beats a hundred percent waived.**

From Unit 12: **if you cannot say what a number is not, you do not yet know what it is.**

Book 6 takes the last step: who is accountable when all of this has been done correctly and the thing
still fails.
