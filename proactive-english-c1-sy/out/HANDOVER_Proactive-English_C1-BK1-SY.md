## 7 · Register rules

- **Hedging:** one per sentence, never two. **Approximators** (*about · roughly · between X and
  Y · on average · out of*) are not hedges.
- **Reported speech:** write *said* unless you can say out loud why you have chosen something
  else. **Unit 10 is where the exceptions become the subject** — *stated · claimed · conceded ·
  maintained* — and the rule afterwards is that the verb is chosen once and deliberately.
- **A figure never appears without its base, its period and its counter.** At C1 this is turned
  inwards in Unit 12: a claim about your own writing needs its denominator too.
- **A limitation goes inside the sentence it limits**, from 9.3 on. A limit in its own sentence
  is a separate object and can be dropped without anything appearing to break — which is the
  finding of 6.5 and the reason for the rule.
- **No Americanisms, no policy vocabulary, no currency symbols.** `qa.sh` sweeps all three with
  `\b` boundaries. ⚠ **Sweep the built PDF and not the source** — `color:` is a docx option key
  and flags on every page of every script.
- **No sentence over 45 words**, checked on the built text.
- ⚠ **`lines(N)` prints one rule, not N.** Consecutive identically-bordered empty paragraphs
  merge in the renderer. `lib.js` is frozen and byte-identical across all four trees, so this is
  the as-built behaviour of the whole series and must not be "fixed" here. **A ruled writing
  block reads as a blank page to any text-based sweep.**

## 8 · Toolchain, and the page-count levers

- `build.sh <script> <Out.docx> ["preview pages"]` · `build.sh bound` · `build.sh zip <name>`.
  Exports `PE_LEVEL=C1` and `PE_BOOK` (default 1). ⚠ The script argument is the **bare script
  name**, no `pipeline/` and no `.js`. ⚠ `build.sh bound` writes **docx only** — convert the two
  bound files by hand:
  `python3 /mnt/skills/public/docx/scripts/office/soffice.py --headless --convert-to pdf --outdir <dir> <file>.docx`.
- `bound.js` is **one parameterised script across all four trees**, unmodified. It inserts
  `midtest`/`midkey` after unit 06 and `endtest`/`endkey` at the end.
- QA scripts live in the scratchpad: `qa.sh <unit> <pdf> [source.js]` and
  `akqa.sh <unit> <pdf> [source.js]`. Both end in `RESULT: PASS` or `*** CHECK ***`.
  Helpers: `measure.py` (the six house figures per cycle), `lexcheck.py` (a proposed lexis set
  against B2, against the other C1 units, and against A2/B1 occurrence for *Back at C1*),
  `app9.py` (appends a cycle fragment before a `//__TAIL__` marker), `ak_reports.py` (lifts the
  seven 4R reports out of the Student's Book into the key).
- **Fix scripts.** Edit the JS with Python, never by hand: build the list of (old, new) pairs,
  **assert every needle occurs exactly once in a first loop**, replace in a second, and write
  once at the end — so a failed assert leaves the file untouched.
  ⚠ The JS files store apostrophes as `\'`; a needle with a bare `'` will not match.
  ⚠ **Never begin or end a needle with `**` unless the bold run actually starts or ends there.**
  This was the single most frequent failure of the whole book — about a dozen times. `grep -o`
  the real text first.

### The page-count levers at C1, in the order to try them
⚠ **At C1 the page count follows the number of unsplittable blocks, not the word count.** This is
the one structural finding of Book 1 and it governs Book 2's planning.
1. **Prose barely moves a page.** About 250 words came out of Unit 9's audio script and the unit
   did not lose a page.
2. **A `box()` or `table()` that will not fit jumps whole**, because `box()` is `cantSplit`. The
   closing *Vocabulary of this unit* box therefore takes a page of its own on any unit that has
   reached 49, and no amount of trimming above it will help.
3. **A redundant `pb()`** is still the cheapest page, where one exists. A seventh `pb()` was
   found before 8.6 and removed.
4. ⚠ **Unit 9 was trimmed three times before the band was widened** — the nine-degrees table
   regrouped into a box, then folded into the language box, then the vocabulary box shortened —
   **and it measured 50 after all three.** The band was amended to 45–50 and the reason recorded
   in plan §16. **Do not gut a cycle to hit a projection.**
5. **The Answer Key has no such closing block** and its content is paragraphs, which is why its
   band held for twelve units without one amendment. **Grow a short key with real content** —
   board tables, Card B samples, a traceability table — **never with padding.**

## 9 · Faults found and fixed — watch for these again

1. ⚠ **Examination items with more than one valid answer. Fifteen of them, and no automated check
   can find one.** Unit 8 check items 3 (*for*) and 4 (*Although*); Unit 9 check item 6 (*on
   page 14* / *at page 14*); **Unit 10 Reading Part 1 items 1, 3 and 8** — *taken away*, *scale
   back* and *set out* each fitted as well as the key; Unit 12 check items 1, 4, 5 and 6 —
   *had deleted*, *which I enjoyed*, *is costing*, *to report*. **The pattern is always one of
   three shapes:** a preposition or conjunction that happens to fit the frame, a continuous or
   perfect form that is grammatical in a different reading, or a true statement about the text
