Four students on how they chose

A Teodora: I chose the course because of the city, and I would say that out loud to anybody who asked. Three years is a long time to be somewhere you do not like, and the course content at the six places I looked at was almost identical anyway.

B Nnamdi: I picked by employment figures and I now think that was a mistake. The numbers were real, but they described people who had finished five years before me, in a different economy. I chose using a photograph of the past.

C Isolde: Nobody in my family had been, so I applied to the one I had heard of. My teacher spent an afternoon showing me that there were forty others, and that the one I had heard of was famous for a subject I was not taking. That afternoon changed my life, and it should not have depended on one teacher having time.

D Pavel: I went to an open day at a place I had already decided against and changed my mind within an hour. What did it was the students, not the building or the talk. I would tell anybody to go, including to the ones you think you do not want.
