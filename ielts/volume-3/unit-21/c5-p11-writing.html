Take the cycle 5 passage. For each of its eight paragraphs, mark where you would split it, or write "no split" and say why.

The books rate this rather than mark it, so nothing here is scored. Open Learn this for what a good answer does.

1.Which paragraphs did you split, and on what word?
2.Which paragraph was hardest to find a seam in?
3.Did splitting let you close a question earlier than you otherwise would?
4.How many paragraphs did you read twice?
5.What would you do differently on the next passage?
