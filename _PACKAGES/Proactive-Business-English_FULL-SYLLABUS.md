# Proactive Business English · Syria Edition · A1–B2

## The full syllabus, unit by unit

**Author: Mo (Mohamad Khorbatli).**

**8 books · 84 units · 588 teaching cycles · 5,442 pages.**

Every unit runs **seven cycles**, and cycle 7 is always **Review & Mediate**. Each cycle is one 90-minute lesson in thirteen stages: Lesson A speaking (Get ready · Listen and read · Practise · Perform · Your turn · Report) and Lesson B writing (Read · Write with help · Check together · Share · Write freely · Respond · Share · Homework).

The objectives below are the books' own, copied from each unit's *In this unit you will…* page.
