What you can do at the end of this unit

Operator track

  • I can name the jobs and the services I find in a bank, describe what banking is like where I live, and talk about a recent visit to a bank.
  • I can use this unit's banking roles and service words correctly, tell a front-office job from a back-office one and say why, and write a short paragraph using at least five of them.
  • I can tell a routine from something happening now, choose present simple or present continuous for each, correct the commonest mistake between the two, and say both what I usually do and what I am working on this week.
  • I can predict what a new colleague says on a first day, follow three workplace conversations closely enough to judge True, False or Not Given, and match each person to what they are doing at the moment.
  • I can introduce myself with my name, my role, my routine duties and my current task, take either part in a professional exchange, and ask the questions that close an information gap.
  • I can read a bank's company profile for its standing facts and its current plans, say which tense carries which, and describe a colleague's role from a short staff profile.
  • I can recognise the parts of a professional introduction email and what each is for, complete one from a word list, and write my own in 50 to 70 words.
  • I can use this unit's roles, services and tenses again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.

Practitioner track

  • I can name the jobs and the services I find in a bank, describe what banking is like where I live, and talk about a recent visit to a bank.
  • I can welcome a new colleague as a manager does: use the phrases that put a new starter at ease, work through an induction checklist in order, and brief a new hire on their first week.
  • I can read how a large bank onboards its staff, take the vocabulary of induction from it, and set out my own view in writing.
  • I can read what happens when a branch opens, weigh what the branch should do first, and defend the order I choose.
  • I can use this unit's roles, services and tenses again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.
