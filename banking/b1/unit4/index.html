What you can do at the end of this unit

Operator track

  • I can name the products my bank offers and say which kind of customer each one suits.
  • I can use this unit's product words correctly, tell one account type from another, and write about a product using at least five of the words.
  • I can compare two products with a comparative and name the best of several with a superlative, form both correctly from short and long adjectives, and correct the commonest mistake.
  • I can predict what a customer asks about a product, follow three product conversations, and identify which product was recommended and on what grounds.
  • I can explain a product's features and its cost plainly, compare two products aloud for a customer, and recommend one without overselling it.
  • I can read a product sheet for its rates, fees and conditions, find the comparisons it makes, and say which customer it is for.
  • I can recognise the parts of a recommendation email, complete one from a word list, and write my own in 50 to 70 words.
  • I can use this unit's product words and comparisons again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.

Practitioner track

  • I can name the products my bank offers and say which kind of customer each one suits.
  • I can brief a team on a new product as a manager does: say what it is for, what has changed, what staff must tell customers, and what they must not promise.
  • I can read how a product is launched across countries, take its vocabulary, and give my own view on what must be adapted locally.
  • I can read a small business's loan request, weigh the case for and against, and make a decision I can justify.
  • I can use this unit's product words and comparisons again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.
