What you can do at the end of this unit

Operator track

  • I can say why people send money abroad, and describe how it is usually sent where I live.
  • I can use this unit's exchange and transfer words correctly, read a rate board, and write about a transfer using at least five of the words.
  • I can turn a direct question into an indirect one, choose the right opening for the politeness the situation needs, correct the commonest word-order mistake, and ask a customer for sensitive details courteously.
  • I can predict what a customer asks about sending money, follow three transfer conversations, and identify the fee, the rate and the time quoted in each.
  • I can quote a rate and a fee clearly, explain why the amount received differs from the amount sent, and take a transfer request from start to confirmation.
  • I can read a transfer instruction for its parties, its reference and its charges, find the indirect questions in it, and say what is still missing.
  • I can recognise the parts of an inquiry and its reply, complete one from a word list, and write my own inquiry in 50 to 70 words.
  • I can use this unit's transfer words and indirect questions again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.

Practitioner track

  • I can say why people send money abroad, and describe how it is usually sent where I live.
  • I can handle a rate query as a manager does: explain a spread without jargon, hold a line I cannot move, and offer what I can.
  • I can read how money actually crosses a border, take the vocabulary of correspondent banking from it, and explain the route to a customer.
  • I can read what a branch does for families receiving money from abroad, weigh cost against speed, and decide.
  • I can use this unit's transfer words and indirect questions again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.
