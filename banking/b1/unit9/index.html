What you can do at the end of this unit

Operator track

  • I can say why people save, and what stops them.
  • I can use this unit's saving and investing words correctly, tell a saving product from an investment one, and write about a saving goal using at least five of the words.
  • I can tell what a customer has done from what they did, choose present perfect or past simple correctly, correct the commonest mistake, and ask about a customer's saving history in both.
  • I can predict what a customer says about their savings, follow three planning conversations, and identify the goal, the amount and the timescale in each.
  • I can help a customer set a saving goal they can actually meet, explain interest plainly, and avoid promising a return I cannot guarantee.
  • I can read a savings plan for its goal, its monthly amount and its term, find the perfect and past forms in it, and say whether the plan is realistic.
  • I can recognise the parts of a short saving plan, complete one from a word list, and write my own in 50 to 70 words.
  • I can use this unit's saving words and the perfect and past forms again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.

Practitioner track

  • I can say why people save, and what stops them.
  • I can coach a savings goal as a manager does: ask the questions that uncover what the customer really wants, and set a target they will keep.
  • I can read how a bank built a saving habit among its customers, take its vocabulary, and judge what would work where I am.
  • I can read what a branch advises when prices are rising, weigh saving against spending, and give advice I can defend.
  • I can use this unit's saving words and the perfect and past forms again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.
