What you can do at the end of this unit

Operator track

  • I can say how banking for a business differs from banking for a person.
  • I can use this unit's corporate-banking words correctly, name what a business customer needs that a personal one does not, and write about a business account using at least five of the words.
  • I can report what a customer said without changing its meaning, shift the tense and the pronouns correctly, correct the commonest mistake, and write up what was agreed in a meeting.
  • I can predict what a business customer asks for, follow three meetings, and identify what each customer asked and what the bank undertook to do.
  • I can open and close a meeting with a business customer, summarise what was agreed back to them, and confirm the next step and its date.
  • I can read a meeting note for its decisions and its actions, find the reported speech that carries them, and say what is still unresolved.
  • I can recognise the parts of a meeting note and a business reply, complete one from a word list, and write my own in 50 to 70 words.
  • I can use this unit's corporate words and reported speech again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.

Practitioner track

  • I can say how banking for a business differs from banking for a person.
  • I can manage a relationship as a manager does: prepare for a review meeting, raise a difficult point without damaging the relationship, and agree what both sides will do.
  • I can read how a bank kept a corporate client for twenty years, take its vocabulary, and say what sustained it.
  • I can read what a branch does for a workshop that needs finance, weigh the risk against the relationship, and decide.
  • I can use this unit's corporate words and reported speech again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.
