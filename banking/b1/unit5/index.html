What you can do at the end of this unit

Operator track

  • I can describe how people bank online where I live, and say what stops some customers from using an app.
  • I can use this unit's digital-banking words correctly, name the steps of logging in and making a payment, and write about an app using at least five of the words.
  • I can tell active from passive, form the passive to describe what the system does without naming who does it, correct the commonest mistake, and explain a process to a customer in it.
  • I can predict what a customer asks about an app, follow three support conversations, and identify the step where each customer is stuck.
  • I can talk a customer through the app one step at a time, check they have followed each step, and reassure someone who is nervous about security.
  • I can read a digital-service notice for what the system does and when, find the passives that carry it, and say what the customer must do.
  • I can recognise the parts of a customer notice, complete one from a word list, and write my own clear notice in 50 to 70 words.
  • I can use this unit's digital words and the passive again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.

Practitioner track

  • I can describe how people bank online where I live, and say what stops some customers from using an app.
  • I can lead a digital change as a manager does: explain why it is happening, answer the objection I expect, and say what support staff and customers will get.
  • I can read about a bank with no branches, take its vocabulary, and argue what is gained and what is lost.
  • I can read what a branch does where the signal is weak, weigh what can and cannot move online, and decide.
  • I can use this unit's digital words and the passive again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.
