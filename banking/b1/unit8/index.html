Unit 8 · Rules and Compliance

In this unit you learn the language of banking rules, how to explain an obligation politely, how to follow a conversation about a check, and how to write a short procedure note. Grammar focus: obligation — must, have to, mustn’t, don’t have to.

What you can do at the end of this unit

Operator track

  • I can say which bank rules customers find hardest to accept, and why the rules exist.
  • I can use this unit's compliance words correctly, distinguish an identity check from a source-of-funds check, and write about a procedure using at least five of the words.
  • I can tell must from have to and mustn't from don't have to, choose the right one for a rule, correct the commonest mistake, and state an obligation to a customer politely.
  • I can predict what a customer objects to in a check, follow three compliance conversations, and identify what was required and what was produced.
  • I can explain a requirement without apologising for it, ask for a document courteously, and hold the rule when a customer pushes back.
  • I can read a procedure note for what is mandatory and what is not, find the obligation language that carries it, and say what happens if a step is missed.
  • I can recognise the parts of a procedure note, complete one from a word list, and write my own in 50 to 70 words.
  • I can use this unit's compliance words and obligation language again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.

Practitioner track

  • I can say which bank rules customers find hardest to accept, and why the rules exist.
  • I can handle an escalation as a manager does: decide what I can settle, what must go higher, and how to tell the customer without promising an outcome.
  • I can read how know-your-customer works in practice, take its vocabulary, and explain to a customer why it applies to them.
  • I can read what a branch does about an unusually large deposit, weigh the duty to report against the duty to serve, and decide.
  • I can use this unit's compliance words and obligation language again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.
