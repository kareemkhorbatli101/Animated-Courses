### 2 · Two regions, two grades

### 3 · Two refineries

%%FIG ra00-f3 | A card taped inside a notebook, printed with two refinery entries, one above the other, each giving a name, a year and a capacity, with a line beneath reading nameplate is not output.%%

**3A  Read the card. Then say what the word *nameplate* means.**

> **Homs.** Commissioned **1959**. Nameplate capacity around **107,000 barrels a day**.
>
> **Banias.** Commissioned **1979**. Nameplate capacity around **133,000 barrels a day**.
>
> **Together: about 240,000 barrels a day of nameplate capacity.**

**Nameplate capacity** is what a plant was designed to do. It is not what it did last month. In Unit
12 you will meet the difference between the two, and in Book 4 you will meet why it matters.
