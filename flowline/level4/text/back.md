## Unit 1 · Procedure; repeatability and reproducibility

| | |
|---|---|
| A hundred milliliters **is measured into** the flask. | the sample and the vessel |
| The thermometer **is fitted at** a specified depth. | a position |
| The bath **is held at** a specified temperature. | a controlled condition |
| The heater **is brought up at** a specified rate. | a controlled rate |
| The temperature **is recorded when** the first drop leaves. | an event trigger |
| Two results from the **same operator** may differ by up to **r**. | repeatability |
| Two **laboratories** may differ by up to **R**, which is **wider**. | reproducibility |
| The result is **inside the limit but within the reproducibility of the method**. | the honest report |

> **Careful:** two correct results from one sample can differ. Neither laboratory is wrong.

## Unit 2 · Hedging a generalization; a correlation's range

| | |
|---|---|
| The duty is **approximately** ten megawatts. | a figure, not exact |
| Gas oils **typically** fall in this range. | the usual case |
| The cost is **of the order of** ten million. | the right factor of ten |
| The chart is good **to within about** five per cent. | a stated accuracy |
| **Broadly**, the yield rises with temperature. | a direction |
| **At best** it is a first estimate. | the ceiling on the claim |
| The correlation **was fitted over** densities from 0.78 to 0.92. | the range |
| Reading a value outside it is **an extrapolation**. | the right word |

> **Careful:** inside the range is interpolation; outside it is extrapolation. Use the word.

## Unit 3 · A process history; naming a kind of document

| | |
|---|---|
| The process **was developed** in the 1930s. | impersonally |
| It **was commercialized** in 1937. | the first commercial use |
| Thermal cracking **had already been** in use for a decade. | the earlier state |
| It **was superseded by** fluid catalytic cracking, **within about** five years. | what replaced it, and when |
| This is **a commemoration**, not a technical document. | naming the kind |
| The engineering **is not in dispute**; the popular story **is a simplification**. | separating the two |

> **Careful:** a commemoration is reliable about what it is for. Celebration selects.

## Unit 4 · Deadlines and phasing; two specifications

| | |
|---|---|
| Transposed **by** a stated date. | the latest moment |
| Ten milligrams per kilogram, **with effect from** 1 January 2009. | when it starts |
| **No later than** 31 December. | the same, in legal drafting |
| **From that date** the earlier limit no longer applied. | after a point |
| The earlier limit applied **until** that date. | before it |
| **During a transitional period**, a higher limit was permitted. | a temporary exception |
| Both grades **comply — with different instruments**. | the honest summary |

> **Careful:** *by 1 January* and *from 1 January* are not the same instruction.

## Unit 5 · Contrast; saying what a number is

| | |
|---|---|
| The research method runs at the lower speed, **whereas** the motor method runs at the higher. | one clause against another |
| **While** the research number is higher, the motor test is harder. | softer, at the front |
| **By contrast**, the motor method heats the inlet mixture. | a new sentence |
| **On the other hand**, neither predicts modern engine behavior. | the second view |
| **The former** is less severe; **the latter** more so. | two things already named |
| An octane number is **the result of a comparison**, not **a measurement of** anything. | what kind of number it is |

> **Careful:** 95 is not a quantity in the fuel. It is the percentage of a reference liquid in a
> matching blend.

## Unit 6 · Specification language; commercial and statutory

| | |
|---|---|
| Viscosity, **maximum** 380. | a ceiling |
| Flash point, **minimum** 60 degrees. | a floor |
| Water **not more than** the tabulated figure. | a ceiling, in words |
| Flash point **not less than** 60 degrees. | a floor, in words |

## Unit 7 · Attributing a figure; comparing capacities

| | |
|---|---|
| **According to** the 2024 bulletin, about 240,000 barrels a day. | the publication |
| **As reported by** the compiler. | the body publishing |
| **The figure given for** refining capacity **is** a nameplate figure. | what the entry is |
| **As supplied by** the national authority. | who provided it |
| **Estimated by** the compiler, **on the basis of** secondary sources. | how it was derived |
| Utilization is throughput **divided by** capacity — **which** capacity? | the ambiguity |

> **Careful:** never write *capacity* or *utilization* alone. There are four of the first and three
> denominators for the second.

## Unit 8 · Targets and theoretical limits; trade-offs

| | |
|---|---|
| **The theoretical minimum** heating is 24 megawatts. | the least possible, under assumptions |
| It is **a target rather than a prediction**. | what kind of number it is |
| **Achievable in principle** with a fully connected network. | the condition |
| We got **as close as** 31 megawatts, **within about** thirty per cent. | the gap |
| **In practice**, two of the matches cannot be made. | reality |
| A smaller approach recovers more heat **and** needs larger exchangers: the two **trade against each other**. | the trade-off |

> **Careful:** the minimum temperature approach is an economic choice, not a physical constant.

## Unit 9 · Scenarios and the governing case

| | |
|---|---|
| **In the event of** a blocked outlet, the load is 18 tonnes an hour. | the scenario |
| **Assuming** the inlet continues at design rate. | what it rests on |
| **On the assumption that** cooling is lost completely. | the same, formal |
| **The governing case is** external fire, at 41 tonnes an hour. | the largest |
| Unrelated failures occurring **simultaneously** **are not considered**. | the convention |
| The device **is sized for** the governing case, and for **nothing else**. | the limit of the protection |

> **Careful:** a scenario nobody listed is not relieved. The valve lifts and may be far too small.

| | |

| The pipeline we do not operate is **outside the boundary**. | not counted |

## Unit 11 · Classifying and assigning

| | |
|---|---|
| Crude oil **is classified as** a flammable liquid. | the class |
| It **is assigned to** packing group II. | the group |
| The tanker **bears the number** 1267. | the UN number |

## Unit 12 · Explaining a market figure

| | |
|---|---|
| It is **an indicator of** market conditions. | what it points at |
| It **assumes** a fixed yield. | an assumption, named |

# The British Layer

This book is written in American English, and the switch to British English is a layer rather than a
correction. The patterns are printed in full in Book 1. The ones this book touches most are the
**-er** ending in measuring words, the **-ize** and **-yze** endings, **sulf-**, the **-se** and
**-ce** pairs, the doubling of a single **l** in *labeled* and *traveled*, and **aluminum**, **gray**
and **skeptical**.

**Three things never change, in either layer.**

**Standards, laws and test method numbers.** ASTM D86 is ASTM D86. ISO 8217 is ISO 8217. Directive
98/70/EC is Directive 98/70/EC, and UN 1267 is UN 1267. Where a quoted instrument spells a word
differently from this book's base, the instrument's spelling stands in its own title.

**Names of people, journals, agencies and societies.** Nelson, Houdry, Linnhoff, Hindmarsh. The
journals and the publishing bodies, including several whose own names contain endings this layer
would otherwise change.

**Units and element names in a quoted figure.** A limit quoted in milligrams per kilogram stays in
milligrams per kilogram, and this book gives parts per million beside it rather than instead of it.
Where a specification parameter names an element, this book uses its own base spelling in its own
words and does not restyle a quoted title.

**One deliberate choice, which is not a spelling layer.** Money is written with no currency symbol
and no currency name anywhere in this series — including in the unit that is entirely about money.
Every figure in Unit 12 is a number per barrel, and the currency is whichever one the reader deals
in.

# Sources

Each unit of this book is built on one real published document. Two of them are not technical
documents at all: Unit 3 is a professional society's commemoration and Unit 12 is a government
agency explaining a market indicator to the public. **Read the published documents.** The last one is
the plainest writing in the whole series.

| Unit | The document | What the text prints as the document's own limit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ASTM D86**, *Standard Test Method for Distillation of Petroleum Products and Liquid Fuels at Atmospheric Pressure* | The test is **empirical**: the recorded temperatures are vapor temperatures in that apparatus under that heating rate, not the true boiling points of the molecules, and have to be converted by correlation for process design. The **precision** section gives **repeatability** and the wider **reproducibility**, and both vary along the curve: the fifty per cent point is the most reproducible, the initial and final boiling points the least. A result inside a limit by less than the reproducibility is inside the limit and inside the noise |
| 2 | W. L. Nelson, ***Petroleum Refinery Engineering***, McGraw-Hill, **fourth edition, 1958** | The charts are a **compilation of other people's measurements**, named and credited, good over the range the data covers and no better. Thermodynamics, material balances and hydrocarbon property relationships have not aged; catalysts, specifications, metallurgy, instrumentation and energy costs have aged completely — and **the book does not mark the boundary**, because in 1958 nobody could |
| 3 | The **National Historic Chemical Landmark** designation of the **Houdry process for catalytic cracking**, made by the American Chemical Society in **1996** | A **commemoration**, written to celebrate, and celebration selects. The engineering is not in dispute; the popular account in which one process decided a war is a simplification — several processes in several countries contributed and historians disagree about how decisive any one was. The engineering lesson the plaque does not carry: the process was superseded within about five years, by a better way of handling a solid rather than better chemistry |
| 4 | **Directive 98/70/EC** of the European Parliament and of the Council, **13 October 1998**, on the quality of petrol and diesel fuels, **as amended** | It is a **directive**: it does not take effect directly but must be **transposed** into each member state's national law, so the law that binds a seller is the **national law of the country of sale**. It applies in **one bloc** and **not here**, and it is in the book because a refinery that exports must meet the buyer's specification. It covers **road** fuels; vehicle emission requirements are separate instruments; and every limit in it depends on a **named test method** |
| 5 | **ASTM D2699** (research method) and **ASTM D2700** (motor method) for the knock characteristics of spark-ignition engine fuels | An octane number is **the result of a comparison** on a defined single-cylinder engine from the 1920s against two liquids **defined** as 100 and 0 — not a measurement of a quantity in the fuel. Neither method claims to predict behavior in a particular modern engine with several cylinders, varying load and a knock sensor. And the two conventions for pump labeling mean **95 and 91 can be the same fuel** |
| 6 | **ISO 8217**, *Petroleum products — Fuels (class F) — Specifications of marine fuels* | **Commercial, not statutory**: the legal sulfur limit comes from a separate maritime convention and its implementing rules, so a cargo can meet the specification and be illegal where the ship is going. Meeting it does not guarantee the fuel suits a particular engine; engine builders publish their own requirements. The most damaging items — catalyst fines and total sediment — cannot be assessed at a manifold in the time available, which is why the **jointly witnessed sample** matters more than the certificate |
| 7 | The ***Annual Statistical Bulletin*** of the Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries | A **compilation** from two kinds of input — **direct communication** from member countries and **secondary sources** — and open about which. **Syria is not a member**, so figures for it come from secondary sources. Refining capacity in such a table is a **nameplate** figure, one of at least four capacities, and the gaps between them can be a third of a plant. It is good at comparison across countries and years and is not a description of any plant on any day |
| 8 | B. Linnhoff and E. Hindmarsh, "The Pinch Design Method for Heat Exchanger Networks", *Chemical Engineering Science*, **1983** | The utility targets are **thermodynamic minima for a chosen minimum temperature approach**, and that approach is an **economic choice**, not a physical constant. Four things are invisible to the method: streams nobody listed, **distance** between units, operability, and fouling. And it is a **design** method; retrofitting an existing plant is a different and much harder problem |
| 9 | **API Standard 521**, *Pressure-relieving and Depressuring Systems*, published as an identical international standard | A device is sized for the **largest case on the scenario list**. A scenario nobody listed is not sized for — the valve lifts anyway and may be far too small, so **a vessel can fail with its relief valve fully open**. The **fire case** rests on assumptions about area, drainage and fire-fighting that change when a plot plan changes and are almost never revalidated. Unrelated simultaneous failures are **not normally considered**, which is a convention and a judgment |
| 10 | ***Petroleum industry guidelines for reporting greenhouse gas emissions***, **second edition, 2011**, IPIECA, API and IOGP | **Voluntary industry guidelines**, not a regulation. The **organizational boundary** — operational control or equity share — changes a company's total by a large fraction **without changing a molecule**, which is why the basis must be stated. Almost nothing is weighed: combustion is close to arithmetic, **venting and fugitives are calculated from factors** and carry most of the uncertainty. A fall in a reported total may be entirely a boundary change, which is why restatements exist |
| 11 | The UN ***Recommendations on the Transport of Dangerous Goods — Model Regulations***, with the road agreement **ADR** | **Recommendations**, not law: they take effect through modal instruments and national legislation, and it is the national law a court reads. The **consignor** determines the classification, from **properties**, which rest on **test results** on a sample that has to be representative. And a classification determined correctly from one set of properties **can become wrong when the product changes** — a souring crude may acquire a subsidiary toxic hazard that nobody has declared, with every document in the company a correct copy of the original decision |
| 12 | The **United States Energy Information Administration**'s published glossary and explanatory series, on the **crack spread** | An **indicator** of market conditions, published daily, built on a **fixed assumed yield** and **benchmark** prices, with **no operating cost and no transport in it at all**. The publishing agency says plainly that it is an approximation. A refinery's own margin differs from it on five named lines — crude differential, actual yield, realized prices, operating cost and transport — and a plant can have a poor month in a strong market, which is arithmetic rather than an excuse |

**Book 4's honesty core.** Every unit is about a number whose meaning lives in its definition. A
boiling range is defined by a test method; an octane number by an engine and two reference liquids; a
correlation by whose data and over what range; a fuel limit by a law in a place; a capacity by which
of four capacities; an energy target by an approach temperature; a relief valve by a scenario list; an
emissions total by a boundary; a transport classification by properties on a date; and the crack
spread by an assumed yield. In every case the definition is short, published, and does not travel with
the number. That is the whole argument of the book, and the remedy costs about six words.

# A Note on Everything Else in This Book

**The documents above are real.** Every other named thing in this book was written for it.

**Jazira Petroleum Operations** does not exist, and in this book it does not own a refinery — which is
deliberate, because the position of learning the downstream from outside it is the position almost
everybody in this industry is in. Every person named is invented, and so is every batch number, test
result, certificate, cargo, bunker delivery, capacity figure, energy study, relief scenario, emissions
inventory, transport classification and margin reconciliation attributed to the company. The tanker
*Northern Pearl* is invented. None of it is a record of anything that happened anywhere.

**The real places are named as matters of public record only.** Homs, Banias, Deir ez-Zor, Hasakah and
the Syrian Petroleum Company appear because they are facts about the country. Where this book gives
the opening years and nameplate capacities of the two refineries it gives the figures, the years and
the kind of source, and says that nameplate is what a plant was built to do and that published figures
for any country come from compilations whose provenance differs.

**One real event is referred to**, in Unit 3: a war in which high-octane aviation fuel mattered. The
unit says plainly that several processes in several countries contributed to supplying it and that
historians disagree about how decisive any single one was.

**This is a first-edition teaching draft.** It is not published, endorsed or approved by the Syrian
Ministry of Petroleum and Mineral Resources, by the Syrian Petroleum Company, by any other Syrian
state body, by any standards organization, professional society, statistical agency, regulator or
legislature named in it, or by any company. It carries no official status, and nothing in it is
engineering, legal, safety, commercial, regulatory or financial advice.

**And the standing line.** Safety information in a language course is not a substitute for a competent
person, a procedure and a permit. This book will teach you to say *the governing case was set in 1979
and three units have been built since*. It will not tell you whether the valve is big enough.

# At the End of the Book

Twelve units ago you could follow a barrel from the rock to the refinery gate. You can now follow it
through the plant and out again as products — and, more usefully, you can read any number this
industry produces and ask the one question that matters about it.

You can describe a test procedure and read its precision section; hedge a generalization and tell
interpolation from extrapolation; tell a process history with years in it and read a commemoration for
what it is; read a phased deadline and explain why a foreign law decided what your plant makes; say
why one fuel has two octane numbers and why ninety-five and ninety-one can be the same petrol; read a
specification table and know that its most important clauses have no numbers in them; name four
capacities and refuse to use the word without one; separate a thermodynamic target from a promise;
read a relief device's scenario list and notice its date; state an emissions boundary and split a
change into real and definitional; classify a product and date the classification; and reconcile a
published market figure against your own in five lines that add up.

That is the whole downstream, in a language that keeps the definition attached to the number.

**Flowline 4 — *Risk, Integrity and the Permit*** does not add another piece of plant. It goes back
over everything in the first four books and asks how organizations fail. It opens with a 1931 book
that counted accidents, goes through four public inquiries whose findings changed the industry, and
ends with a 2014 book that argues safety is not the absence of failure but the presence of something
else.

The barrel is finished. The questions are not.
