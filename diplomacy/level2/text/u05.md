### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

Statelessness is the one consular subject where the person in front of you may have no document at
all, and where the absence of documents is the case rather than an obstacle to it. Three habits
help. First, do not treat an empty register as evidence about the person: in large parts of the
world, in some decades, birth registration simply did not happen, and a missing entry says something
about an administration and nothing about a human being. Second, never leave a nationality box empty
on a form that will travel — write *not established* or *not known*, in full, because an empty box
gets filled in later by somebody who was not there. Third, remember that the word *stateless* is a
legal finding, not a description you are free to apply: writing it in a file before a determination
has been made can close doors that your letter was meant to open.

**Discuss:** In your own records, what is the difference between *none* and *not known*? Who later
reads them as the same thing?
