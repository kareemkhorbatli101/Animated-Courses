### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

Record-keeping is one of the places where ministries differ most, and the differences are not about
tidiness. In some systems the file is the official record and a decision does not exist until it is
on the file; in others the file is a copy and the decision lives in a database. Who may take a file
out varies too: in some registries nobody removes an original and you work with copies, in others
the original travels around the building. Signing out can be a formality or a legal act. And the
idea of a *closed* file differs: in some ministries a file is closed after a fixed number of years
and goes to a national archive by law, which means the person who writes a note today is writing
for a historian in thirty years, whether they think about it or not.

**Discuss:** In your office, where does the official record live? Who may take an original out?

> Registry now has a printed weekly return check, which anybody can do, and which exists because for
