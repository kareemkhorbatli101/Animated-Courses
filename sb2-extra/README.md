# Al-Hasan International — Extra Material for Student Book 2

**CEFR B1 · 120 lessons · ten units · runs from a folder, offline**

Extra reading and listening for every unit of *Al-Hasan International
Student Book 2*. Ten units, twelve lessons each: **six readings and six
listenings**, every one of them read aloud by the speech voices already
installed on the computer the browser is running on. No audio files, no
build step, no network.

Unzip and open `index.html`.

Companion to the Student Book 1 pack (`book1-extra/`, CEFR A2). The two
are independent and keep separate scores.

---

## What a lesson is

| Step | | Exercises |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before you read / listen | 3 |
| 2 | The text or the conversation — audio + glossary | — |
| 3 | Comprehension | 6 |
| 4 | Words in context | 3 |
| 5 | After you read / listen | 3 |

**15 exercises a lesson, 1,800 in the pack.** Twelve mark themselves
(multiple choice, several-answer, true/false, gap-fill, matching,
ordering); three are written — one short answer and two longer — and show
a model answer the learner compares with their own and marks.

Scores are kept per lesson and totalled per unit and on the front page,
in the learner's own browser.

## How B1 differs from the A2 pack

Same shell, same five steps, same eight exercise types. What changes is
inside:

| | A2 pack | B1 pack |
|---|---|---|
| Reading length | 300–380 words | 420–520 words |
| Dialogue | 14–17 turns | 16–20 turns, with hedging and repair |
| Sentences | simple and compound | subordination: *although*, *unless*, *which* |
| Comprehension | mostly retrieval | two of six need inference |
| Gap-fills | vocabulary | **the unit's grammar form** |
| Written tasks | 3–5 sentences | a paragraph or a document |
| Glossary | word → definition | word → definition **+ its collocation** |

The lessons follow the Student Book's own year and keep inside its
figures: steel at $640 CFR Lattakia, 1,200 tonnes at $608 after the five
per cent, $729,600, a landed cost of $712, 48 containers of which one
held eleven coils, 40 tonnes at 4.8 mm, 6,400 tonnes of steel against
eleven of cement, and Ms. Maya's 406 people. The cast is the Student
Book's own — Mr. Adnan, Mr. Tarek, Ms. Rania, Karim, Lina, Huda, Fadi,
Rami, Mr. Samir, Ms. Dana, Ms. Maya, Eng. Bilal, Mr. Haitham, Ms. Chen
and Mr. Delgado — plus **Yara**, a new import assistant at Bashak, who
is the only addition and who is not at head office.

## Files

```
index.html             the shell; regenerated by build.py
assets/site.css        one stylesheet
assets/app.js          router, eight exercise types, speech, scoring
assets/data/u1r1.js …  one file per lesson, 120 of them
build.py               rewrites index.html from whatever is in data/
check.py               gates every lesson file
README.txt             the same notes, for learners
```

`check.py` enforces: 15 exercises per lesson, the required fields,
`paras:` on readings and `turns:` + `setting:` on listenings, a model
answer on every written task, well-formed multiple-choice / gap / match /
order data, no duplicate ids, and no stray non-Latin characters.

### Adding a lesson

```sh
python3 check.py     # must print 0 problems
python3 build.py     # rewrites index.html
```
