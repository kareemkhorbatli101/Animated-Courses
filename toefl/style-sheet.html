The visual standard

One page that renders every template, every cast member and the chart style at once, so “does this look like one book, and is it good enough?” can be answered by looking rather than by argument.

Everything here is authored vector and CSS. No photographs, no downloaded assets, no fonts from the network, no licence exposure — and it is all that loads from file:// with no server. These are Unit 1’s actual assets, so this page is also the Unit 1 asset proof.

Two families, held apart by job: serif is the world, sans is the test. A learner never has to wonder which they are reading.

2 · The cast

The 2026 spec requires “a visual representation of the speaker(s)” on every listening item. A fixed recurring cast — same face, same name, same voice, same accent across all twelve units — rather than stock photography: it gives the book an identity, costs nothing to license, carries no model-release exposure, and makes the audio coherent. Professor Haddad sounds the same in Unit 2 and Unit 11.

6 · What “equivalent in quality” has to mean

Acceptance criteria, so this is checkable rather than a matter of taste. The gate fails the build on any of them.

  • One book, not twelve. Every unit draws from this page. A template that appears in Unit 7 and not here is a defect in Unit 7.
  • Documents are documents. Real typography, real alignment, real rules and small print. No document is a grey box with text in it.
  • Nothing rasterised that could be vector. Crisp at any zoom, a few KB, themeable in both modes.
  • Every listening item shows its speaker, and the same character always has the same face, name, voice and accent.
  • Every Lesson 5 carries a data visual, in this one chart style, with at least one item that cannot be answered without it.
  • Both modes are selected and validated. Dark mode is re-stepped, never an automatic flip, and the palette validator passes in both.
  • Licence-clean and offline. Nothing from the network at runtime; every Tier-D photograph recorded in ASSETS.csv with source, accession number and licence.
  • No clutter. One viewport, one interactive object, and a hard ceiling on interface words per scene — the rule that fixed the CMA package.
