# Parity check: the pharmacy pair against its dental counterparts

Scope: the four pharmacy units written so far (Pharmacists u01–u02,
Pharmacy Students u01–u02) measured against their direct dental
counterparts (In the Chair u01–u02, The Mouth and the Clinic u01–u02),
on the engine's own instruments plus a close qualitative read.
Method: every unit was run through the same measurement script —
the gates' own counters for task demand, variety, register and
scaffolding — so the numbers below are computed, not estimated.

## Verdict first

**Parity holds. No quality drift.** On every gated dimension the
pharmacy units sit inside the dental range, and on two (cognitive
demand in unit 2, recycling) they measure slightly above it. Three
minor gaps were found; two were fixed during this check, and one is
lineage, not drift. Details below.

## The qualitative dimensions

**Teachability.** The teacher's books carry the same load-bearing
apparatus: a 90-minute core route with the stage-not-to-cut named, a
per-part procedure with timings, board plans, five engagement moves,
and difficulty notes written against the specific unit (the fog-word
handling in PHA-S t02, the guessed-time correction in PHA-P t02) —
not recycled dental advice. A first-time teacher gets the same grip.

**Domain relevance.** The strongest evidence against noun-swapping:
the pharmacy units are organised around artefacts that have no dental
counterpart — the basket note, the waiting-bag label, the fog-word
case, the pack read in order, the cold chain, the honest-nothing
promise. Where the architecture mirrors dental on purpose (the
two-identifier check, the calm column), the content is re-derived
from the counter, and the mirroring is the series design: the same
safety logic taught in each profession's own things.

**Sequencing.** Both pairs run the same arc: u01 establishes place,
people and identification; u02 teaches the first core procedure
(looking in the mouth / taking in the paper; the dentition /
the dose forms). recycles/prepares chains are declared and real:
all 8 of Pharmacists u01's core terms reappear in u02 (dental: 7 of
8); 7 of 8 for the student book (dental: 5 of 8). Recycling is at
or above dental parity.

**Publishability.** Scanned mechanically: zero numeric doses,
strengths or quantities anywhere in the four pharmacy units
(regex over mg/ml/mcg/units patterns), zero named medicines
(checked against common drug stems), and every unit that touches
rules says local law and the pharmacy's own written procedures
govern them. The pharmacy pair additionally inherits the
"sentence as a safety device" spine; the strength/quantity words
name lines on the paper and never carry values. This is the one
dimension where the pharmacy books are *stricter* than the dental
ones by design.

**Scaffolding.** Every closed task carries a worked example (gate-
enforced in both series); every speaking task a model and checklist;
every writing task model + scaffold + checklist at dental counts;
pre/while/post structure on every listening and reading; answer
keys and transcripts in the teacher's book.

**Register fidelity.** The P/S differentiation survives the fork:
practitioner prose at 10.6–11.8 mean words per sentence against the
student book's 14.7–15.4 — the same two-register split as dental
(10.0/10.2 vs 13.7/14.9), both inside their bands. The student book
speaks in the passive and the language of definition; the
practitioner book in counter turns capped at 12 words.

**Cognitive demand.** Identical remember-level shares; the pharmacy
unit 2s actually carry more above-apply tasks (7–8 vs dental's 4–5),
mostly from the sort/true-false/correction shapes.

**Visual support and icon honesty.** The same per-chapter survey
discipline is being applied, and it already caught and fixed the
same defect class dental went through: whole picture strips falling
back to the speech bubble. The fix (a term that is an icon name
draws itself; signature, calendar, bottle, door remaps) is engine-
level, so it hardens both series.

**Assessment support.** Sample answers for every open task (17 per
unit after this check, including the 6.4 fluency model dental
carries), Arabic on the warm-up and case answers, five marking
criteria per unit with the same "mark articles once at the end"
stance.

**Case quality.** Three arguable cases per unit, each with facts,
three non-yes/no questions, a language box, poles and marks, and
teacher notes with the push questions and an Arabic line — at
dental structure and, in the reviewer's judgement, at dental bite
(the taxi-meter case and the photograph-of-the-label case give the
discussion real stakes).

## Gaps found

1. **Own-practice hooks** — Pharmacists u02 sat at the gate floor
   (2) where dental runs 3–4. *Fixed:* the basket-note writing task
   now asks for the waiting line as the learner's own pharmacy says
   it. Both books now measure 3–4.
2. **Missing 6.4 fluency sample answers** — dental teacher books
   model the fluency task's third run; the pharmacy ones didn't.
   *Fixed:* added to all four pharmacy t-files, with the Arabic line.
3. **Defects sections thinner (4 vs 10–11)** — not drift. The dental
   defects lists accumulated across four evaluation rounds the
   pharmacy books haven't lived through; the pharmacy entries are
   first-generation self-critique of the same kind. This gap closes
   naturally as the pharmacy pair goes through its own rounds; it
   should not be padded artificially.

All gates re-run after the fixes: units 1–2 of both pharmacy books,
zero failures, zero warnings; teacher audits clean.
