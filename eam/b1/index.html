English for Auto Maintenance

English for the service department — the parts of a car, the people who work on it, the paper that records the job, and the talk that holds it together. Every part of the book is a screen you work through, and every screen has an acted 3D lesson that teaches the same thing with the same five people.

الإنجليزية لقسم صيانة السيارات: أجزاء السيارة، والعاملون عليها، والأوراق التي تسجّل العمل، والحوار الذي ينظّمه. كل جزء من الكتاب شاشة تعمل عليها، ولكل شاشة درس ممثَّل ثلاثي الأبعاد.

The five people you will meet

• Maher — the manager, in the office
• Nabil — the mechanic, in the workshop
• Rana — the service advisor, at reception
• Lina — the diagnostic technician
• Tarek — the apprentice, learning

Units

1 Introduction to the Vehicle and the Service Department 21 sections · present simple and present continuous · 10 words live 2 Systems and Fluids there is / there are · some, any, much, many in preparation 3 Tools and Safety imperatives · the order of the steps in preparation 4 Measuring and Diagnosis comparatives and superlatives in preparation 5 The Service Process the passive, for describing a process in preparation

Units 6 to 10 of the book exist and are not yet built as pages.

The acted lessons

Each of Unit 1’s twenty-one sections has a 3D lesson acted by the same five people, in the same workshop. They are a different way through the same material — watch first and then work the screen, or the other way round.
