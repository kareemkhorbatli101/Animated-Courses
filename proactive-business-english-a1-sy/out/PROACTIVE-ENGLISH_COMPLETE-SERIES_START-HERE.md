
**Every book folder has the same six files**, so you can find anything without looking:

```
<LEVEL>_Book-<n>_Units-<range>/
   01_Students-Book_BOUND.docx          the book, in teaching order, with a title page
   02_Answer-Key_COMBINED.docx          every unit key + the test keys
   03_Mid-Book-Test.docx                after the book's sixth unit
   04_Mid-Book-Test_Answer-Key.docx
   05_End-of-Book-Test.docx             after the book's last unit
   06_End-of-Book-Test_Answer-Key.docx
   units/                               the same units again, one .docx each,
                                        for printing or teaching a single unit
```

⚠ **The two A1 folders have four files, not six, and that is correct.** A1 is the only
level with **six-unit books**, and at six units the test at the end of the book *is* the
mid-point of the course — so A1 has no mid-book test. Book 1 ends with the Book 1 Test
(60 marks) and Book 2 with the End-of-Course Test (100 marks).

## 3 · The one book that is NOT here, and why

⚠ **Proactive English (general) A2 Book 1, Units 1–12, is not in this package.**

It is not missing by accident and it is not lost. That book was written **before this
repository existed** and came into it only as handover files — the tree for the general
A2 level is titled, in its own README, *"Units 13–24 of Proactive English (A2),
continuing the Syria Edition from Book 1."* Every other book in both strands was built
here and is in this package.

**So the count is 15 books of a 16-book series.** If you have general A2 Book 1 in your
own files, it drops straight into `01_Proactive-English_General/` as
`A2_Book-1_Units-01-12/` and the series is complete.

## 4 · The spine every book shares

**Thirteen stages, ninety minutes, seven cycles a unit.**

Lesson A (speaking, 45 min) — 1 Get ready · 2 Listen and read (+ Language box) ·
3 Practise · 3R Perform · 4 Your turn · 4R Report.
Lesson B (writing, 45 min) — 5 Read (+ Writer's box) · 6 Write with help ·
6P Check together · 6R Share · 7 Write freely · 7P Respond · 7R Share · Homework.

**Cycle 7 of every unit is Review & Mediate** — and the mediation is the series' signature:
a disagreement in which **both people are right**, which the learner has to resolve by
naming what each one is right about. Across the whole series there is almost no cycle in
which somebody is simply at fault.

**Components, every level:** a Student's Book with the audio scripts printed at the point
of use (no appendix to flip to) and the Answer Key as a separate book. No teacher's notes
— the key carries them.

**The Answer Key rule, every level:** *every* task gets an answer or a full sample answer.
Brainstorms, personalised speaking, reports, peer-review checklists, feedback slips, free
writing and homework included. There is no task in 11,797 pages that a teacher has to
improvise.

**The tests follow the examination each level is anchored to**, not a house template:

| Strand | Level | Anchored to | Paper split |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Business | A1 | LanguageCert A1 (A2 Key, one step down) | 30 · 30 · 20 · 20 |
| Business | A2 | A2 Key | 30 · 30 · 20 · 20 |
| Business | B1 | B1 Business Preliminary | 25 · 25 · 25 · 25, reported as an **average** |
| Business | B2 | B2 Business Vantage | 25 · 25 · 25 · 25 |
| General | A2 | A2 Key | 30 · 30 · 20 · 20 |
| General | B1 | B1 Preliminary | 30 · 30 · 20 · 20 |
| General | B2 | B2 First | **40 · 25 · 25 · 10** |
| General | C1 | C1 Advanced | **40 · 20 · 20 · 20** |

⚠ **The two strands diverge at B2 and the difference is not cosmetic.** General B2 follows
B2 First, whose Reading and Use of English paper carries 40 per cent and whose Speaking
carries 10; Business B2 follows Business Vantage and weights the four skills equally. A
teacher moving a class between strands at B2 is changing what the test rewards, and
should say so to the class.

⚠ **Business B1 is the one to read twice.** Its examination reports the overall result as
the *average of four skill scores*, so both its keys tell the teacher to write the four
scores and the average and **never the total** — because a learner shown only a total will
optimise the total, which is the habit a four-skill average exists to break.

## 5 · If you are teaching from this tomorrow

1. **Read `03_Plans-and-Handovers/` for your level first.** Each handover says what each
   unit is *for*, which is not always what its title says, and lists the things that will
   catch a teacher out.
2. **Teach from `01_Students-Book_BOUND.docx`** and print from `units/` when you want one
   unit on paper.
3. **The teacher's note at the end of each unit key is the most useful page in the
   series.** It is long on purpose, and it is the only place the mediation in that unit's
   cycle 7 is explained. Read it before the lesson, not after.
4. **Play every recording three times.** The first is noise, the second is words, the
   third is meaning. Classes that cut it to two lose marks the learners have the English
   for.
5. **Never accept a bare number.** *12* is not a quantity; *12 boxes* is. It starts in
   Business A1 Unit 2 and is still being marked in the C1 tests.

## 6 · Rebuilding any of it

Each level's pipeline is in `04_Source-Pipelines/` as a zip: one script per unit, one per
key, the shared renderer (`lib.js`, frozen and byte-identical across every level), the
bound-volume builder, and the QA harness.

```
cd pipeline && node qa.js && ./build.sh bound
```

⚠ **`node qa.js` must print `0 fault(s)` before a build is kept**, and it must be run over
the **whole tree** rather than the units you touched — a lexis collision is *between* two
units and a single-unit run cannot see one.

The harness checks structure, not taste: house figures per unit and per key, the rubric
words, cast and surname consistency, speaker alternation, declared lexis with no repeat
anywhere in a level, the ordinal each mediation cycle claims about itself, and — for the
tests — four papers, the mark split, contiguous question numbering, and that every
numbered question has an answer in the key.

**Every level in this package builds clean.**
