# Grammar reference

Every structure in this book, in unit order. The second column is the mistake it causes, which is
the column worth reading twice.

## Starting Point · four habits for leading on imperfect evidence

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name what the instrument measures** | if you cannot, you do not yet know what you are claiming |
| **Say what it cannot distinguish** | *on these data* is the clause everybody leaves out |
| **Decide anyway, and say what you are not resting on** | a refusal with no decision in it is not advice |
| **Record when the instrument is next republished** | some of them restate history when they arrive |

## Unit 1 · Extraposition and *it*-structures

| | |
|---|---|
| ***it is for X to*** allocates · ***it is open to X to*** permits · ***it is not for X to*** excludes | three different answers in a dispute two years later |
| **Extrapose a heavy subject** | *That no jurisdiction requires it is surprising* |
| ***it should not be inferred that*** | the readings you refuse in advance, in the document |
| **The agentless passive imposing a duty** | *a decision will be taken* — by whom? |

## Unit 2 · Attribution and evidentiality

| | |
|---|---|
| ***X reports that*** · ***according to*** · ***on X's own account*** | borrow the authority, carry the attribution |
| ***reportedly*** / ***it is claimed that*** carry audible doubt | dishonest when you have none and only want distance |
| **Always give the base** | *78% of employees*, not *78% of people* |
| **Non-restrictive commas claim it of all members** | *Employees, who trust their employer, stay longer* |

## Unit 3 · Definition and stipulation

| | |
|---|---|
| ***for the purposes of this paper, X means*** | a definition binds the document, and that is its value |
| **Write the negative half** | *means A and does not include B* — the half writers omit |
| ***shall be construed as*** · ***save that*** · ***is net of*** | contractual register, used precisely |
| **A measure is not the thing measured** | gloss it between dashes, in six words |

## Unit 4 · Verbs of allegation; the passive of process

| | |
|---|---|
| ***it is alleged that*** vs ***it is common ground that*** | the second says how much of your paper is safe |
| ***contend*** · ***maintain*** · ***submit*** · ***plead*** | not degrees of politeness; different commitments |
| **The passive is right for a procedural step** | *the tribunal was constituted* — nobody failed |
| **Writing your case as fact** | the sentence you will be asked about in disclosure |

## Unit 5 · The mandative subjunctive

| | |
|---|---|
| ***recommend / propose / require that*** + bare verb | *I recommend that the threshold remains* |
| **Negative: *that X not be*** | *that it be not changed* |
| ***be***, not ***should be*** | *should* is advice; the bare form is the recommendation |
| **Compliance is not effect** | *we have no evidence either way* is a complete sentence |

## Unit 6 · Quantified uncertainty

| | |
|---|---|
| ***cannot be distinguished on these data*** | without the last three words it is a claim about the world |
| ***the intervals overlap by N*** | give the overlap in units, not as an impression |
| ***among the highest*** · ***no higher than*** | different and more defensible claims, not weaker ones |
| ***I am not saying … I am saying …*** | nine words that pre-empt the misreading |

## Unit 7 · Purpose and fitness clauses

| | |
|---|---|
| ***is designed to*** / ***is not designed to*** | the publisher's negative is the most reliable sentence |
| ***with a view to*** + *-ing* · ***for the purpose of*** + noun | *with a view to price* |
| ***so as not to***, not ***so as to not*** | and never *for to* |
| **Replace a proxy with a measure *and its spread*** | a median alone is a new proxy in better clothes |

## Unit 8 · Commitment and *have yet to*

| | |
|---|---|
| ***have yet to*** + bare verb | *have yet submitted* · *have yet to submitting* |
| **Present perfect for the record, *as at* + past perfect for a position** | mixed, they produce a timeline nobody can follow |
| **Commitment language has no object in the world** | *we are committed to* describes a state of mind |
| ***on track*** without a date and a measure | means the dates will be met; say so or do not say it |

## Unit 9 · Attribution of a divergence

| | |
|---|---|
| ***is attributable to*** + noun or *-ing* | *attributable to reroute* |
| ***accounts for N of*** | the quantified form, and the checkable one |
| ***reflects*** claims a sign, not a cause | careful publishers use it; borrowers misuse it |
| **A composition effect is not a cause** | nothing got dearer; the weight moved |

## Unit 10 · The modality of soft law

| | |
|---|---|
| ***shall*** duty · ***should*** norm · ***is expected to*** norm with a holder | chosen deliberately in every instrument |
| ***has no power to*** | an express limit, and only half the position |
| ***not obliged to*** ≠ ***not expected to*** | the company that hears only the first is surprised later |
| ***will*** where you meant ***shall*** | gives away a contractual right in one word |

## Unit 11 · Proportion, ratio and divergence

| | |
|---|---|
| ***for every X, Y*** | forces you to state both quantities |
| ***twice as fast as*** (rates) vs ***twice as much as*** (quantities) | over 25 years these are very different claims |
| **Present perfect with *since*** | *a gap opened since 1999* |
| **A percentage point is not a percent** | 4 to 5 is one point and 25% more money |

## Unit 12 · Scenario conditionals

| | |
|---|---|
| ***were X to hold, Y would …*** | *will* converts a consequence into a prediction |
| ***under a scenario in which*** · ***on current policies*** | consequence, not likelihood |
| ***is projected to*** + an interval | the probabilistic half, with its range attached |
| ***has been revised between vintages***, not ***was wrong*** | nothing has happened yet |

# Sources

Twelve units, twelve different source bodies. Every figure quoted in a reading text in this book is
real, published, dated and named in the text where it is used, and no figure is reused from any
earlier book in this series.

The organizing idea of this book is **the gap between an instrument and what it can actually do**,
and in every unit that gap is stated by the instrument's own publisher.

## Nine honesty notes, printed here because they are printed in the units too

- **Unit 3 is a measure that is not the thing it is named after.** The OECD's effective tax rates are
  forward-looking policy measures computed from the tax code, not a record of tax paid, and the
  publisher says so in its own reader's guide. The unit prints that sentence, defines both measures
  on page one, and makes the learner write the clause that blocks the subtraction a two-column table
  invites.
- **Unit 5 is the cleanest case in the series of compliance without effect.** Technical compliance
  improved by about twelve percentage points over a decade while measured effectiveness stayed low,
  and both figures come from the same assessment system. The unit also prints the publisher's
  admission that the standard-setter does not reassess effectiveness after a jurisdiction leaves the
  gray list, so the index **assumes** graduates reached at least a moderate level: an assumption
  inside a score, declared by the people who built it, affecting exactly the cases where the score is
  relied on hardest.
- **Unit 6 is a ranking whose own publisher tells you not to rank with it.** A ninety percent
  interval is the estimate plus or minus 1.64 standard errors, and the World Bank's guidance is to
  consider those margins before comparing. Two countries four points apart on intervals that overlap
  by twenty-two cannot be distinguished on those data, and the unit's task is to say so and still
  reach a decision.
- **Unit 7 is an index its own factsheet says is not a diagnostic tool.** It is a perception survey
  with weights derived by principal components analysis, the 2023 edition stopped asking respondents
  about their own country, and the published economy counts differ between the index and the data
  portal. The unit keeps the index for the band comparison it was built for and replaces the promise
  with the company's own corridor data, median and tail.
- **Unit 8 is a count of promises and a count of deliveries.** 10,051 validated targets; 12,647
  targets or commitments; 239 commitments removed in one year; 17 of those 239 validated in the
  following eighteen months. All four figures are the body's own. The unit teaches *have yet to*,
  which is the only honest tense for a promise that has been neither kept nor broken.
- **Unit 4 prints two published counts of one year's new cases**, 60 and 57, and explains that the
  smaller omits the Additional Facility filings. It also keeps the company's own case in the pleading
  register throughout, because a board paper that states a legal conclusion as a fact is a document
  the other side would be pleased to obtain.
- **Unit 9 is one quantity measured two ways, diverging for a physical reason.** Volume grew 2.2 
  percent and tonne-miles 5.9, because ships went round the Cape instead of through the Suez Canal.
  Nothing about demand explains it. The unit also separates a composition effect from a cause, which
  is the distinction that decides whether a company renegotiates rates or re-times a service.
- **Unit 10 is a dispute mechanism with no power to compel anybody.** Agreement was reached in 24 
  percent of all concluded cases and 57 percent of mediated ones, against 73 and 36 in the two previous
  years on samples of a few dozen; the unit refuses to average them. The commonest reason for failure
  is that one side declined to take part, which makes refusal both permitted and the largest single
  cause of failure.
- **Unit 12 separates a probabilistic projection from a scenario and keeps two institutions apart.**
  The population projection carries a published interval 2.4 billion wide and has moved six percent
  between vintages. The freight outlook is conditional: "nearly double by 2050" in one edition, "a
  factor of 2.6" in an earlier one, and eleven percent lower under a high-ambition case. A figure
  that policy can move by eleven percent is, by construction, not a forecast, and the unit refuses
  to put the two instruments in one table.

# Closing

Six books. Seventy-two units. Seventy-two source bodies, and not one figure reused.

This last book has been about instruments: a board charter, a trust survey, a tax measure, a treaty
claim, a compliance score, a governance indicator, a logistics index, a target register, a maritime
statistic, a voluntary guideline, a wage series and a population projection. Every one of them is
the best available. Every one of them publishes its own limits. And in every unit the professional
act was the same: use the instrument for what it can do, say in the same breath what it cannot, and
decide anyway.

If one habit survives the whole series, let it be the one this book began with and ended with:
**when a publisher qualifies a figure, the qualification is part of the figure.** It travels with it
into your slide, your paper, your minute, your proposal and your recommendation — or somebody else
carries it for you, and chooses when to put it down.

The people in these books are invented. Ostberg is invented. Every figure in every reading text is
real, published, dated, attributed, and printed together with whatever its publisher said about it.

Thank you for reading to the end of the series.
