**1C  Study the chart.**

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| **-er than** | Vandermeer is cheap**er than** Ostergaard. |
| **more … than** | Ostergaard is **more reliable than** Vandermeer. |
| **the -est** | Kjeldsen has **the shortest** lead time. |

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

What counts as a good reason to choose a supplier varies. In some business cultures the lowest
compliant price is the only defensible answer, and choosing anything else needs written
justification. In others a long relationship outweighs a price difference, and switching supplier
over four cents would be thought reckless. Some companies require three quotes by rule; some would
find asking a long-standing supplier to re-quote against strangers insulting. And in some places
the person who signs the order is expected to visit the factory first — a comparison table is not
considered evidence at all.

**Discuss:** How are suppliers chosen where you work? Who decides, and what counts as proof?

> ### Recommendation — carton stock, Q3
>
> **Recommended: Ostergaard, 4,000 cartons at 1.38.**
>
> | Criterion | Ostergaard | Vandermeer |
> |---|---|---|
> | Unit price | 1.38 | 1.26 |
> | Order value at our quantity | 5,520 | 12,600 (10,000 minimum) |
> | Lead time | 3 weeks | 6 weeks |
> | References | 2 | 0 |
>
> Vandermeer is genuinely cheaper per carton — 12 cents, or about nine percent — and I would
> expect us to use them eventually. The deciding point is the minimum order. At 10,000 we would
> pay 12,600 for stock worth 5,520 to us this quarter and store about fifteen pallets for roughly
> eleven weeks, at 40 a pallet a month. The saving is 480; the storage is in the region of 1,800.
>
> One further observation, which I would not call a test: we stacked a Vandermeer sample six high
> with water bottles in the warm part of the warehouse and it folded at five. The board is 0.3 mm
> thinner than the others and is within specification, so this may say more about our stacking than
> about their carton. I mention it because I saw it, not because it is evidence.
>
> **Proposal.** Order 4,000 from Ostergaard now. Ask Vandermeer for two references and a 4,000
> trial price for Q1.

> **Writer's Tip** — Concede the strongest point against you, in figures, and early. A reader who
> can see you have understood the other side stops looking for what you hid.
