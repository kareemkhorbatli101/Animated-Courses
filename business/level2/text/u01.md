### 1 · Comparatives

**1A  Listen and read. Then answer the opinion question.** %%AUD CL2-01-06%%

> **Chiamaka:** The slot is shallower than ours.

**1B  Underline the comparative. What is it comparing with, and is that stated?**

**1C  Study the chart.**

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| **-er than** | It is **longer than** the drawing. |
| **more … than** | Grade 316 is **more expensive than** 304. |
| **as … as** | This batch is **as good as** the last one. |
| **not as … as** | It is **not as deep as** the sample. |
| **a little / much / far** | It is **much** heavier. · It is **a little** thicker. |

> **Grammar Note** — A comparative needs a second thing. *It is shallower* is not finished until
> you say *shallower than what?* In a specification, the missing half of a comparison is where
> the argument starts.

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

What a specification is *for* differs. In some engineering cultures the drawing is the whole
agreement and anything not on it is genuinely free; in others the drawing is a summary and the
relationship fills in the rest, so asking for every detail in writing can look like distrust. In
some workplaces a supplier who spots a gap and asks is admired; in others, asking makes you look
as though you cannot work it out, and people guess instead. And a sample kept in a drawer is a
record in some companies and a souvenir in others.

**Discuss:** Where you work, if a drawing is incomplete, does the supplier ask or guess? What
happens to each of them?
