# Clearline — Business English, International Edition

A six-book business English course for people who already work, or are about to. Built on the same
lesson architecture as the Wayfinder series (an architecture is a method, not protected
expression — 17 U.S.C. §102(b), *Baker v. Selden*); every word, character, company, document,
figure and task in these six books is original to them.

---

## 1. Who it is for, and what makes it *international*

Not "business English for Americans" and not "business English for Britons". The learner is a
Polish quality engineer emailing a Korean supplier, a Nigerian procurement officer negotiating
with a German plant, a Brazilian account manager running a call in which nobody present is a
native speaker. English is the **shared second language**, which changes what has to be taught:

| A domestic business course teaches | Clearline teaches |
|---|---|
| idiom and native fluency | **intelligibility between non-native speakers** |
| "sound natural" | "be understood by somebody whose English is unlike yours" |
| one business culture's norms | **where norms differ, and how to find out which one is in the room** |
| the language of one legal system | the language of contracts, Incoterms and compliance in general |
| small talk as rapport | small talk as a **negotiable** custom with different rules per market |

Two strands carry this and run in every unit of all six books:

- **Culture Check** — intercultural pragmatics. Directness, silence, hierarchy, the second refusal,
  what "we'll see" means in six places, who may disagree with whom in public.
- **Clear English** — the pronunciation and phrasing strand, aimed at *mutual* intelligibility:
  stress-timing, chunking, numbers, spelling aloud, slowing down, and the repair moves
  (*say that again*, *let me read that back*) that keep a call alive when two accents collide.

## 3. The unit, lesson by lesson

Identical slots to Wayfinder, re-aimed at work. The converter, the styles and the gates are the
same, so the website build parses all twelve books with one parser.

| Lesson | What it does | Oral slots |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Vocabulary** | 16 core items + a second set; at Book 4+ a nine-item **business register** set. Ends with a team research task (**AUG-a**, digital & AI literacy at work). | 1E model exchange · 2B say-and-check · 2D *Think about it* · the team task |
| **2 · Business Reading** | An authentic document type — a works order, an RFQ, a packing list, a KPI dashboard, a board paper — with True/False/Not Given from Book 3, word study, and a chart with real published figures. Ends with **Take It Outside**: find the same document in your own working life. | prediction poll · *Talk it over* in threes · the harvest |
| **3 · Grammar** | Two structures, taught as choices with consequences. Opens with **Remember?** (AUG-b) and closes with a **team error hunt**. A *Language connection* box from Book 3. | 1D ask-and-answer · 3B picture-cued pairs · 4A personalize · the error hunt |
| **4 · Workplace Conversation** | A document to read aloud, **Clear English** (AUG-c), a focused listening, an **On the Job** exchange set, the model dialogue, a pair task, **Culture Check**, a group ranking, and a role-play with cards. | seven slots; this is the spine lesson |
| **5 · Writing** | A model text of the unit's genre, a planner, then the learner's own, with an editing checklist and peer feedback. | 1A read-and-answer aloud · the planner role-play · peer feedback |
| **Team Up & Review** | Picture task, team summary, report, a four-role **team project**, a class survey with a chart, **Think It Through** (a case with no clean answer), **My Progress** (AUG-d). | all of it |

**The speaking spine is mechanical, not aspirational.** Gate 13 fails the build if any lesson
carries fewer than three oral slots or no open-ended question, or if a lesson opens silently.

## 4. Non-negotiables

1. **Every figure quoted in a reading text is real, published, dated and named in the text.**
   Where two sources disagree, both are printed and the disagreement becomes the task.
2. **No real company, product or brand appears anywhere.** Every company, person, document and
   transaction is invented. Logos, trade names and slogans are never imitated.
3. **No single legal system is assumed.** Where law differs (notice periods, consumer rights,
   working time), the book says *this differs by country; find yours* and makes that the task.
4. **American English base, switchable.** `_series/VARIANTS.json` governs; gate 14 fails the build
   on a British token in the base. Business terms that differ (*invoice/bill*, *CV/résumé*,
   *turnover/revenue*) are taught as a pair, in a box, deliberately.
5. **Numbers are taught as a skill.** Saying them, hearing them, writing them, and the three
   conventions that collide internationally: the decimal comma, the date order, and the billion.

## 5. The world

**Ostberg** — an invented mid-sized port city, unplaced, with a container terminal, an industrial
estate and a university. Nobody says which country it is in and nothing in the books depends on it.

| Company | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Lindmark Freight** | freight forwarding, customs, a warehouse on the estate |
| **Arvo Components** | industrial fasteners; sells to six countries, buys from four |
| **Brightwell Pharma** | regulated manufacture; the compliance and audit setting |
| **Corvid Analytics** | a twenty-person data consultancy; the client-work setting |
| **Juno Textiles** | a supplier abroad; the counterparty in every sourcing unit |
| **Sable & Finch** | specialty food importer; small, cash-tight, five staff |
| **Harbour Mutual** | the bank; invoices, terms, currency, a declined payment |
| **Terrace Hotels** | hospitality; travel, hosting, and the unit on being a guest |

## 6. Production

Shared with Wayfinder and unchanged: the parametric art system (`wayfinder/_build/art_*.py`,
340+ icons, a cast of vector figures, scenes, charts and realia), the markdown → Word converter
(`build_docx.py`), the gate runner (`wayfinder/_series/bookcheck.py`) and the contents paginator
(`wayfinder/_series/paginate.py`). This series adds its own figure specs, build drivers, icons
and cast members.

Per unit: 16 figures, 16 audio cues, in the same fixed positions as Wayfinder, so one parser reads
twelve books.

## 7. The gates

The fourteen Wayfinder gates apply unchanged, plus two this series adds:

- **B1 · No brand, no real company.** Checked against the forbidden list.
- **B2 · No single-jurisdiction claim.** A reading text that states a legal rule must name the
  jurisdiction or say that it differs.
