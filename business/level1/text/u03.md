### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

The working week is not the same everywhere. In many countries it runs Monday to Friday; in
others the weekend is Friday and Saturday, or Thursday and Friday, so "see you Monday" lands
oddly. Lunch may be twenty minutes at a desk or two hours away from it. In some workplaces a
message at nine at night is normal and nobody expects a reply; in others it is a message that
somebody feels they must answer.

**Discuss:** What are the normal working days where you are? When is it too late to send a work
message?
