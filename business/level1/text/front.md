# Clearline Foundation
%%SUB Business English · International Edition · CEFR A2%%

## How to use this book

This book is for people who work, or who are about to. It does not teach you to sound like a
British or an American person. It teaches you to be **understood** — by a colleague in Manila, a
supplier in Gdańsk, a customer in Lima, none of whom learned English at home either.

**Twelve units.** Each one is fourteen pages and has the same six lessons, always in the same
order. Once you have done Unit 1, you know where everything is.

| Lesson | What it does |
|---|---|
| **1 · Vocabulary** | Sixteen words you will use this week, then six more, then a team task |
| **2 · Business Reading** | A real document — a staff list, an order, a delivery note — and one real number from a real report |
| **3 · Grammar** | Two structures, with the mistake each one causes |
| **4 · Workplace Conversation** | Saying it clearly, on the phone and in the room |
| **5 · Writing** | A short model, then yours |
| **Team Up & Review** | A picture, a team project, a survey, and one problem with no easy answer |

**Six things come back in every unit.**

- **Unit goals** — three things you will be able to do at the end.
- **Remember?** — five questions at the start of Lesson 3, from earlier units.
- **Digital skills** — at the end of Lesson 1. A screen, a system or a message, and what you do.
- **Take It Outside** — at the end of Lesson 2. Find the same thing at your own work.
- **Culture Check** — in Lesson 4. How people do it differently. No right answer, on purpose.
- **Check Yourself** — at the end of every lesson. Say it to a partner before you tick it.

**The audio.** 🔊 marks a track. 194 tracks, numbered `CL1-unit-track`.

**One rule for this course.** If you do not understand, say so. In this book, *sorry, say that
again* is not a mistake. It is the most useful sentence there is, and Unit 4 teaches six ways to
say it.

## The people

**Ostberg** is a city by the sea. It has a port, an industrial estate and a university. Nobody in
this book says which country it is in, because it does not matter.

**Yara Haddad** works in operations at Lindmark Freight. **Anders Holm** is her warehouse
supervisor. **Chiamaka Obi** buys parts for Arvo Components. **Ravi Menon** checks quality at
Brightwell Pharma. **Sofia Reyes** looks after customers at Corvid Analytics. **Marek Dolny**
works in finance. **Nour Khalil** is in people operations. **Jun-seo Park** works for Juno
Textiles, which is a long way away and sells to all of them. **Paula Moreau** sells. **Daniel
Okonjo** runs a site. **Hanna Lindqvist** reads the contracts. **Wei Zhang** plans the trucks.

You will meet the same twelve people in all six books. Nobody in this book is good at English
because of where they come from, and nobody is bad at it either.
