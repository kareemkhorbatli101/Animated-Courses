| | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|

> **Grammar Note** — In a short answer, do not repeat the whole sentence: *Are you Anders?* —

### 2 · Possessives: *my, your, her* and *'s*

**2A  Study the chart.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **my · your · his · her · its · our · their** | **My** manager. **Their** warehouse. |
| **noun + 's** | Anders**'s** team. The compan**y's** address. |
| **plural noun + '** | the driver**s'** break room *(more than one driver)* |

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

How you say your own name at work is not the same everywhere. In some countries you give your
family name first and your first name second, and a form that says "First name / Last name" gets
it backwards. In some workplaces everybody uses first names, including with the director; in
others, using a manager's first name is rude, and the title matters more than the name. And in
some places you are introduced by somebody else and never introduce yourself at all.

**Discuss:** In your country, what do you call your manager? What would be strange?
