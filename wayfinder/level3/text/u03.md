> last. A plan that says a great deal about dates and very little about training is telling you

> *Internal · circulated to all staff · 14 October*
>
> 1  From **1 November** the new records system replaces the legacy database. The legacy system
> 2  will remain readable until **31 January** and will then be archived. Nothing will be lost.
>
> 3  **Why we are doing this.** The legacy system is fourteen years old. It cannot be accessed
> 4  from outside the building, it does not support two-factor login, and the company that built

### 1 · *as … as*

**1A  Listen and read. Then answer the opinion question.** %%AUD W4-03-06%%

> **Mei:** The new system isn't as slow as the old one, but it isn't as obvious either.
> **Grace:** Nothing is as obvious as the thing you already know how to use.
> **Mei:** So it's me, not the software.
> **Grace:** It's both. It's always both.

*Is a system that is harder at first but better later worth the two bad months?*

**1B  Underline every *as … as* in 1A. Which one is negative?**

**1C  Study the chart.**

| Use | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| The same | **as** + adjective + **as** | It's **as fast as** the old one. |
| Not the same | **not as** + adjective + **as** | It isn't **as slow as** the old one. |
| With an adverb | **as** + adverb + **as** | She learned it **as quickly as** he did. |
| With a quantity | **as much / many … as** | It takes **as many clicks as** before. |

> **Grammar Note** — *Not as … as* is a comparison, not an insult. "It isn't as good as the old
> one" says the old one is better. "It isn't as bad as the old one" says the new one is better.
> Learners often hear only the adjective and miss which way the sentence points.

**1D  Ask and answer with a partner. Three each.**

1. Is your phone as fast as it was a year ago?
2. Is reading a manual as useful as asking a colleague?
3. Is this course as hard as the last one you did?

**1E  Write four sentences** comparing two tools, systems or places you know, using all four rows.

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

In some workplaces, saying "I don't know how to do this" is normal and gets you help in a minute.

different costs.
