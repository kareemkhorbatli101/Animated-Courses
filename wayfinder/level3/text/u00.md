### 1 · The eight words you need for the rest of this book

**1A  Match the part of speech to its job. Then say each one aloud with your partner.**

| Part of speech | Its job | Example from the sentence on the board |
|---|---|---|
| **noun** | names a thing, a person, an idea | *decision* |
| **verb** | says what happens | *decide* |
| **adjective** | describes a noun | *decisive* |
| **adverb** | describes a verb or an adjective | *decisively* |
| **preposition** | places a thing in space, time or relation | *about* |
| **pronoun** | stands in for a noun | *it* |
| **conjunction** | joins two parts | *because* |
| **article** | says *which* or *how many* | *the*, *a* |

**1B  Listen. Ms Nkemelu reads the sentence twice. What is wrong with it?** %%AUD W4-00-01%%

> *Everybody in the group were very agree with the decision, because it was decide quickly.*

**1C  Find the three mistakes. Say what part of speech each one is.**

1. *were* → the subject is *everybody*, which is a ______________ and takes a singular verb.
2. *very agree* → *agree* is a ______________ , and *very* can only describe an adjective or an
   adverb.
3. *was decide* → the passive needs the past participle: a ______________ form, not the base.

### 3 · The tenses you already have

**3A  Say which tense each sentence uses, and what it is for.**

1. I work at Northgate.
2. I'm working at Northgate until June.
3. I worked there for two years.
4. I was working there when it closed.
5. I've worked there since 2024.
6. I've been working there all morning.
7. I'm going to work there next year.
8. I'll work there if they ask me.

**3B  Listen. Eight speakers, one sentence each. Write the number of the sentence above that
matches.** %%AUD W4-00-02%%

**3C  Which of the eight do you avoid?** Everybody avoids one. Tell your partner which, and
what you say instead.

> **Grammar Note** — The tense people avoid most at this level is number 6, the present perfect
> continuous. The usual substitute is number 5. That substitution is understandable nine times
> out of ten, and the tenth time it changes the meaning: *I've worked all morning* says the
> morning is over; *I've been working all morning* says you are still at it.

### 4 · What this book will ask of you

**4A  Read, then discuss in your group.**

> This book will ask you to read documents that were not written for you. A contract, a hospital
> letter, a job description. You will not understand every word, and you are not meant to. You
> are meant to find the three things that matter and be able to say what they are.
>
> It will also ask you to disagree. Several of the texts contain a number that does not support
> the sentence beside it. One of them is in Unit 1.

1. What do you do now when you meet a document you do not understand?
2. Is there a document you have signed without reading? What was it?
3. What would make you more likely to read the next one?
