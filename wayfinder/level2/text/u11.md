### 1 · The past continuous

**1A  Listen to the conversation.** %%AUD W3-11-05%%

> **Engineer:** What time did it go?
> **Yusuf:** Twenty past eight. I was cooking and the extractor stopped, so I noticed straight
> away.
> **Engineer:** Did you hear anything?
> **Yusuf:** A bang, about a minute before. I thought it was a bin.
> **Engineer:** It wasn't a bin. Was anybody in the lift?
> **Yusuf:** That's what I was checking when you arrived. Mrs Fiorelli was on the fourth floor and
> she wasn't answering, but she was asleep.
> **Engineer:** She was asleep.
> **Yusuf:** She slept through all of it. She's eighty-one. She's earned it.

**Your opinion:** Yusuf checked the lift before he called anybody. Was that the right order?

**1B  Look at the conversation again. Underline every *was* or *were* + *-ing*.**

**1C  Study the chart.**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| I / he / she / it | **was cooking** | when the power went. |
| you / we / they | **were waiting** | outside. |
| negative | She **wasn't answering**. | |
| question | **What were** you **doing**? | |

> **Grammar Note** — The past continuous sets the scene; the simple past is the event that
> interrupts it. *I **was cooking** when the power **went**.* The long thing takes *-ing*; the
> sudden thing does not.

**1D  Practice with a partner. Put the verb in the past continuous.**

1. I ______ (cook) when it happened.
2. They ______ (wait) for a bus.
3. She ______ (not answer) her phone.
4. What ______ you ______ (do) at eight?
5. We ______ (watch) something and the screen just went.

**1E  Write three sentences about what you were doing at three different times yesterday.**

### 2 · Past continuous and simple past together

**2A  Study the chart.**

| | |
|---|---|
| I **was cooking** **when** the power **went**. | background, then interruption |
| **While** I **was cooking**, the power **went**. | the same, with *while* |
| **When** the power **went**, I **called** 105. | two completed events in order |
| She **was sleeping** **when** it **happened**. | she did not wake up |

> **Grammar Note** — *when* + simple past is the sudden event. *while* + past continuous is the
> background. If you remember that *while* loves *-ing*, you will get it right most of the time.

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

In some places a power cut is an ordinary weekly event and every home has candles, water and a
plan. In others it happens twice a decade and nobody can find a torch.

**Talk about it in groups.**

1. How often does the power go where you are from? What does everybody do?
2. Who checks on the neighbors there? Is it organized or does it just happen?
3. Here, most people did not know about the priority register. Who should have told them?

> No right answer — but the people who manage best in an emergency are the ones who know their
> neighbors' names.
