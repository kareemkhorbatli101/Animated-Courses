### 2 · The tense this book gives you

Until now you could say what you **were**. From Unit 4 you can say what you **did**.

| you have this already | this book adds it |
|---|---|
| I **was** a teacher. | I **taught** for nine years. |
| It **was** difficult. | It **got** easier. |
| I **wasn't** ready. | I **didn't** apply. |

**2A  Listen and repeat.** %%AUD W3-00-02%%
