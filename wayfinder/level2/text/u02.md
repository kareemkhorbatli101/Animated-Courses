### 1 · *will* for predictions and decisions

**1A  Listen to the conversation.** %%AUD W3-02-05%%

> **Priya:** The forecast says showers until eleven.
> **Committee member:** It said that last year.
> **Priya:** It did. So I've booked the hall as well.
> **Committee member:** That's forty we might waste.
> **Priya:** It's forty we'll be glad we wasted. If it rains at two, thirty stalls will be standing
> in it.
> **Committee member:** And if it doesn't?
> **Priya:** Then I'll have spent forty on nothing and everybody will have a nice day. I'll take
> that.
> **Committee member:** All right. I'll help you carry the tables.

**Your opinion:** Priya says *"It's forty we'll be glad we wasted."* Is that a good way to spend
other people's money? Say what you think.

**1B  Look at the conversation again. Underline every *will* and *'ll*.**

**1C  Study the chart.**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **prediction** | It **will** rain. · It **won't** rain. | what you think will happen |
| **decision now** | I**'ll** take that. · I**'ll** help you. | decided as you speak |
| **offer** | **I'll** carry those. | |
| **question** | **Will** it rain? — Yes, it will. / No, it won't. | |

> **Grammar Note** — *will* and *be going to* are both future. Use **be going to** for a plan you
> already had (*I'm going to book the hall — I decided yesterday*). Use **will** for a decision you
> make as you speak (*I'll help you carry them — I just decided*).

**1D  Practice with a partner. Say *will* or *be going to*.**

1. (The phone rings) I ______ answer it.
2. I ______ visit my sister in June. I booked it last month.
3. Look at that sky. It ______ rain.
4. (Somebody drops a box) Wait, I ______ help you.
5. We ______ move the stalls if it rains. That's the plan.

**1E  Write three predictions about the next twelve months.** Use *will* and *won't*.

### 2 · Prepositions of time and place

**2A  Study the chart.**

| time | place |
|---|---|
| **at** eleven · **at** the weekend | **at** the hall · **at** the entrance |

### 2 · Say it clearly — *'ll* is almost nothing

**2A  Listen.** %%AUD W3-02-08%%

| written | said |
|---|---|
| I **will** help. (strong, deliberate) | *I will help.* |
| I**'ll** help. (normal) | *Aɫ help* — one syllable |
| It**'ll** rain. | *I**t**l rain* |
| That**'ll** be fine. | *Tha**tl** be fine* |

**2B  Listen. Is it a future or a present?** %%AUD W3-02-09%%

1. ______  2. ______  3. ______  4. ______

**2C  Practice with a partner.** Say four offers with *'ll*. Make them one syllable. Then say them
with the full *will* and notice how much more serious they sound.

> **Why this matters:** *I'll do it* is an offer. *I **will** do it* is a promise, or an argument.
> The difference is entirely in how much you say the word.

### 3 · The conversation

**3A  Read and listen.** %%AUD W3-02-10%%
