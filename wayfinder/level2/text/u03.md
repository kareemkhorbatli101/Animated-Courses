### 2 · Read

> **Reader's Tip** — In an agreement, find the clauses that begin **the tenant must** and **the
> landlord must**. The balance between those two lists tells you what kind of agreement it is.

%%FIG w03-f5 | A page of a tenancy agreement: five numbered clauses about rent, notice, repairs, what is not permitted, and the deposit, with the repair timescales shown in a small table.%%

> ## Tenancy agreement · extract
> *12 Market Street, Flat 2*
>
> **3. Rent** is due on the first of the month. The tenant is liable for rent until the tenancy
> ends, whether or not they are living there.
>
> **4. Notice.** Either side may end the tenancy with **one month's notice in writing**. A text
> message is not writing.
>
> **5. Repairs.** The landlord must repair the structure, the heating and the water supply.
> Emergencies: **24 hours**. Everything else: **14 days**. The tenant must report a problem within
> **48 hours** of noticing it.
>
> **6. Not permitted:** smoking inside, subletting, pets without written permission, or changing
> the locks.
>
> **7. Deposit** is held by a protection scheme. It is returned within 10 days of the end of the
> tenancy, less any agreed deductions.

<sub>**liable** — responsible for paying · **permitted** — allowed ·
**subletting** — renting your room to somebody else</sub>

### 1 · Comparatives

**1A  Listen to the conversation.** %%AUD W3-03-05%%

> **Adeel:** The first one is bigger.
> **Linh:** It's also damper, colder and further from the college.
> **Adeel:** It's forty cheaper.
> **Linh:** It's forty cheaper in rent. The boiler is nineteen years old, so the bills will be
> higher. Which is more important to you, the room or the money?
> **Adeel:** … the money.
> **Linh:** Then the first one isn't cheaper. It just looks cheaper in April.

**1B  Look at the conversation again. Underline every comparative.**

**1C  Study the chart.**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| short words | big → **bigger** · cheap → **cheaper** · damp → **damper** | add **-er** |
| ending in *-y* | easy → **easier** · noisy → **noisier** | *y* → *ier* |
| long words | expensive → **more expensive** · important → **more important** | **more** + adjective |
| irregular | good → **better** · bad → **worse** · far → **further** | learn these |
| comparing | A is cheaper **than** B. | always **than** |

> **Grammar Note** — Double the final consonant after a short vowel: *big → bigger*, *hot →
> hotter*, *wet → wetter*. Not *cheap → cheapper*, because *cheap* has two vowel letters.

### 2 · *Which* questions and adverbs of degree

**2A  Study the chart.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Which** one is cheaper? | A choice from a known set |
| **What** is cheap round here? | An open question |
| It's **much** cheaper. · **far** better. · **a lot** nearer. | a big difference |
| It's **a bit** smaller. · **slightly** colder. | a small difference |
| It's **not quite** as warm. | nearly, but not |

> **Grammar Note** — Use **which** when there is a set to choose from (two flats, four options).
> Use **what** when the field is open. *Which flat?* / *What kind of place are you looking for?*

### 3 · *Is … allowed?*

**3A  Study the chart.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Is** smoking **allowed**? | No, it isn't. |
| **Are** pets **allowed**? | Only with written permission. |
| You**'re not allowed to** change the locks. | |

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

In some countries a tenancy is a long relationship with one family and everything is spoken. In
others it is a contract with a company and nothing exists unless it is in writing and photographed.

**Talk about it in groups.**

1. How do people rent where you are from? From a person or a company?
2. Is asking for things in writing normal there, or does it suggest you do not trust somebody?
3. Linh says she rented *"badly, the first time."* What do you think happened?

> No right answer — but almost everybody rents badly the first time, in any country.

> ### Forty Cheaper
> *by Linh Pham*
>
> The first flat was bigger, forty cheaper and twenty minutes further from the college. It also had
