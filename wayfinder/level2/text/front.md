# Wayfinder 2
%%SUB Communication for Study, Work and Life · CEFR A2%%

## How to use this book

The third book in the Wayfinder series. A **Starting Point** and **twelve units**, each with the
same six lessons in the same order.

| In every unit | What happens there |
|---|---|
| **Lesson 1 · Vocabulary** | Two sets of new words, then a digital-skills task about a screen, a bill or a label that is telling you something other than the truth. |
| **Lesson 2 · Real-life Reading** | A real document, read the way you would read it outside: an agreement, a job listing, a council page, a safety notice. |
| **Lesson 3 · Grammar** | Two or three structures, each in a conversation before it is in a chart. |
| **Lesson 4 · Everyday Conversation** | Pronunciation, a model conversation, a workplace box and a culture box with no right answer. |
| **Lesson 5 · Writing** | A longer text by one of the people in this book, then your own. |
| **Team Up & Review** | Team tasks, a class survey with a chart, and one problem with no answer printed. |

**What is new in this book.** Three things:

- **The past tense opens properly.** Unit 4 gives the regular simple past; Unit 8 gives the
  irregular verbs and *had to*; Unit 11 gives the past continuous. By the end you can tell any
  story that has already happened.
- **Texts are longer and arguments are real.** Readings run to about 200 words. The conversations
  contain disagreements where both people are partly right.
- **Phrase banks are nearly gone.** Two per unit, and the *Think It Through* cases have none at
  all. From here, the support is a strategy line, not a list of phrases.

**The people.** Portmere, two years after Wayfinder Starter. Nadia is on the nursing access course.
Ahmad runs a team. Mei is on placement. Tomás supervises a shift. Linh and Adeel are looking for
somewhere to live. Oksana, who was a nurse for nineteen years, is trying to prove she lives where
she lives.

**The audio.** 🔊 marks a track. 193 tracks, numbered `W3-unit-track`.

## Scope and sequence

| Unit | Vocabulary | Grammar | Pronunciation | Reading | Writing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starting Point** | Review; four questions | Present, past of *be*, *going to* | — | — | — |
| **1 · Study Smarter** | Study habits; obstacles | *want / need / like to*; information questions; *this / that* | *to* reduced | A study-skills handout | Eleven hours |
| **2 · Make Plans** | Events; weather | *will* for prediction and decision; prepositions; time expressions | *'ll* | An event page; an hourly forecast | Forty on nothing |
| **3 · A New Place** | Housing; problems | Comparatives; *which*; adverbs of degree; *Is … allowed?* | *-er than* | A tenancy agreement | Forty cheaper |
| **4 · Find Work** | Work actions; job search | Simple past (regular); past questions; adverbs of manner | The three *-ed* sounds | An online job listing | What I actually did |
| **5 · First Week at Work** | Starting a job | *might*; *should*; requests with *can / could* | Politeness in the tune | An induction handout | Ten seconds |
| **6 · Stay Connected** | Phones and plans | Present simple vs continuous; two objects | Contractions carry the tense | A plan comparison; a bill | Forty and eleven |
| **7 · Cook at Home** | Kitchen; amounts | Count / noncount; *How much / many*; *There is / are* | *of* reduced | A food-dates leaflet | What an hour is worth |
| **8 · Feeling Better** | Injuries; urgent care | Irregular past; *have to / had to* | Short past vowels | A triage notice | Six lines in the car |
| **9 · Manage Money** | Banking; problems | Purpose with *to / because*; *too / not enough*; *would like* | *'d like* | A complaints page; a statement | Thirty-two pounds |

| **11 · When Things Go Wrong** | Emergencies | Past continuous; with the simple past; *There was / were* | *was / were* reduced | A power-cut page | Twenty past eight |
| **12 · Time to Relax** | Leisure; comparing | Superlatives; comparative vs superlative | *the* before superlatives | A leisure guide | The hill |
