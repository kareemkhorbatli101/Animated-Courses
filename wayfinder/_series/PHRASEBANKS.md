# Need help? — the phrase banks

The speaking spine (§2.1 of the plan) guarantees an open-ended oral task in every lesson. A phrase
bank is what stops that task dying in a mixed class. "Discuss in pairs" with no language supplied is
not a task; it is a hope.

**Rule:** every open-ended speaking prompt at Starter, Level 1 and Level 2 has a bank beside it on
the page. Banks are withdrawn across Levels 3–5, deliberately and visibly, as learners can carry a
turn themselves. A withdrawn bank is replaced by a strategy line, not by nothing.

## Starter (A1) — four banks, recycled all book

**Bank S1 — Saying what you think**
I think so. · I don't think so. · Me too. · Not me. · Yes, and … · I like … · I don't like …

**Bank S2 — When you don't understand**
Sorry? · Again, please. · Slowly, please. · How do you spell it? · What does … mean? · Like this?

**Bank S3 — Asking a partner**
And you? · What about you? · Do you …? · Is it …? · Your turn.

**Bank S4 — Agreeing on an answer**
Number one is … · I think it's … · Is it … or …? · OK. · You're right. · Let's write it.

## Level 1 (A1+/A2) — four banks, building on Starter's

**Bank 1-1 — Giving a reason**
because … · so … · for example … · the reason is …

**Bank 1-2 — Not sure**
Maybe. · I'm not sure. · I think maybe … · It depends.

**Bank 1-3 — Disagreeing politely**
Yes, but … · I see, but … · Really? I think … · That's true, but …

**Bank 1-4 — Keeping it going**
Why? · Why not? · Tell me more. · What happened then? · And after that?

## Level 2 (A2) — withdrawal begins

Level 2 carries **two** banks per unit rather than four, and the *Think It Through* case carries
none: by Level 2 Unit 6 the class argues a case with no language supplied, which is the first time
the course asks that. The banks that remain are for the functions that are genuinely new at A2 —
making a suggestion, conceding a point, reporting what a partner said.

## Levels 3–5 — strategy lines, not banks

Instead of phrases, one line of strategy beside the prompt: *"Give your opinion, then one reason,
then ask your partner for theirs."* · *"Disagree with the reason, not the person."* ·
*"If you cannot answer, say what you would need to know."*
