### 2 · Four questions

These four questions start almost every conversation in this book. Learn them now.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where are you from?** | I'm from Lviv. |
| **How long have you been here?** | Eight months. |
| **What do you do?** | I work in a warehouse. |
| **What are you studying for?** | A nursing course. |

**2A  Listen and repeat.** %%AUD W2-00-02%%

**2B  Ask four people. Write their answers. Then tell the class about one person.**

> **Need help?** Bank 1-4 — Why? · Why not? · Tell me more. · And after that?

### 3 · This is …

**3A  Introduce the person next to you to somebody else. Use the model.**

> **A:** Samira, this is Adeel. He's from Lahore.
> **B:** Nice to meet you, Adeel.
> **C:** You too. Are you on the nursing course?
> **B:** Not yet. I'm trying.

**3B  Change partners and do it again. Say one extra thing about your partner each time.**
