# Grammar reference

### Starting Point and Unit 1 · *be*

| | long | short | negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I am | **I'm** | I'm not |
| you / we / they | you are | **you're** | you aren't · you're not |
| he / she / it | she is | **she's** | she isn't · she's not |

Yes/no questions put *am / is / are* first: **Are** you a student? *Wh-* questions put the question
word first: **Where are** you from? Short answers keep the full form: *Yes, I **am***.

### Unit 2 · *at*, *on*, *in*

| **at** + clock time | **on** + day or date | **in** + month, year, period |
|---|---|---|
| at six · at half past seven · at night | on Thursday · on 30 September · on Monday evening | in September · in 2027 · **in nine days** · **in five minutes** |

**in** also means *after this much time*. *In nine days* is not the same as *on the ninth*.

### Unit 3 · *have* / *has*, possessives

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | **have** / **don't have** | a key. |
| he / she / it | **has** / **doesn't have** | a key. |
| **Do** you **have** …? | **Does** she **have** …? | never *Does she has* |

You will also hear **have got**: *I've got · Has she got? · She hasn't.* Same meaning. Do not mix
the two: *Do you have got* is not English.

| | |
|---|---|
| my · your · his · her · our · their | + noun: *her* key |
| mine · yours · his · hers · ours · theirs | alone: it's *hers* |
| name + **'s** | Mei**'s** key |
| plural + **'** | the tenant**s'** kitchen |

### Unit 4 · *there is* / *there are*

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **There's** | a shower. | one thing |
| **There are** | two bedrooms. | more than one |
| **There isn't** / **There aren't any** | a bath · any cupboards. | negative |
| **Is there** …? / **Are there any** …? | Yes, there is. / No, there aren't. | |

Use **any** in negatives and questions with plurals; **some** in positives.

Prepositions of place: **in · on · under · above · below · next to · beside · between · behind ·
in front of · opposite · in the corner · in the middle · against the wall**.
*above* is higher but not touching; *on* is touching.

### Unit 5 · Present continuous

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| I | **'m standing** | on the corner. |
| you / we / they | **'re walking** | the wrong way. |
| he / she / it | **'s looking** | out of the window. |

Spelling: *walk → walking*, *come → coming* (drop the *e*), *get → getting* (double the consonant).

*Where are you?* gets a place. *Where are you standing?* gets the exact spot.

### Unit 6 · Simple present and frequency

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | **start** / **don't start** | at seven. |
| he / she / it | **start*s*** / **doesn't start** | at seven. |
| **Do** you …? | **What time does** she …? | the *-s* moves into *does* |

**always · usually · often · sometimes · rarely · never** go before the main verb but **after**
*be*: *I **often** work late.* · *I am **always** tired.*
*never* is already negative: *I never go*, not *I don't never go*.

### Unit 7 · *have*, *want*, *need*; *How much* / *How many*

*have* = what is in your hand. *want* = what you would like. *need* = what you cannot do without.

**How much** + things you don't count (money, rice, time as a quantity).
**How many** + things you count (months, payments, hours).

### Unit 8 · Count and noncount

| | I count them | I don't count them |
|---|---|---|
| **+** | some · a few · a lot of · three | some · a little · a lot of |
| **−** | not any · not many | not any · not much |
| **?** | How **many**? | How **much**? |

*a few* and *a little* = a small amount, and that is fine. *not many* and *not much* = a small
amount, and it is a problem.

### Unit 9 · Imperatives, *should*, *since* / *for*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Keep** moving. · **Don't** rest. | an instruction |
| You **should** come back. · You **shouldn't** lift that. | advice |
| **Should I** …? | asking for advice |

*should* is the same for everybody and takes a plain verb: *she should come*.

**since** + a point in time (*since August*, *since Monday*).
**for** + a length of time (*for three months*).

### Unit 10 · Past of *be*; *and*, *too*, *but*

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| I / he / she / it | **was** / **wasn't** | an engineer. |
| you / we / they | **were** / **weren't** | late. |
| **Was** he …? | **Were** you …? | Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't. |
| **There was** a problem. | **There were** six people. | |

*and* adds · *too* goes at the **end** (*I was nervous too*) · *but* contrasts.

In a sentence, *was* and *were* are weak and quiet. In a short answer they are the loudest word.

### Unit 11 · *should* / *shouldn't*; question types

| yes/no | information |
|---|---|
| **Is** the exit blocked? | **What** is blocking it? |
| **Should** I report it? | **Who** should I report it to? |
| **Was** it there yesterday? | **How long** has it been there? |

A yes/no question gets one bit of information; an information question gets what you need.

**should have** + past participle talks about the past: *They **should have** told you.*

### Unit 12 · *be going to*

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| I | **'m going to** | sleep. |
| you / we / they | **'re going to** | meet at one. |
| he / she / it | **'s going to** | rain. |
| negative | I **'m not going to** pay twelve. | |

Use it for a plan already made, and for something you can see coming.
In speech it sounds like *gonna*. **Write** *going to*.

**was/were going to** = a plan that changed: *I **was going to** sleep, but Nadia woke me up.*

**Most tracks read text that is printed in the book.** Tracks 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 10, 11, 12, 14 and
16 are the words on the page, read aloud at about 110 words a minute with a clear pause at every
sentence boundary. They need no separate script.

**Five kinds of track are not printed in the book**, because the learner has to catch something
that is not in front of them. Those are below.

or date read aloud. Unit 5 is printed in full here as the model; the other eleven follow its shape.

> in her other hand she has a marker pen and a piece of card.
