# Wayfinder 1
%%SUB Communication for Study, Work and Life · CEFR A1+ to A2%%

## How to use this book

This is the second book in the Wayfinder series. It has a **Starting Point** and **twelve units**,
and every unit has the same six lessons, in the same order, so that after Unit 1 you always know
where you are.

| In every unit | What happens there |
|---|---|
| **Lesson 1 · Vocabulary** | Two sets of new words, with pictures, then a digital-skills task about a screen, a label or a form that is misleading. |
| **Lesson 2 · Real-life Reading** | A real document — a leaflet, a handbook page, a listing, an advertisement — read the way you would read it outside. |
| **Lesson 3 · Grammar** | Two structures, each one in a conversation first and a chart second. |
| **Lesson 4 · Everyday Conversation** | Pronunciation, a model conversation, a workplace box and a culture box with no right answer. |
| **Lesson 5 · Writing** | A short true-sounding text by one of the people in this book, then your own. |
| **Team Up & Review** | Team tasks, a class survey with a chart, and one problem with no answer printed. |

**What is new in this book.** Three things:

- **The past.** Unit 10 gives you *was* and *were*, and Unit 12 gives you *be going to*. For the
  first time you can say what you **were** and what you are **going to** do. The rest of the past
  tense comes in Wayfinder 2.
- **Longer texts.** Readings run to about 140 words and the writing models to about 90. The
  *Need help?* phrase banks are still here, but there are two per unit instead of four — they are
  being taken away on purpose, a little at a time.
- **Harder questions.** The *Think It Through* cases at the end of each unit no longer have a
  simple answer. Several of them have no good answer at all. That is deliberate.

**The people.** This book is set in Portmere, one year after Wayfinder Starter. Nadia is applying
for a nursing course. Tomás runs a shift. Mei is on hospital placement. Yusuf has moved his family
across the city. Ahmad is interviewing for a job he would not have applied for last year. Four new
people arrive: Linh, Adeel, Oksana and Samira.

**The audio.** 🔊 marks a track. 195 tracks, numbered `W2-unit-track`.

## Scope and sequence

| Unit | Vocabulary | Grammar | Pronunciation | Reading | Writing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starting Point** | Greetings; four opening questions | *be* review | — | — | — |
| **1 · Hello Again** | Jobs; enrolling | Contractions of *be*; questions with *be* | Weak *are* and *a* | A course leaflet; a class list | Starting again |
| **2 · Can You Help Me?** | Around a college; asking for help | *at*, *on*, *in*; yes/no questions | The question tune | An attendance page; a timetable | Twenty minutes |
| **3 · People I Know** | Describing people; sharing a home | *have* / *has*; possessives | *has/hasn't*, *his/he's* | A house agreement; a rota | The people I live with |
| **4 · My Place** | Furniture; rooms | *there is* / *there are*; prepositions of place | *there's* / *theirs* | A property listing; an inventory | Our new place |
| **5 · Around the Block** | Streets; directions | Present continuous; *Where* questions | Stress on the important word | An access note; a street map | The blue door |
| **6 · My Week** | Routines; work and study | Simple present; adverbs of frequency | Third-person *-s* | A shift notice; a shift pattern | My week |
| **7 · Spend Smart** | Paying; things people buy | *have*, *want*, *need*; *How much* / *How many* | *-teen* and *-ty* | An offer; a payslip | Three hundred and sixty |
| **8 · Good Food** | Food; containers and amounts | Count and noncount; quantity words | Linking words together | A budget-cooking leaflet; a recipe | The first Sunday |
| **9 · Stay Healthy** | Symptoms; at an appointment | Imperatives; *should*; *since* / *for* | *should* / *shouldn't* | A health leaflet; an appointment letter | Three lines on a piece of paper |
| **10 · Job Hunt** | Job-hunting words; the interview | Past of *be*; *and*, *too*, *but* | Weak and strong *was* | A job advertisement; a CV | What I was |
| **11 · Be Safe** | Safety signs; hazards | *should* / *shouldn't*; question types | The stressed warning | A safety notice; a hazard book | Forty-one signs |
| **12 · Free Time** | Free time; planning | *be going to*; *was* / *were* for results | *going to* in fast speech | A what's-on page; a bus timetable | The first Saturday |
