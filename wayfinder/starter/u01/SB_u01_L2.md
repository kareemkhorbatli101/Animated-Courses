### 🌍 `TAKE IT OUTSIDE`

Find **one sign** with a name on it — in your building, on your street, on a bus, on a door. A shop
name, a street name, a person's name.

1. Copy the name exactly. Count the letters.
2. Bring it to the next class. **Say it and spell it** to your group.
3. Your group writes it down. Then you check: is it correct?

*Your class brings all the names to* **Team Up & Review** *and builds one list.*
