# Starter · Unit 1 · Audio scripts
**16 tracks.** Ids `W1-01-01` … `W1-01-16`.

**Voices.** Nadia Rahman — 24, unhurried, a slight hesitation before long words. Mrs Adaeze Oyelaran
— 52, warm and deliberate, says important things twice. Ms Clara Nkemelu — 35, clear classroom
voice, never rushed. Ahmad Sultani — 41, formal, careful. Grace Abara — 29, brisk. Yusuf Dalmar —
38, few words, even tone. Tomás Alcaraz — 31, quick and friendly. Narrator — neutral, adult, slower
than natural speech by about 15% at this level.

**Pace.** Starter runs at roughly 100–110 words per minute with a clear pause at every sentence
boundary. Spelled letters are given at one letter per second with a half-beat between groups.

**Variant note.** Only one line in this unit diverges between editions: see `W1-01-07` below.
