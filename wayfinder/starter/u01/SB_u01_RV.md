## Team Up & Review  (pp. 16–17)

### A · Look at the picture

`[ART u01-a13 — Room 4 on day one, eight students at four tables, Ms Nkemelu at the board writing
GROUP 2 · ROOM 4; bags, notebooks, one empty chair, a window onto Fenner Street]`

**In your team, answer.**

1. How many students are in the picture?
2. How many chairs are empty?
3. What is on the board?
4. What is on the tables?

### B · Write a team summary

**Use the model. Change the words for your class.**

> This is Room 4. There are eight students. Ms Nkemelu is the teacher. The students are new. Their
> notebooks are new too. One chair is empty.

Write four sentences about **your** classroom. One person writes. Everyone checks.

### C · Report

Read your summary to another team. They listen and say **one** thing that is the same and **one**
thing that is different.

> desk: B-L-A-N-C-A. On Tuesday her student card is ready. But the name on the card is "Blanka". It is
