### 1 · *I am, you are, she is*

**1A  Listen to the conversation.**  `🔊 W1-01-05`

> **Mrs Oyelaran:** Good morning. Are you a new student?
> **Nadia:** Yes, I am. I'm Nadia Rahman.
> **Mrs Oyelaran:** Welcome, Nadia. Your group is Group 2.
> **Nadia:** Room 4?
> **Mrs Oyelaran:** That's right. Ms Nkemelu is your teacher. She's in Room 4 now.
> **Ahmad:** Excuse me — I'm Ahmad. Am I in Group 2?
> **Mrs Oyelaran:** You are. You're both in Room 4.

**Your opinion:** Mrs Oyelaran says *"Welcome, Nadia."* Is that important on a first day? Why?

**1B  Look at the conversation again. Underline *am*, *is* and *are*.** How many did you find?

**1C  Study the chart.**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| I | **am** | a student. |
| You | **are** | in Group 2. |
| He / She / It | **is** | in Room 4. |
| We / You / They | **are** | new students. |

> **Grammar Note** — In speaking we join the words: *I am → I'm · you are → you're · she is → she's ·
> they are → they're*. Write the short form with an apostrophe (').

**1D  Practice with a partner. Say the sentence with the short form.**

1. I am Nadia. → *I'm Nadia.*
2. You are in Room 4. → ______________
3. She is my teacher. → ______________
4. They are new students. → ______________
5. He is at the front desk. → ______________

**1E  Write one sentence for each rule.** Use *am*, *is* and *are* — one of each. Write about you,
your partner and your class.

### 2 · *a*, *an* and plurals

**2A  Study the chart.**

| one | more than one |
|---|---|
| **a** pen | two pen**s** |
| **a** book | three book**s** |
| **an** email | four email**s** |
| **an** address | two address**es** |

> **Grammar Note** — Use **an** before *a, e, i, o, u*: *an email, an address, an apple*. Use **a**
> before the other letters: *a pen, a book, a student*.
> Add **-es** after *s, sh, ch, x*: *address → addresses*.

**2B  Listen and repeat.**  `🔊 W1-01-06`

**2C  Complete with *a*, *an* or a plural.**

4. Please write your ______ (address) here.
5. Is that ______ email or ______ phone number?
6. Ms Nkemelu is ______ teacher. Her students are in two ______ (group).
