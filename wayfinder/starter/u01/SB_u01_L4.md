### 3 · The conversation

**3A  Read and listen.**  `🔊 W1-01-10`

`[ART u01-a09 — the front desk, Nadia and Mrs Oyelaran, the tablet turned so both can see it;
character poses: Nadia leaning in to read, Mrs Oyelaran pointing at the screen]`

> **Mrs Oyelaran:** Good morning. Are you a new student?
> **Nadia:** Yes, I am.
> **Mrs Oyelaran:** What's your name?
> **Nadia:** Nadia Rahman.
> **Mrs Oyelaran:** How do you spell your last name?
> **Nadia:** R-A-H-M-A-N.
> **Mrs Oyelaran:** Thank you. And your first name — is that N-A-D-I-A?
> **Nadia:** Yes, that's correct.

**3B  Choose a or b.**

1. Nadia is  (a) a new student  (b) a teacher
2. Mrs Oyelaran asks about  (a) her address  (b) her last name
3. Nadia spells  (a) Rahman  (b) Nadia
4. At the end, the name is  (a) correct  (b) wrong

**3C  Think about the grammar.**

1. Mrs Oyelaran says *"Are you a new student?"* Nadia says *"Yes, I am."* Why not *"Yes, I'm"*?
2. Find two questions that start with a question word.

**Study the chart. Then listen and repeat.**  `🔊 W1-01-11`

| | |
|---|---|
| **What's** your name? | Nadia Rahman. |
| **How do you spell** it? | R-A-H-M-A-N. |
| **Are you** a new student? | Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. |
| **Is that** correct? | Yes, that's correct. / No, sorry. |

> **Grammar Note** — Short answers keep the full form: *Yes, I **am**.* Not *Yes, I'm.*

### 💼 `ON THE JOB`

**Giving your name on a first shift.**  `🔊 W1-01-12`

> **1** — **Supervisor:** Good morning. Are you the new driver?
> **Yusuf:** Yes, I am. I'm Yusuf Dalmar.
> **Supervisor:** Welcome, Yusuf. I'm Grace.
>
> **2** — **Grace:** What's your last name again?
> **Yusuf:** Dalmar. D-A-L-M-A-R.
> **Grace:** Thanks. It's on the gate list now.
>
> **3** — **Grace:** This is Tomás. Tomás, this is Yusuf. He's new.
> **Tomás:** Hi, Yusuf. Nice to meet you.
> **Yusuf:** Nice to meet you too.

**A  Practice all three in groups of three.** Change roles each time.

**B  Think about it.**

1. Grace says *"I'm Grace."* — not *"I'm Ms Abara."* What does that tell Yusuf about this workplace?
2. Yusuf spells his name **before** anyone asks twice. Why is that a good idea on a first day?
3. Someone says your name wrong on your first shift. What do you say? Say it out loud.

> *Need help?* It's **Yusuf**, actually. · Sorry — it's … · Nearly! It's … · Let me spell it.

### 🌐 `CULTURE CHECK`

In some places you say your **family name first** and your own name second. In other places it is the
other way round. On a form here, your family name goes in **Last name**.

**Talk about it in groups.**

1. In your language, which name do you say first?
2. What do people call you at school? And at work? Is it the same?
3. Your teacher calls you by your family name. Is that normal for you — or strange?

> There is no right answer here. The point is that **your** normal is not everybody's normal, and a
> form does not know that.
