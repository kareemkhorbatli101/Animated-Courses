### 2 · Read the model

**2A  Listen and read.**  `🔊 W1-01-14`

> ### My First Day
> *by Nadia Rahman*
>
> My name is Nadia Rahman. Nadia is my first name. Rahman is my last name. I am from Tabriz. I am a
> student at Portmere Language Center. My group is Group 2. My classroom is Room 4. My teacher is Ms
> Nkemelu. My notebook is new and my pens are blue. It is a good first day.

> **Writer's Tip** — Start with your name. Use a **capital letter** for your name, your country and
> the name of your school: *Nadia · Tabriz · Portmere Language Center*.

**2B  Circle the correct word.**

1. Nadia's first name is ( Nadia / Rahman ).
2. She is a ( student / teacher ).
3. Her classroom is Room ( 2 / 4 ).
4. Her pens are ( new / blue ).

**2C  Listen to Ahmad. Circle what you hear.**  `🔊 W1-01-15`

1. Ahmad is from ( Herat / Tehran ).
2. He is in Group ( 1 / 2 ).
3. His classroom is Room ( 4 / 2 ).
4. In his country he is ( a student / an engineer ).

**Now compare with a partner.** Is your list the same? Where is it different? Listen once more and
agree on the answer.

### 4 · Write

**4A  Write about your first day. Use the frame.**

> ### My First Day
> *by _________________*
>
> My name is _________________ . _________________ is my first name. _________________ is my last
> name. I am from _________________ . I am a _________________ at _________________ . My group is
> _________________ . My classroom is _________________ . My _________________ is
> _________________ . It is a good first day.

**4B  Read your text to a partner.** Your partner listens and checks three things:

- Is there a capital letter on every name?
- Is it *a* or *an*?
- Is every sentence finished with a full stop?
