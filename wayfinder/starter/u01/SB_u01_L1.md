# Starter · Unit 1 · Nice to Meet You
## Lesson 1 — Vocabulary  (pp. 4–5)

> **UNIT GOALS**
> • Say and spell your name, your country and your phone number
> • Use *a*, *an* and plurals for the things in your bag and your classroom
> • Fill in an arrival form — and check it

*Setting: Portmere Language Center, Fenner Street. Monday, 8:40 a.m. Nadia Rahman is at the front
desk. Mrs Oyelaran has a tablet and a question.*

---

### 1 · Personal information

**1A  Circle the words you know. Then say them to a partner.**
`[ART u01-a01 — eight word cards, each with a small icon]`

first name · last name · country · address · phone number · email · student · teacher

> *Need help?* **Bank S2** — Sorry? · Again, please. · How do you spell it?

**1B  Listen. Look at the picture. Where is Nadia?**  `🔊 W1-01-01`
`[ART u01-a02 — the front desk: Mrs Oyelaran, a tablet, a queue of three, a board behind her]`

**1C  Listen and repeat.**  `🔊 W1-01-02`

**1D  Write the words. The first one is an example.**

1. Her ______________ is Nadia.  → *first name*
2. Her ______________ is Rahman.
3. Her ______________ is 12 Fenner Street.
4. Her ______________ is 7700 418 206.
5. Her ______________ is n.rahman@mail.example.
6. Nadia is a ______________ . Ms Nkemelu is a ______________ .

**1E  Ask and answer. Use the model.**

> **A:** What's your first name?
> **B:** Hassan.
> **A:** How do you spell it?
> **B:** H-A-S-S-A-N.

Now ask your partner for their last name, their country and their phone number. Write the answers.
Then check: read them back and ask *Is that correct?*

### 2 · In your bag and in your classroom

**2A  Match the words to the picture.**
`[ART u01-a03 — an open bag on a desk and the classroom behind it, nine items numbered 1–9]`

book · notebook · pen · pencil · bag · chair · table · board · computer

**2B  Listen and check. Then say the nine words to your partner. Your partner points.**  `🔊 W1-01-03`

**2C  Complete the paragraph.**

This is Room 4. It is a classroom. There is a big (1) ______________ at the front. Ms Nkemelu is at
the (2) ______________ with her (3) ______________ . Nadia is on a (4) ______________ near the
window. In her (5) ______________ there is a new notebook.

**2D  Think about it.** *Talk with a partner.*

1. What is in your bag today? Tell your partner three things.
2. Your teacher says a word and you don't understand it. What do you say?

> *Need help?* **Bank S2** and **Bank S3** — And you? · What about you? · What does … mean?

### `AUG-a` · Check the screen

`[ART u01-a04 — the front desk tablet, a filled form, the name fields swapped]`

The tablet at the front desk reads Nadia's passport and fills in the form by itself. Look at the
screen.

| | |
|---|---|
| **First name** | RAHMAN |
| **Last name** | NADIA |
| **Country** | — |

1. Find the mistake.
2. What does Nadia say to Mrs Oyelaran? Choose: *"It's wrong."* / *"Excuse me — my first name is
   Nadia."* / Say nothing.
3. **Talk about it.** A machine filled in this form. Is a machine always correct? Who checks it?

> **Writer's Tip** — *first name* is your own name. *Last name* is your family name. On most forms in
> this country, the family name goes in **Last name** — even if you say it first at home.
