**1B  Look at the conversation again. Underline *am*, *is*, *are*, *isn't* and *I'm not*.**

**1C  Study the chart.**

| | | |
|---|---|---|

| He / She / It | **is** / **isn't** | free. |

> **Grammar Note** — The negative has two short forms: *she is not* → *she isn't* **or** *she's

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

In some places, *"How are you?"* is a real question and people answer it properly. In other places
it is only a greeting, like *hello*, and the answer is always *"Fine, thanks."*

**Talk about it in groups.**

1. In your language, is *"How are you?"* a real question?
2. Your neighbor asks how you are. You are not fine. Do you say so? To a neighbor? To a doctor?
   To a manager?
3. Somebody answers *"Fine"* but they look terrible. What do you do?

> There is no right answer here. But at a clinic, *"Fine, thanks"* is the wrong answer.
