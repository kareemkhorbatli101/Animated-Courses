### 1 · *What* and *Where* with the present continuous

**1A  Listen to the conversation.** %%AUD W1-11-05%%

> **Nadia:** Excuse me. Where are the computers?
> **Priya:** At the back, past the children's books. What are you doing — printing?
> **Nadia:** Printing two pages. The college wants them today.
> **Priya:** The printer is next to the computers. It takes coins.
> **Nadia:** I don't have coins.
> **Priya:** The machine by the door gives change. Where are you going after?
> **Nadia:** The post office. Is it still open?
> **Priya:** Until half past five. You're fine. Go to the printer first.

**Your opinion:** Priya asks *"Where are you going after?"* before she answers. Why?

**1B  Look at the conversation again. Underline every *What* and *Where* question.**

**1C  Study the chart.**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | are you **doing**? | I'm printing. |
| **Where** | are you **going**? | To the post office. |
| **Where** | **is** the printer? | Next to the computers. |
| **Who** | **is** helping you? | Priya. |

> **Grammar Note** — Word order does not change for *What* and *Where*: question word, then
> *am / is / are*, then the person, then *-ing*. *Where are you going?* never *Where you are
> going?*

**1D  Practice with a partner. Make the question.**

1. (what / you / do) → *What are you doing?*
2. (where / she / go) → ______________
3. (what / they / print) → ______________
4. (where / he / wait) → ______________
5. (who / you / wait for) → ______________

**1E  Write three questions to ask your partner about now, not about every day.**

### 3 · The conversation

**3A  Read and listen.** %%AUD W1-11-10%%

### 💼 ON THE JOB

**Directing somebody at work.** %%AUD W1-11-12%%

> **1** — **Visitor:** Excuse me. Where's the office?
> **Yusuf:** Straight on, past the forklift, then left. The blue door.
> **Visitor:** Thank you.
>
> **2** — **Visitor:** Sorry — is this the blue door?
> **Yusuf:** That's the store. The office is the next one. I'll show you.
>
> **3** — **Grace:** Yusuf, why are you on aisle one?
> **Yusuf:** I'm showing a visitor to the office.
> **Grace:** Good. Nobody finds it the first time.

**A  Practice all three in pairs.**

**B  Think about it.**

1. The second time, Yusuf stops explaining and walks with the visitor. When is that the right
   choice?
2. Grace does not tell him off. What does *"Nobody finds it the first time"* tell you about the
   place, not the person?
3. Somebody at your work or college is lost. What do you say? Say it out loud.

> **Need help?** Go straight on, then … · It's the … door. · I'll show you. · Follow me.

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

In some cities people give directions with street names and numbers. In others they use buildings,
shops and *"where the old market used to be"*. Some people will walk with you rather than explain.

**Talk about it in groups.**

1. In your city, how do people give directions — streets, or landmarks?
2. Somebody does not know the way but does not want to say so. What do they do where you are from?
3. You ask three people and get three different answers. What do you do?

> No right answer — but *"I don't know, sorry"* is a complete and helpful sentence.
