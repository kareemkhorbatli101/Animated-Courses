# Wayfinder Starter
%%SUB Communication for Study, Work and Life · CEFR A1%%

## How to use this book

This book has a **Starting Point** and **twelve units**. Every unit is the same shape, so after
Unit 1 you always know where you are.

| In every unit | What happens there |
|---|---|
| **Lesson 1 · Vocabulary** | Two sets of new words, with pictures. You meet them, say them, write them, and use them with a partner. |
| **Lesson 2 · Real-life Reading** | A real document — a notice, a timetable, a label, a form. You read it the way you would read it outside. |
| **Lesson 3 · Grammar** | Two structures, each one shown in a conversation first and explained second. |
| **Lesson 4 · Everyday Conversation** | A pronunciation point, a model conversation, a workplace box and a culture box. |
| **Lesson 5 · Writing** | A short true-sounding text by one of the people in this book, then your own. |
| **Team Up & Review** | Team tasks, a class survey with a chart, and one problem with no answer printed. |

Six things come back in every unit:

- **Unit goals** — three things you will be able to do at the end.
- **Remember?** — five questions at the start of Lesson 3, from earlier units. Say them first, then write them.
- **Digital skills** — at the end of Lesson 1. A screen, an app or a label that is wrong, and what you do about it.
- **Take It Outside** — at the end of Lesson 2. Something to find in the real world and bring back.
- **Culture Check** — in Lesson 4. No right answer, on purpose.
- **Check Yourself** — at the end of every lesson. Say it to a partner before you tick it.

**The people.** This book happens in Portmere, a city by the sea. You will meet the same people in
every unit — Nadia, Tomás, Mei, Yusuf, Priya, Grace, Ahmad and the others — and they come back in
Wayfinder 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5. By the last book, you will be able to tell their story in English, and
your own.

**The audio.** 🔊 marks a track. The number tells you which one.

## Scope and sequence

| Unit | Vocabulary | Grammar | Pronunciation | Reading | Writing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starting Point** | Classroom things; the alphabet | Imperatives | The alphabet | Classroom instructions | — |
| **1 · Nice to Meet You** | Personal information; classroom objects | Nouns and plurals; subject pronouns + *be*; *a* / *an* | /ɪ/ and /e/ | A welcome sheet; an arrival form | My first day |
| **2 · How Are You Today?** | Feelings; at the clinic | *be* + adjective; negative *be*; *from* and *in* | Counting syllables | A clinic notice; a sign-in screen | A bad morning |
| **3 · What Time?** | Times of day; getting there | *It's* + time; *at*, *in*, *on* | Yes/no intonation | A bus timetable; a shift board | One day of my week |
| **4 · Mark the Date** | Days, months, dates | *in* / *on* / *at* with dates; *When?* *What time?* | /t/ and /θ/ | An appointment card | A day I remember |
| **5 · How Much Is It?** | Money; at the stall | *How much is / are*; this, that, these, those; plurals | /s/ and /ʃ/ | A price board; a receipt | Saturday at the market |
| **6 · My People** | Family; housemates | Possessive adjectives; simple present; information questions | *who* and *how* | A student handbook; a contact form | The people I live with |
| **7 · At the Market** | Food; containers and amounts | *don't* / *doesn't*; *some*, *any*, *much*, *many* | The *-s* ending | A market leaflet; a food label | One meal I cook |
| **8 · I Don't Feel Well** | The body; at the pharmacy | *have* and *has*; how often | The sounds of *a* | A medicine label; a prescription | A bad week |
| **9 · What Should I Wear?** | Clothes; the weather | Present continuous; adjective + noun; *It's* + weather | *I* and *I'm* | A returns notice; a size chart | My first month |
| **10 · Where I Live** | Rooms and things; where it is | Possessive *'s*; prepositions of place | The strong syllable | A repairs notice; a room listing | My room |
| **11 · Around the City** | Places in the city; services | *What* and *Where* + present continuous; directions | /θ/ | A services page; a council page | My Thursday afternoon |
| **12 · I Can Do That** | Jobs; what you can do | *can* and *can't*; simple present at work | *can* and *can't* | A job notice; an application form | What I can do |
