Tomás cooks for four people on Sunday. He has a recipe from his grandmother, which says *some
rice* and *a little oil* and does not say how much of anything. At the market this is not a
problem. At the supermarket, with a trolley and a list, it is.

### 1 · *I don't* and *Do you?*

**1A  Listen to the conversation.** %%AUD W1-07-05%%

> **Tomás:** Do you have small bags of onions?
> **Hana:** Not today. We have big ones. Two kilos.
> **Tomás:** Two kilos! I don't need two kilos.
> **Hana:** Do you cook for a lot of people?
> **Tomás:** For four. On Sunday.
> **Hana:** Then take one kilo loose. I don't sell them loose, but for you I do.
> **Tomás:** Thank you. And do you have fresh fish?
> **Hana:** We don't sell fish. The stall next to us does.

**Your opinion:** Hana says *"I don't sell them loose, but for you I do."* Why does she do that?

**1B  Look at the conversation again. Underline *don't* and every question with *do*.**

**1C  Study the chart.**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | **don't** | eat fish. |
| he / she / it | **doesn't** | eat fish. |
| **Do** you / they | | eat fish? — Yes, I **do**. / No, I **don't**. |
| **Does** he / she | | eat fish? — Yes, he **does**. / No, he **doesn't**. |

> **Grammar Note** — After *doesn't*, the verb has **no -s**: *she doesn't eat*, never *she
> doesn't eats*. The *-s* is already in *doesn't*.

**1D  Practice with a partner. Make the sentence negative.**

1. I eat meat. → *I don't eat meat.*
2. She drinks milk. → ______________
3. They sell fish. → ______________
4. He cooks on Sunday. → ______________
5. We need rice. → ______________

### 2 · *some*, *any*, *a lot of*, *a little*

**2A  Study the chart.**

| | I count them | I do not count them |
|---|---|---|
| **+** | some eggs · a lot of eggs · a few eggs | some rice · a lot of rice · a little rice |
| **−** | I don't need **any** eggs. | I don't need **any** rice. |
| **?** | Do you have **any** eggs? · **How many** eggs? | Do you have **any** rice? · **How much** rice? |

> **Grammar Note** — **How many** + things you count. **How much** + things you do not count.
> You count *eggs* and *bottles*; you do not count *rice*, *milk*, *water* or *money*.

### 3 · The conversation

**3A  Read and listen.** %%AUD W1-07-10%%

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

In some places you choose your own fruit and put it in a bag. In others you point and the seller
chooses for you, and touching it yourself is rude. In some shops you weigh it; in others the till
does.

**Talk about it in groups.**

1. In your country, who picks up the fruit — you or the seller?
2. Where do you weigh vegetables — at the shelf or at the till?
3. You do it the wrong way and somebody tells you. How do you feel? What do you say?

> No right answer — but watch one person in front of you before you reach for anything.
