### Part 3 · Grammar and Dialogues

**Grammar: roles on a plant: who operates, who maintains, who authorises**

- Use I plus the verb for your own job. I operate the unit on the night shift.
- Use I do not for the job that is not yours. I do not authorise work on the pump.
- Use a name for the person who does it. Samer Oweis signs the permit.
- Use is plus the past form for the thing that happens. The permit is signed before work starts.

### Part 3 · Grammar and Dialogues

**Grammar: must, must not and you may: the rules of an induction**

- Use must for a rule with no choice. You must wear a hard hat in the plant.
- Use must not for a rule that never changes. You must not smoke inside the fence.
- Use may for something allowed. You may use a telephone in the office.
- Use must not for what is never allowed, not for what you dislike. A rule is not a preference.
