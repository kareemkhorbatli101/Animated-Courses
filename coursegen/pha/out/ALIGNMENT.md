# English for Pharmacists — the sixty-row alignment

Every unit is one AI capability, one grammar point and the precision faults its
Brief Lab diagnoses. The three are chosen so that each explains the others.

| ambiguous referent | 24 | reference, substitution and cohesion |
| missing condition | 13 | conditionals; unless, provided that, failing that |
| unstated exception | 13 | concession: although, notwithstanding, except where |
| hidden assumption | 13 | articles and attribution for given and new information |
