### Architecture

Each of the sixty units is built on the same twelve-part spine:

1. Opening spread — hero statement, strapline, can-do list
2. Warm-up questions
3. Reading passage with two data panels
4. Comprehension: short answer, true/false, gap-fill, choice, classification
5. Vocabulary — twenty field terms, a six-word figure, two exercises
6. Grammar — the unit's point, a loose-versus-precise figure, two exercises
7. Two dialogues, the second ending on an unresolved objection
8. Speaking tasks in pairs
9. Extended reading on the unit's AI capability, with a second data panel
10. Writing — the six-line tasking, with model, bank and checklist
11. **The Difficult Conversation** — a three-turn escalation with six holding phrases, a redline and a score
12. The Decision Room (case clinic), the Brief Lab, review and foundations

### Calibration, measured not asserted

- Mean sentence length within band: A2 8.5–12.5, B1 11.5–16.5, B2 15.0–21.0 words
- Sentence cap: A2 26, B1 34, B2 44 words; long sentences never above 5 per cent
- Word count per unit: A2 3,500–4,700, B1 4,000–5,200, B2 4,400–5,600
- Vocabulary: 1,200 distinct field terms, no term reused across the series
- Grammar: 60 distinct points, one per unit, each matched to its unit's title
- AI capabilities: 60 distinct capabilities, each with a stated boundary of what it cannot do

### Series gates (all passed)

- **Fault ladder across sixty Brief Labs** — unscoped set 31, unbounded quantity 30, unstated criterion 51, undefined done 13, ambiguous referent 12, missing condition 12, unstated exception 12, hidden assumption 19. Each lab carries exactly three fault types, two entries each, drawn only from the set available at its level.
- **Dissent** — in all sixty case clinics the manager, director or institution is wrong on the facts, and in all sixty the sceptic is right and the plan changes.
- **The Difficult Conversation** — sixty complete repairs, no holding phrase reused more than twice in the series.
- 23 per-book gates passed for each of the six books; diagnostics clean (no repeated gap stems, no duplicated prose, no thin or over-long fields).

### Editorial line on clinical content

These books teach clinical communication and clinical writing, not medicine. No dose, no regimen and no diagnostic rule appears anywhere in the series. Clinical detail is deliberately generic, every unit that touches a clinical decision says that the decision belongs to the treating clinician, and every data panel attributes guidance to general professional sources with the note that requirements differ between jurisdictions. Hospitals and clinicians are invented; cities are real.
