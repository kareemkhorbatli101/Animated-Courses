## The twenty-three per-book gates

Every one of the six books passes all twenty-three:

1. **Structure** — twelve parts present in all ten units, prose band per unit
2. **Answer-key integrity** — every exercise answer derivable from the unit
3. **Multiple-choice integrity** — no give-away distractors, no repeated stems
4. **True/false balance** — no set weighted beyond 2 of 6 either way
5. **Vocabulary** — 200 terms per book, none repeated, every gloss substantive
6. **Classify task** — four categories, six items, each category used
7. **Figure numbering and captions** — 153 figures, every one captioned
8. **Case clinic structure** — brief, exhibit, three voices, five steps, rule, outcome
9. **Register and density** — mean sentence length in band, over-cap under 1%
10. **Dialogue and task parity** — twelve lines per dialogue, six turns per task
11. **Data points sourced** — every panel attributed to law or local arrangements
12. **Continuity and names** — eleven named people, consistent roles throughout
13. **Grammar and skill ladder** — ten distinct points per book, none repeated
14. **Redundancy scan** — no sentence or stem reused across the book
15. **Precision of the model answers** — no vague word in any model answer
16. **No product, model or platform named** — no vendor anywhere in the series
17. **No capability forecast** — no claim about what tools will do in future
18. **Brief Lab integrity** — six-line draft, exactly three fault types × two
19. **Fault ladder** — only faults permitted at that level are diagnosed
20. **AI ladder** — ten distinct capabilities per book, none repeated
21. **Boundary line on every capability** — all sixty carry an explicit limit
22. **Dissent** — ten open-ended dialogues, ten clinics where a manager is wrong
23. **The Sentence You Must Say Before You Are Sure** — ten complete repairs
