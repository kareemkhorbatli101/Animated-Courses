### Part 3 · Grammar and Dialogues

**Grammar: roles in a department: who teaches, who marks, who advises**

- Use the present simple for a job somebody always does. Dr Marsh teaches the module. Mei Chen marks the first essays.

- Use does and do not for the office. The office does rooms. The office does not explain essay questions.
- Use can and cannot for help. Your tutor can explain the mark. The office cannot explain the mark.

### Part 8 · The Decision Room · One list, or eleven days

**STAGE 1 · The Brief**

A college asked 240 first-year students how they found answers in their first term.

In 94 cases the student made a list of who to ask in week one or two. In 146 cases the student did not.

Questions answered within two working days: 81 per cent and 34 per cent. Students who asked the department office a question about course content: 9 and 103. Students who waited more than a week for one answer: 7 and 88. Students who missed a deadline because they asked the wrong office: 1 and 23. Students who had met their personal tutor by week four: 90 of 94 and 61 of 146. Eleven students in the second group said they had stopped asking anybody.

One lecturer argues at every staff meeting that the table is an admission of failure. The office asks for it to be printed on the back of every handbook.

### Part 8 · The Decision Room · Left and right, or the red doors

A university rewrote the directions it gives new students. It compared two induction weeks, four years apart, on the same campus.
