TOEFL WRITING - 300 EXERCISES
Offline practice site for the updated TOEFL iBT Writing section
(three tasks, twelve items, about twenty-three minutes; in force from
21 January 2026).


HOW TO USE IT

Open index.html in any browser. That is the whole installation.

There is no server, no internet connection and no account. Nothing you
write leaves the machine you write it on. The package works from a hard
disk, a USB stick, a phone, or a school network with no outside access.

If your browser blocks local files for some reason, run

    python3 serve.py

in this folder and open the address it prints instead.


WHAT IS IN IT

  100 Build a Sentence exercises, in 12 grammar families
  100 Write an Email tasks
  100 Write for an Academic Discussion tasks
  ----
  300 exercises, one page each

  plus 12 family pages carrying the grammar rule and the questions that
  apply to all ten sentences in that family.

Every exercise has preview questions to answer before you look at
anything, a worked model, follow-up questions that take the model apart,
and a written key for every single question: 1,355 questions, 1,355
keys, around 2,200 answerable prompts once the lettered parts are
counted. None of them is left as an exercise for the reader.


HOW THE CHECKING WORKS

Word tiles are marked by the machine. Your sentence is compared with the
answer word by word, and the first word out of place is marked for you.
Check, Retry and Show answer do exactly what they say.

Every preview and follow-up question has a written key. You answer
first; the key appears when you press Check; you mark yourself Got it,
Partly or Missed it. Retry clears your answer and hides the key again.
Your marks set a review date - a missed question comes back tomorrow, a
partly-right one in three days, a solid one in ten.

Your own email or discussion response is NOT graded, because nothing
running offline can judge an argument and pretending otherwise would
teach you the wrong thing. What is checked is mechanical, and stated as
such: length against the task's floor, whether you opened and closed an
email properly, and - for a discussion - whether you actually engaged
both classmates by name. That last one is the same gate the book applies
to its own model answers. Then you compare your draft with the band-5
model, line by line.

Then you find the faults. Six statements are offered, three of which are
true of the band-3 version in front of you; the other three are faults
of a different task. That exercise is machine-marked too.


YOUR PROGRESS

Drafts, self-marks and completion are saved in this browser, per
exercise. They survive closing the tab. They do not travel between
browsers or devices, and clearing your browser data will erase them.

Use "Export progress" on the contents page to write a single JSON file,
and "Import progress" to load it somewhere else.


FINDING YOUR WAY

The contents page searches the hidden syllabus, not just the titles.
Every exercise carries four teaching targets - a strategy, a grammar
point, a vocabulary set and a convention - and the search box matches
all of them. Searching "subjunctive", "concede", "register" or "passive"
gives you every exercise that teaches it, which is something the printed
book cannot do.


LICENCE AND PROVENANCE

The exercises are original. The task formats, timings and scoring bands
follow the official ETS description of the updated test. The two scored
samples in the official overview are the basis for the band-5 and band-3
contrasts throughout: both carry errors, and what separates them is
whether an error damages meaning, not how many there are.
