# Teacher's book model

One per student-facing book. Written **unit by unit, immediately after the unit
it covers**, because writing the procedure is the fastest way to find out that a
task has no usable instruction, that a stage will not fit the hour, that a model
is missing, or that a figure does not support what the task asks. A unit is not
finished until its teacher notes exist and the defects they exposed are fixed.

---

## Front matter

- what the course is, and who it is for
- the methodology, in four pages: why preview precedes text, why the productive
  core is small, why items come back, what the AI page is for and what it is not
- the unit skeleton explained stage by stage, with timings
- how to run the role-plays, the Decision Room, the Brief Lab
- the mixed-group problem: practitioners and students in one room
- the assessment plan: what the progress tests cover and when
- the full syllabus map: unit × domain thread × function thread × grammar ×
  productive core × recycled items

## Per unit

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| Aims | mapped to CEFR descriptors and to the professional or academic task |
| Position | what this recycles, what it prepares, what must already be known |
| Routes | a 90-minute core route and a 180-minute full route, stage by stage, with a reduction option |
| Procedure | every stage, step by step, with the actual words of instruction suggested |
| Answers | the key, with accepted variants and why the wrong options are wrong |
| Transcripts | with the listening tasks mapped onto them, and the markers flagged |
| Difficulties | what learners get wrong here and what to do about it |
| Pronunciation | the unit's lexis: stress placement, especially on morphology |
| Figures | what each image is for, and how to exploit it beyond the task |
| Extension | fast finishers, stronger groups, weaker groups, one-to-one |
| Homework | and where the recycling lands |
| Domain notes | for the teacher who is not a clinician: what a hygienist may do, why this book gives no doses, what FDI notation is |

## Per two units

- a **progress test**: productive core, the grammar points, one listening, one
  reading, one short writing task, with a marking scheme and a cut score

## Back matter

- photocopiable resources: role cards, cue cards, labelling worksheets,
  flashcard sets, the substitution frames enlarged
- the full wordlist with definitions, by unit and alphabetically
- all transcripts
- the answer key to the progress tests
- a bibliography: what the teacher can read to go deeper

---

## Size

35–50k words for a practitioner title, 45–60k for a student title. That is
roughly 40–55% of the student book, which is the normal ratio for a teacher's
book of this type.

## The feedback loop, stated as a procedure

1. Write unit *n*.
2. Check it against the gates.
3. Write the teacher notes for unit *n*, including both timing routes.
4. Record every defect the notes exposed.
5. Fix the unit.
6. Only then write unit *n+1*.

The defect log is kept per book and summarised in the build record, so the
pattern of what goes wrong is visible across a series rather than fixed silently
unit by unit.
