# Publishability specification

What has to be true before a volume can be sold rather than merely printed.

## Front matter

1. Half-title and title page
2. Copyright page: edition, year, rights, image credits pointer, ISBN slot
3. **The disclaimer** (SOURCE_POLICY.md, verbatim)
4. How to use this book — the unit skeleton, one page, with a labelled spread
5. Contents, with unit titles and page numbers
6. **The syllabus map**: unit × domain thread × function thread × grammar ×
   productive core × recycled items × genres × can-do statements
7. CEFR level statement: what this level means, in plain terms

## The body

- running heads: book short title verso, unit title recto
- page numbers on every page
- figures numbered `FIG unit.sequence`, captioned, and **referenced by number**
  from the task or text that uses them
- tasks numbered continuously within the unit
- no orphaned heading, no stage split across a spread boundary mid-task
- every stage begins on a fresh block, with its stage name visible
- the preview is a visually distinct spread — it is the page a learner returns to

## Back matter

1. **Wordlist / glossary**: every productive and receptive item, with its
   definition, its unit, and its pronunciation note where it is not obvious
2. **Morphology table** [student series]: every word part taught, with meaning
   and examples
3. **Transcripts**: all three listenings per unit, numbered to their tasks
4. **Answer key**: every task, with accepted variants
5. **References**: Tier A sources, numbered, by unit
6. **Image credits**: every third-party figure, with licence and attribution
7. **Appendix plates**: the instrument plate, the notation map, the form
   annotations — the figures a learner will want to find again

## Accessibility

- alt text on every figure, written for a reader who cannot see it
- colour never the only carrier of meaning
- minimum type sizes for body, caption and label specified in the renderer
- every figure checked for greyscale legibility

## Build gates

A volume does not build unless: every figure has a caption, an alt text, a
cross-reference and a licence record; every Tier A claim has a register entry;
every task has rubric, example, model and checklist; every listening has a
transcript; every item in the wordlist has a definition; the contents, syllabus
map and page numbers regenerate cleanly; and the teacher's book for that volume
exists with notes for all ten units.
