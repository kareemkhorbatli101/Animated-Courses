# Domain inventory · Dentistry

The shared physical and procedural world of both dental series. The
practitioner series sequences it by the patient visit; the student series by the
curriculum. Level columns give **first productive appearance**; an item may be
met receptively earlier.

Abbreviations: **P** = practitioner series (A2.1–B2.2), **S** = student series
(A2.2–C1.1).

## Coverage gate

Each book declares the inventory sections it must cover. The domain-coverage
gate fails the build if a declared section has no productive item in the book,
and reports any inventory item that never appears anywhere in a series.
