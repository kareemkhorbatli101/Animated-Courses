# What a real coursebook does that this series does not

A scored comparison of this series against the Cambridge books you teach
from — **Complete IELTS Bands 4–5, 5–6.5 and 6.5–7.5**, and **Bridge to
IELTS** below them — across **72 factors in 9 categories**.

### How to read the benchmark column, and how far to trust it

The **Ours** column is measured: every score traces to `ielts/pedagogy.py`,
`ielts/census.py`, `ielts/gate10.py` or a named file in `ielts/`. You can
re-derive all of it.

The **Cambridge** column is **not measured**. It is my reading of the
published architecture of those books — the unit shape, the section
sequence, the apparatus and the back matter — and this container has no
copy to check against and no network access to one. You teach from them
every week, so **treat that column as a draft for you to correct.** Where
I have scored them generously or meanly, say so and the plan moves. I
have flagged with ⚠ the eleven rows where I am least sure.

Scores: **0** absent · **1** token or incidental · **2** present and
systematic · **3** present, systematic and better than the competition.

## The short answer to your question

**Yes — the Volume 3 plan is the right foundation, and it was missing the
single most important thing your Cambridge books do.**

The audit found the apparatus gaps: no contents, no previews, no
follow-up, thin grammar. The plan fixed those. What neither the audit nor
the plan had was Complete IELTS's **core teaching move**:

> **A skill is taught, and only then is the exam task set.**

In Complete IELTS, a Reading section does not open on a passage. It opens
on a short exercise that trains the *sub-skill* the upcoming task needs —
recognising a paraphrase, finding a signpost, deciding what "Not Given"
actually means — and the exam-style task is the *application* of what was
just taught. Our cycles have no such step anywhere: **the exam task is
the first and only thing.** That is why the books read as a test bank
with explanations rather than a course. It is factor 15 below, and it is
the most valuable thing in this document.

Everything else divides into three piles: **borrow** (34 factors),
**keep — we already win** (11 factors), and **neither series has it, and
one of them should** (6 factors).

## The eleven things we already do better

Do not let the rebuild damage any of these.

1. **A line for every wrong option** — 7,475, keyed to the text. No
   commercial IELTS book does this.
2. **A second model at band 6.5** with a list of what the band-9 answer
   does that it does not.
3. **Gated question-type coverage** — all 11 reading and 6 listening
   types taught as sets in every book, no type over its ceiling.
4. **Measured levelling** — a word ledger, a density gate, a syntax
   ladder. Cambridge grades by editorial judgement; we grade by
   measurement and can prove it.
5. **42 timed modules** — a full mock exam in every one of eight books.
6. **Both Academic and General Training in one book**, with Task 1
   forking properly.
7. **Band descriptors in plain language**, plus a raw-score-to-band page.
8. **Error correction** — "Find the fault", every unit.
9. **Grammar drawn from the text the learner has just read**, 46 of 50
   cycles.
10. **A critical-thinking spine** — every unit ends on a question about
    evidence. Complete IELTS rarely goes near this.
11. **Five cycles a unit.** One of our units is roughly five Complete
    IELTS units of material on one theme. That density is the whole point
    of your design and it is why a learner can actually move a band.

## The six things neither series has, and we should

1. **Paraphrase recognition as a trained, gated strand.** Complete IELTS
   drills it in exercises; nobody builds it as a systematic thread with a
   visual and a progression. It is the one skill that moves Reading and
   Listening together, and it is measurable.
2. **A text-structure visual for every long passage** — a skeleton
   showing what each paragraph *does*. Teaches the macro-skill that
   matching headings and matching information both test.
3. **"What this is for outside the exam"** — one line a cycle. Your
   stated goal, and factor 51 scores 0 and 1. A learner who knows that
   reading an eligibility notice for its exceptions is how you keep a
   tenancy will do it differently.
4. **Every visual declaring its teaching function in the data**, gated,
   so decoration is impossible.
5. **A diagnostic that routes the learner** — sit 20 questions, be told
   which four cycles to start at.
6. **The band-6.5-model idea extended to speaking and to reading
   decisions** — not just "here is a good answer" but "here is the answer
   one band below and the three moves that separate them".
