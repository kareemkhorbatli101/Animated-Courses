# Starting Point · Forty Minutes
%%SUB Two hundred and fifty words, four criteria, and the difference between a topic and a position%%
%%GOALS
Say what Task 2 asks for
Tell a topic from a position

Forty minutes. Two hundred and fifty words at least. One statement, and a question about it. You have
written essays before; what this book is about is the difference between writing about the subject
and answering the question.

### 1 · Six things you already have

%%FIG qe00-f1 | A single review sheet with six boxes. Each box has a heading — opinion language, reasons, examples, linking words, concession, and the conditional — and one short example in each.%%

**1A  Read the six examples aloud with a partner. Which two do you use most?**

| What you have | An example |
|---|---|
| Opinion language | *In my view* · *I would argue that* |
| Reasons | *because* · *since* · *one reason is that* |
| Examples | *for instance* · *a clear example of this is* |
| Linking words | *however* · *furthermore* · *on the other hand* |
| Concession | *although* · *admittedly* · *it is true that* |
| The conditional | *if this were the case* · *unless* |

**1B  Listen and repeat.** %%AUD PL5-00-01%%

### 2 · A topic, and a position

%%FIG qe00-f2 | Four panels in a row: a statement with a question beneath it, a topic written as a noun phrase, a position written as a full sentence with a verb, and a card reading one of these can be defended.%%

**2A  Read the four rows. Then say which column you would write at the top of your plan.**

| The question | The topic | The position |
|---|---|---|
| *Should university be free?* | university fees | *University should be free for first degrees only.* |
| *Do cities need cars?* | cars in cities | *Large cities should restrict private cars in their centers.* |
| *Is remote work better?* | working from home | *Remote work suits experienced staff and harms new ones.* |
| *Has technology made us lonelier?* | technology and loneliness | *It has changed how we are lonely, not how much.* |

**2B  Listen.** %%AUD PL5-00-02%%

### 3 · Two essays about the same statement

%%FIG qe00-f3 | A card taped inside a notebook, printed with two opening paragraphs one above the other, with a tick beside the first and a cross beside the second.%%

**3A  Read the two openings. Then say which one you could disagree with.**

> **✓** *Governments should fund public transport before building new roads, because a road that
> relieves congestion fills again within a decade while a rail line does not.*

> **✗** *Transport is a very important issue in modern society and there are many different opinions
> about it. In this essay I will discuss the advantages and disadvantages of public transport and
> roads.*

The second is better English than most candidates write, and it has taken a position on nothing.

### 4 · Forty minutes, spent

%%FIG qe00-f4 | A clock face divided into four unequal segments, labeled read and plan, write the introduction, write the body, and conclude and check, with the minutes marked on each.%%

**4A  Look at the four segments. Then discuss: where do your forty minutes actually go?**

| What | Minutes |
|---|---|
| Read the statement and plan | 6 |
| Write the introduction | 4 |
| Write the body | 24 |
| Conclude and check | 6 |

**4B  Listen and read.** %%AUD PL5-00-03%%

**4C  One question to finish.** In your last Task 2 essay, how many sentences could somebody have
disagreed with? Count them. If the answer is none, you wrote about the topic.
