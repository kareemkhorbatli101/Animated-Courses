# Starting Point · Twenty Minutes
%%SUB A hundred and fifty words, four criteria, and one distinction the whole book turns on%%
%%GOALS
Review the English you need to describe a figure
Say what Task 1 asks for and what it does not
Tell apart what a chart shows and what you have concluded
%%

Twenty minutes, at least a hundred and fifty words, and one piece of information to describe. The
hardest thing in the task is not the English. It is not saying anything the figure does not say.

### 1 · Six things you already have

%%FIG qd00-f1 | A single review sheet with six boxes. Each box has a heading — verbs of change, adverbs of degree, time expressions, comparison, proportion, and the passive — and one short example describing a chart.%%

**1A  Read the six examples aloud with a partner. Which two do you use most?**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Verbs of change** | Sales **rose**. Output **fell**. The figure **remained** stable. |
| **Adverbs of degree** | It rose **sharply** · **gradually** · **slightly**. |
| **Time expressions** | **Between** 1990 **and** 2000 · **in** the first decade · **by** 2010. |
| **Comparison** | Twice **as many as** · the **highest** of the four · **far more than**. |
| **Proportion** | **About a quarter** · **just over half** · **12 percent of** the total. |
| **The passive** | The sample **is placed** in water. The figures **were collected** in 1994. |

**1B  Listen and repeat.** %%AUD PL4-00-01%%

### 2 · What the task asks, and what it does not

%%FIG qd00-f2 | Four panels in a row, each showing one kind of task input: a line graph with two lines, a bar chart with four bars, a flow diagram of five stages, and a simple map of a town before and after.%%

**2A  Read the four rows. Then say what each one would need most.**

| What you may be given | What a description of it needs |
|---|---|
| A **graph or chart** | an overview, then the main movements and comparisons |
| A **table** | an overview, then the largest, the smallest and the pattern |
| A **process diagram** | the number of stages, the order, and what happens at each |
| A **map or a plan** | what was there, what is there now, and where |

The task asks you to **describe**, **summarize** and **compare where relevant**. It does **not** ask
you to explain why, to say whether the change was good, or to give your opinion. **A sentence that
does any of those three is a sentence that cannot be checked against the figure.**

**2B  Listen.** %%AUD PL4-00-02%%

### 3 · Two sentences about the same chart

%%FIG qd00-f3 | A card taped inside a notebook, printed with two sentences about one chart, one above the other, with a tick beside the first and a cross beside the second.%%

**3A  Read the two sentences. Then say which one you could check against the chart.**

> ✓ **"Output fell by about a third between 1994 and 1998, and then recovered to its earlier
> level."**
> *Every word of this is in the chart.*
>
> ✗ **"Output fell between 1994 and 1998 because of the recession, which shows that the industry was
> badly managed."**
> *The fall is in the chart. The recession, the cause and the judgment are not.*

The second sentence is better English and worse work. **Four criteria are applied to your answer and
the first of them asks whether you described what you were given.**

### 4 · Twenty minutes, spent

%%FIG qd00-f4 | A clock face divided into four unequal segments, labeled read and plan, write the overview, write the body, and check, with the minutes marked on each.%%

**4A  Look at the four segments. Then discuss: where do your twenty minutes actually go?**

| Minutes | What they are for |
|---|---|
| **3** | Read the figure. Find the two or three things that matter. |
| **2** | Write one overview sentence. |
| **13** | Write two body paragraphs with figures in them. |
| **2** | Check: every number right, nothing explained, nothing judged. |

**4B  Listen and read.** %%AUD PL4-00-03%%

**4C  One question to finish.** In your last Task 1 answer, how many sentences explained **why**
something happened?
