## How to use this book

This is the second book of six. It is about **one paper**: Listening. Forty questions, four parts,
played once, and about thirty minutes in which almost all of the damage is done by things that are
not vocabulary. Every unit is built on one real published document, named with its edition and its
date.

**Twelve units**, sixteen pages each, with the same six lessons in the same order every time.

| Lesson | What it does |
|---|---|
| **1 · Vocabulary** | Sixteen words, a second set of six, and a team research task |
| **2 · Reading** | One real published document, with its date and its limits |
| **3 · Grammar** | Two structures, with the mistake each one causes |
| **4 · In the Test** | Clear English, a dialogue, a culture check and a role-play |
| **5 · Writing** | A model, then your own |
| **Team Up & Review** | A picture, a team project, a survey, and one problem with no easy answer |

**The audio.** 🔊 marks a track. 195 tracks, numbered `PL2-unit-track`.

## What is different about this book

**It is about one paper, and about the sound of English.** Half the sources in this book are not
about tests at all. They are about how a stream of speech is cut into words, what happens to small
words when people talk quickly, what a listener does in the first eighty milliseconds of a word, and
why a lecture that signposts itself is easier to remember.

**Twelve real sources, and twelve limits.** A model of word recognition from 1978 that has since
been revised by its own author. A collection of misheard sentences that cannot tell you how often
mishearing happens. A study of accents whose authors warn you not to generalize from it. A
reliability coefficient from 1951 that its own inventor later wrote against. The point of naming the
limit is not modesty: it is that you cannot use a finding safely until you know its edge.

**The practice is diagnostic, not decorative.** Nothing in this book asks you to listen and then
check your answers. Every listening task asks a second question: **why did you not get it?** Book 1
Unit 5 gave you five reasons. This book spends twelve units making them precise.

**Nothing here is a sample of the test.** The recordings that come with this book were made for it.
For material that sounds like the examination there is one source: the test owners' own published
sample material. Use it, and nothing else, for that purpose.

**Money has no symbol in this series.** Every fee and figure is written without a currency sign,
because this book is used in many countries and the unit is whatever currency you pay in.

**No question, passage, recording or prompt from any past examination is reproduced.** Every practice
