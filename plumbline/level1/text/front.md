# Plumbline Foundation
%%SUB Academic English for IELTS · International Edition · Target band 4.5 · CEFR B1%%

## How to use this book

This is the first book of six. It teaches the English of **the test itself**: what the four papers
ask, how the marking works, what a band is, and how many words you need before any of it can help
you. Every unit is built on one real published document, named with its edition and its date.

**Twelve units**, sixteen pages each, with the same six lessons in the same order every time.

| Lesson | What it does |
|---|---|
| **1 · Vocabulary** | Sixteen words, a second set of six, and a team research task |
| **2 · Reading** | One real published document, with its date and its limits |
| **3 · Grammar** | Two structures, with the mistake each one causes |
| **4 · In the Test** | Clear English, a dialogue, a culture check and a role-play |
| **5 · Writing** | A model, then your own |
| **Team Up & Review** | A picture, a team project, a survey, and one problem with no easy answer |

**The audio.** 🔊 marks a track. 195 tracks, numbered `PL1-unit-track`.

## What is different about this book

**It starts with the document, not the trick.** Most preparation books tell you what examiners
want. This one shows you what the published papers actually say, prints the sentence in which they
say what they do **not** cover, and leaves the rest to you. There are no secrets in this book
because the test does not keep any that are written down.

**Every unit has one real source.** Not a summary of good advice: one document, named, dated, and
quoted at the point where its own authors state its limits. Eleven of this book's twelve units carry
a published document, paper or word list; the twelfth is a review.

**The sources are older than you expect.** A word list published in 1953. A German psychologist
counting his own forgetting in 1885. A framework written for Europe in 2001 and rewritten in 2020.
Language teaching did not begin with the app on your phone.

**Nothing in this book is a prediction.** A practice test tells you what you did on one morning with
one set of questions. It does not tell you what you will get. Unit 2 explains why, Unit 4 shows what
the published figures do and do not say, and the series comes back to it in the last unit of Book 6.

**The English is simple and the honesty is not.** At this level the sentences are short. What they
say is exact: which document, which edition, what it covers, and what it leaves to you.

**Money has no symbol in this series.** Every fee and figure is written without a currency sign — *a
fee of 240*, *a rise of 15* — because this book is used in many countries and the unit is whatever
currency you pay in. Where a figure matters, the text says what it is a figure of.

## The people

One preparation center and one class, in every unit of all six books.

**Meridian Language Center** is an independent preparation center of about forty teachers. It
prepares adults for the Academic test. **Priya Raman** teaches the class in this book and is the
voice you will hear most. **Grace Mwangi** is the academic manager. **Lars Eriksen** is the center's
assessment coordinator: he keeps the records, builds the practice papers and is the person who says,
every time, that a practice band is not a band.

The class: **Junseo Park**, an engineer who needs 6.5. **Nour Khalil**, who needs 7.0 for a medical
register. **Marek Nowak**, who has 6.0 and needs 6.5 in Writing only. **Mei Lin**, the strongest
reader and the quietest speaker. **Yusuf Demir**, the strongest speaker and the weakest writer.
**Ines Oliveira**, who needs 6.0 overall with nothing under 5.5. **Oksana Hryn**, a nurse who needs
7.0 in all four. **Samira Aziz**, who is taking the test for the first time. **Tomas Varga**, who
learned three answers by heart and found out what that costs. **Hana Sato**, who scores higher in
practice than on the day.

**Chiamaka Obi** joined four weeks ago. She has never taken the test, she has read nothing about it,
and she asks the questions this book is an answer to.

The universities and employers — **Ashworth University**, **Calderbrook Institute of Technology**,
**Northvale College**, **Harrowgate School of Nursing** and **Pennine Regional Hospital** — are
invented, on purpose. The band each one asks for in this book is invented too. **Ask the real one.**
