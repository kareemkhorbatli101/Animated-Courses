English for the Hospitality Industry

Ten units for people who work in hotels, restaurants and cafés — reception, housekeeping, food service, the concierge desk and the duty manager's office. The course is set in the Orient Rose Hotel in Damascus, and the English goes from CEFR A2 in Unit 1 to B1 by Unit 10.
