# Starting Point · Editions, Status and the Reader Outside
%%SUB Which edition, is it still current, and who outside the building reads this%%
%%GOALS

Name the reader who is not a colleague

Four of this company's standing answers cite documents that have been withdrawn, superseded or
confirmed under another number. Nobody was careless. Nobody checked, because a citation is the one
part of a document that looks permanent.

### 1 · Six structures you already have

%%FIG f00-f1 | A single review sheet with six boxes. Each box has a heading — hedged attribution, commencement verbs, the passive with an agent restored, guidance verbs, intended use, and reporting conditions — and one short example sentence from a governance note.%%

**1A  Read the six examples aloud with a partner. Which two do you use most?**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hedged attribution** | **According to** its own framing, the framework is voluntary. |
| **Commencement** | The prohibitions **apply from** 2 February 2025. |
| **The agent restored** | **Hana Sato** maintains the register, monthly. |
| **Guidance verbs** | A model **should** be evaluated on deployment-period data. |
| **Intended use** | It **was not evaluated on** anything about individuals. |
| **Reporting conditions** | Recall 41 per cent **at a threshold of** 0.5, **baseline** 98.5 per cent. |

**1B  Listen and repeat.** %%AUD KS6-00-01%%

### 2 · Two questions about any citation

%%FIG f00-f2 | Two boxes in a column, each with a question in bold, three lines of smaller type under it, and an arrow at the foot pointing to a box reading four of our standing answers cite documents that no longer exist in the form we cite them.%%

**2A  Read. Then ask your partner both questions about a document they quote.**

> **1  Which edition, and is it still current?**
> *A standard can be amended, confirmed, superseded or withdrawn. The number stays the same and
> the content does not. An edition year is part of the citation, not decoration.*
>
> **2  Who outside this building is going to read this sentence?**
> *A procurement officer scoring you. A researcher who has found something. An auditor. A colleague
> in nine years. None of them can ask you what you meant.*

**2B  Listen.** %%AUD KS6-00-02%%

### 3 · Four kinds of status a standard can have

%%FIG f00-f3 | A card taped inside a laptop lid, printed with four short status descriptions in bold with one line of smaller type under each, and a line at the foot reading the number does not change when the content does.%%

**3A  Read the four. Then say which one you have never checked.**

| Status | What it means for your citation |
|---|---|
| **current** | the edition you are citing is the published one |
| **confirmed** | reviewed and kept as it is — still current, with a new review date |
| **superseded** | a newer edition exists; yours is the old text |
| **withdrawn** | no longer published; citing it is citing nothing |

**3B  Ask your partner: which document does your company cite most, and when was it last checked?**

### 4 · Two readers, one sentence

%%FIG f00-f4 | Two columns side by side. The left column is headed written for a colleague and lists three lines; the right column is headed written for somebody outside and lists three different lines; an arrow at the foot points to a box reading the second one cannot ask you anything.%%

**4A  Read the two columns. Then discuss: which does your company send out?**

| Written for a colleague | Written for somebody outside |
|---|---|
| We follow the secure development framework. | We implement 9 of its 19 practices; the other 10 are listed. |
| Our components are documented. | A machine-readable list, regenerated per build, with 14 unresolved entries. |
| That vulnerability does not affect us. | Not affected, because the vulnerable function is never reached; reasoning below. |

Each sentence on the left is true and was written to end a conversation. Each sentence on the right
was written for somebody who will quote it in a document you never see.

**4B  Listen and read.** %%AUD KS6-00-03%%

**4C  One question to finish.** Which document does your company cite most, and has anybody opened it
this year?
