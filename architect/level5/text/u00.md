# Starting Point · Four Kinds of Authority
%%SUB What can require you, what can certify you, and what has no power at all%%
%%GOALS
Review the grammar of Books 1 to 4
Tell four kinds of document apart
Ask what a document can and cannot require
%%

Four documents are open on the table. One of them can be enforced against your company on a date
already fixed. One of them cannot require anything of anybody and never claimed to. Telling them
apart is the whole of this book.

### 1 · Six structures you already have

%%FIG e00-f1 | A single review sheet with six boxes. Each box has a heading — the past perfect, cause and result, the second conditional, impossibility, conditions and provisos, and reference words — and one short example sentence from an architecture review.%%

**1A  Read the six examples aloud with a partner. Which two do you use most?**

| | |
|---|---|
| **The past perfect** | We chose it because we **had already committed** to the platform. |
| **Cause and result** | Four teams, **which is why** there are four services. |
| **The second conditional** | **If** the network **were** reliable, retries would be unnecessary. |
| **Impossibility** | Both **cannot be guaranteed** in an asynchronous system. |
| **Conditions and provisos** | You may redistribute, **provided that** four conditions are met. |
| **Reference words** | **This declaration** is what we conform to. |

### 2 · Four kinds of authority

%%FIG e00-f2 | Four boxes in a column. Each one names a kind of document, says who can hold you to it, says what happens if you ignore it, and gives one example sentence from its own first pages, with an arrow at the foot pointing to a box reading they are not interchangeable.%%

**2A  Read. Then say the four kinds to your partner in your own words.**

| The kind | Who can hold you to it | If you ignore it |
|---|---|---|
| **A voluntary framework** | nobody | nothing, formally |
| **A regulation** | a regulator, on a date fixed in law | enforcement |
| **A management system standard** | an auditor you hired, within a scope you declared | you lose a certificate |
| **A paper proposing a form** | nobody at all | nothing — and yet everyone uses it |

The second row is the only one with a date in it. The fourth row has no authority whatsoever and is
the row most often quoted in a customer questionnaire.

**2B  Listen.** %%AUD KS5-00-02%%

### 4 · Two ways of answering a customer

%%FIG e00-f4 | Two columns side by side. The left column is headed what gets said and lists three lines; the right column is headed what can be shown and lists three different lines; an arrow at the foot points to a box reading one of these can be checked.%%

**4A  Read the two columns. Then discuss: which does your company send?**

| What gets said | What can be shown |
|---|---|
| We follow the AI framework. | We use its four functions to organize our own register. |
| We are compliant with the regulation. | These obligations apply to us from this date. |
| We are certified. | Certified against this declared scope, by this body, in this year. |

Nothing in the left column is a lie. Each line is a sentence somebody wrote in good faith on a Friday
afternoon, and not one of them survives a second question.
