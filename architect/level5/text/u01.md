> is a problem.

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

The most quoted documents in this field are the ones with the least authority. A voluntary framework
cannot require anything, a research paper proposing a form has no standing at all, and both get
cited in contracts. The reason is simple and not cynical: somebody is asked a question they cannot
answer, they look for the most official-sounding document in the area, and they borrow its name. The
answer that follows is not dishonest; it is a sentence written to end an email. What makes it costly
is that it travels. A customer puts it on a slide, the slide becomes a requirement in their next
contract, and two years later a question arrives that treats your sentence as a commitment you made
deliberately. The habit that prevents all of this takes fifteen seconds: read the first pages of the
document, find the sentence where it describes its own status, and quote that sentence before you
quote anything else in it.
