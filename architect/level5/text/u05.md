# Unit 5 · What the Model Is Not For
%%SUB A one-page form from a research paper, and the section everybody leaves blank%%
%%GOALS

In 2019 a group of researchers proposed a one-page form to accompany a trained model. It has no
legal force, no standards number and no mechanism to check anything. It is used by companies and
public bodies all over the world, and the section that matters most is the one asking what the model
is **not** for.

**1A  Listen and read. Then answer the question below.** %%AUD KS5-05-06%%

> **Chiamaka:** I have read your three completed pages. The out-of-scope sections are good and one of
> them is going to cause an argument.
> **Hana:** The staffing one. I know.
> **Chiamaka:** Walk me through how you worded it, because the wording is the whole fight.
> **Hana:** Three different sentences doing three different jobs. **The factual one first: *this model
> was not evaluated on passenger services or on data from outside the eastern region*.** That is
> unarguable. It is a fact about what we did.
> **Chiamaka:** Nobody can object to a fact.
> **Hana:** Nobody can, and that is why it goes first. **Then the inference: *performance outside
> those conditions is unknown, not assumed to be similar*.** Still not a restriction, just honest.
> **Chiamaka:** And then the restriction.
> **Hana:** Then the restriction, and it is one sentence: **_this model should not be used to support
> decisions about individual members of staff_**. That is the one that will be argued with, and
> putting the two honest sentences in front of it means the argument happens in the right place.
> **Chiamaka:** Which is where?
> **Hana:** About whether the restriction is right, rather than about whether I am exaggerating the
> limitation. **If I lead with *should not be used*, the first question is whether I am being

### 🌐 CULTURE CHECK

A documentation page that only describes what a system does functions as a permission, whatever its
authors intended. Readers are not careless; they read a capability and use the capability, and
nothing on the page tells them which conditions the numbers came from or which applications were
never tested. This is why the one section that gives something up — what the model is not for — is
also the section that is almost always empty: eight sections are a writing task that one person can
finish in an afternoon, and that one is a telephone call to a team already relying on the output. The
honest order matters as much as the content. Lead with what you did not evaluate, then with what is
therefore unknown, then with the restriction; and name somebody who can lift the restriction in
writing. A restriction with no exception route does not stop the use. It makes the use invisible,
which is where the whole problem began.
