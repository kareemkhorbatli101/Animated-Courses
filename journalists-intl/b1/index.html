English for International Journalists · B1

Ten units in two tracks, set at Crosswire, an international news agency with bureaux in ten cities. The Operator track is for reporters in their first months; the Practitioner track is for reporters and editors with a few years' experience. Both use B1 English — the Practitioner parts add harder decisions and editorial judgement, not harder language.

Every unit carries a Field Focus on a different dateline, and a Your Newsroom in Focus part where the ground is your own: you describe the newsroom you work in and compare it with the others.
