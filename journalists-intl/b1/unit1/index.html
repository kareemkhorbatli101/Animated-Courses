What you can do at the end of this unit

Operator track

  • I can name the jobs in a newsroom and the things a newsroom works with, describe how news is made where I live, and talk about a story I followed recently.
  • I can use this unit’s newsroom roles and craft words correctly, tell a field job from a desk job and say why, and write a short paragraph using at least five of them.
  • I can tell a routine from something happening now, choose present simple or present continuous for each, correct the commonest mistake between the two, and say both what I usually cover and what I am working on this week.
  • I can predict what a new reporter says on a first day, follow three newsroom conversations closely enough to judge True, False or Not Given, and match each person to what they are doing at the moment.
  • I can introduce myself with my name, my role, my beat and my current story, take either part in a professional exchange, and ask the questions that close an information gap.
  • I can read a news agency’s profile for its standing facts and its current plans, say which tense carries which, and describe a colleague’s work from a short staff profile.
  • I can recognise the parts of a pitch email and what each is for, complete one from a word list, and write my own in 50 to 70 words.
  • I can use this unit’s roles, craft words and tenses again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.

Practitioner track

  • I can name the jobs in a newsroom and the things a newsroom works with, describe how news is made where I live, and talk about a story I followed recently.
  • I can brief a new reporter as an editor does: use the phrases that put a new starter at ease, work through an induction checklist in order, and set out a first week.
  • I can read how an agency hands a story from one shift to the next, take the vocabulary of the handover from it, and set out my own view in writing.
  • I can read what a bureau chief faces at a morning conference, weigh which story should lead, and defend the order I choose.
  • I can use this unit’s roles, craft words and tenses again without prompting, and tick honestly what I can now do.
