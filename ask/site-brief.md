## How the courses work

- Everything runs in the browser. There is nothing to install and no account to make.
- A course works offline, from a folder or a USB stick, with no server and no internet. Only the recordings and the pictures are fetched from the web, and a copy that carries its own media plays without a connection.
- The spoken lines are pre-recorded, so every learner hears the same words in the same voice.
- Answers are the author's own, taken from the book the course is built from, and checked against it automatically.
