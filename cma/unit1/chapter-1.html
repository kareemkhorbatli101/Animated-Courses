The people behind the reports

Financial statements help people decide whether to invest, lend, or provide credit. Investors, lenders, and other creditors are the primary users of general-purpose financial statements.

◉

Investors

Consider buying, holding, or selling shares; focus on future cash flows and returns.

↗

Lenders

Decide whether to lend or renew a loan; focus on interest and principal payments.

▤

Other creditors

Set credit terms; consider whether the company can pay its bills when due.

Managers, employees, customers, and governments may also use information. Managers are internal users; the framework identifies investors, lenders, and other creditors as the primary users.
