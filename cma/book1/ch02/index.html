The Balance Sheet

قائمة المركز المالي (الميزانية العمومية)

In Chapter 1 you learned the accounting equation and saw Barada Wholesale's trial balance. Now we turn a full set of accounts into the first of the four statements: the balance sheet. It is a picture of the company on one day. It shows what the company controls, what it owes, and what belongs to its owners.

What you already know

Under IFRS you call it the statement of financial position, and many IFRS balance sheets put noncurrent assets first. U.S. companies usually start with current assets and list them in order of liquidity: cash first. The rules for classifying debt are also different, as Section 2.3 shows.
