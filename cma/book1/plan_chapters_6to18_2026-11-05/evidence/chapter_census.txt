   5 of the thirteen fit the default.
   6 chapters have MORE than six: 6 section pages would be built and silently dropped.
   2 chapters have FEWER than six: the generator raises KeyError on a page id it invented.

   stops. Too many is SILENT - the chapter ships missing a section, and no gate
   counts section pages against the book.
