   READ `lines` AS 'UNDECIDED', NOT AS 'A READING SCREEN'. It is the fallback
   bucket: a label-and-amount table that none of the shape rules above claimed.
   Each one becomes an EXERCISE chosen when its section is authored - most often
   a `number` to enter or a `waterfall` to add up - and the standard that no
   screen is scored on looking applies to every one of them. The count here says
   how many decisions remain, not how many reading screens there will be.
