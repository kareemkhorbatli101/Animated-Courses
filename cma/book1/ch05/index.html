The Statement of Cash Flows

قائمة التدفقات النقدية

Chapter 3 ended with a surprise: Orontes earned far less profit than cash. This chapter explains that difference. The statement of cash flows shows where cash came from and where it went, and it completes the set of four statements.

What you already know

IFRS uses the same three sections, but IAS 7 lets companies choose where to put interest and dividends. U.S. GAAP gives no choice. If you learned the IFRS habit of putting interest paid in financing, you must unlearn it for this exam.
