CMA PART 1 - BOOK 1
External Financial Reporting Decisions
Chapter 1: The Language and Framework of Financial Reporting


TO START
--------
Unzip this folder anywhere, then open index.html in your browser.
Double-clicking it is enough. There is nothing to install.

No internet connection is needed. No web server is needed. Nothing is
sent anywhere.


WHAT IS HERE
------------
Chapter 1, complete, as thirteen linked pages:

  7 numbered sections  1.1 to 1.7, every box and figure the book prints
  Chapter summary      the key rule from each of the seven topics
  Term bank            all 80 terms, English and Arabic, with drills
  Practice             the 16 exam-style questions, P01 to P16
  Case question        Barada Wholesale, in the exam's own item formats
  Mixed review         items from every section, interleaved
  Answers              every answer, with the reason for every option

133 scored exercises. 13 interactive figures. 80 bilingual terms.

Chapters 2 to 18 are listed on the home page so the shape of the book is
visible. They are written and frozen for review in the source book, and
each will build out to the same thirteen pages.


HOW THE EXERCISES WORK
----------------------
Every exercise has "Check Answers" and "Try Again".

  Try Again re-shuffles the exercise and clears your marks. It never
  reveals the answer as a shortcut.

  Answers are shuffled every time. In any matching or sorting exercise
  there are always more tiles than places to put them, so the last one
  is never a free guess.

  When you are wrong, the feedback begins with the reason for the option
  YOU chose, taken from the book's own explanations - not a generic
  "incorrect".

  Drag and drop always has a click path: tap a tile, then tap where it
  goes. Everything works from the keyboard too - Tab to a tile, Enter to
  pick it up, Tab to a target, Enter to drop it.


PRONUNCIATION
-------------
The speaker icons use the voices already installed on your computer.

On Windows, Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge both expose them directly,
and Edge usually has the better voices.

If an Arabic icon is dimmed, no Arabic voice is installed on this
computer. To add one:

  Settings -> Time & language -> Speech -> Add voices -> Arabic

Then restart the browser. A voice picker appears at the foot of each
chapter page when there is more than one voice to choose from.


YOUR PROGRESS
-------------
Progress is saved in this browser, on this computer, and nowhere else.
It is not uploaded and it does not follow you to another machine.

The foot of any chapter page has a button to clear it.

If you open the pages in a private or incognito window, the browser may
refuse to save anything. The site still works; it just forgets.


KEYBOARD
--------
  Left arrow / Right arrow   previous and next page
  Tab / Enter                operate any drag-and-drop exercise
  Ctrl+P                     print the page as a clean worksheet
                             (the practice and case pages print well)


LANGUAGES
---------
Terms are English and Arabic. The reasoning, the questions and the
feedback stay in English, because the exam is in English and the English
is the thing being taught.

There are no French exercises. The book's False-Friend boxes still warn
about French words such as "resultat", "charges" and "la balance",
because those warnings are part of what the chapter teaches.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
Open _qa.html in the same folder. It loads every page's real exercise
definitions, renders all of them, and reports 59 checks: coverage of the
chapter, the exercise contract, every number in the chapter recomputed
from a single ledger, the bilingual terms, and the offline constraints.

It should read "All 59 checks pass".


CREDITS
-------
Source book: External Financial Reporting Decisions, Chapters 1-18,
version 1.3, by Mohamad Khorbatli.

Covers the ICMA Learning Outcome Statements for CMA Part 1, Section A,
effective September 1, 2024, in graded English (B1+ to B2).

From the September/October 2026 testing window the exam has 100
multiple-choice questions and two case-based questions. The case page
mirrors the CBQ item formats: drag and drop, numeric entry, select from
a list, and fill in the blank.
