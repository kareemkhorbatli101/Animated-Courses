Quality gate

Loads every scene definition in the chapter, renders all of them, and checks the result. The provenance lock is the important one: nothing may be taught that is not in the author's chapter.

Three further checks run when the package is built, because a page opened from file:// cannot read its neighbours: every internal link resolves, nothing points at http(s)://, and no file uses fetch() or an ES module.
