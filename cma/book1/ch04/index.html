Equity and the Statement of Changes in Equity

حقوق الملكية وقائمة التغيرات في حقوق الملكية

Chapters 2 and 3 showed where Orontes stands and how it performed. This chapter looks at the owners' part of the balance sheet. It explains how equity grows when owners invest and when the company earns profits, and how it falls when the company pays dividends or buys back its shares.

What you already know

Under IFRS you may have learned different names for the parts of equity, such as share capital and share premium. U.S. GAAP says common stock, additional paid-in capital, retained earnings and accumulated other comprehensive income. The False Friend box in Section 4.4 compares the words.
