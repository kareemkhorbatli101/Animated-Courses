Appendix B

Index of terms

Each term points to the section that defines it. Terms marked BRIDGE appear in one of the chapter's Term Bridge boxes; the rest are defined in the prose.

The glossary gives the Arabic equivalent and the meaning of every term, with pronunciation in both languages. The term bank of Chapter 1 and of Chapter 2 drills them.
