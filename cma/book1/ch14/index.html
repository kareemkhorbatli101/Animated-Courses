Leases

عقود الإيجار

Many companies use buildings, machines and vehicles that they do not own. Before 2019, most of these leases were hidden in the notes. Today a lessee reports almost every lease on its balance sheet. This chapter explains how.

What you already know

If you learned IFRS 16, you know one lessee model: every lease is like a purchase financed by a loan. U.S. GAAP keeps two models. The balance sheet looks almost the same, but the income statement and the cash flow statement can be very different.
