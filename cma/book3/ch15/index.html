Responsibility Centers, Contribution Reporting and Segments

مراكز المسؤولية وتقارير هامش المساهمة والقطاعات

Chapters 11 to 14 measured what happened. This chapter asks a different question: who should answer for it? A plant manager who cannot set prices should not be judged on revenue, and a division charged a share of head office cannot make that charge go away. Good performance reporting starts by matching the measure to the authority.
