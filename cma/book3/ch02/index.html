Analysing the Environment and Choosing a Strategy

تحليل البيئة واختيار الاستراتيجية

Chapter 1 set the goals of the Orontes plan for 2028 to 2032, such as raising export sales. Before the board can agree how to reach them, it needs a clear picture of the markets outside the company and of what the company does well inside it. This chapter builds that picture and turns it into a choice of strategy.
