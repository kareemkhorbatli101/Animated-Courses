Overhead Variances and Acting on Variances

انحرافات التكاليف الصناعية غير المباشرة والتصرف بناءً على الانحرافات

Materials and labor are now explained. Overhead is harder, because part of it changes with activity and part does not, and because fixed overhead is charged to products at a rate that assumes a particular volume. This chapter completes the June analysis and then asks what the division should actually do about it.
