Critical Success Factors, KPIs and the Balanced Scorecard

عوامل النجاح الحاسمة ومؤشرات الأداء وبطاقة الأداء المتوازن

Book 3 began with a strategy and a five-year plan. It then built a budget, measured the results against it, and asked who should answer for each number. This last chapter closes the circle. It shows how a company turns its strategy into a small set of measures, and how it reads them together instead of one at a time.
