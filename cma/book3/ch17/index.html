Return on Investment, Residual Income and Measurement Issues

العائد على الاستثمار والدخل المتبقي وقضايا القياس

Chapter 15 made the Beverages division an investment center, which means its manager answers for profit and for the assets used to earn it. One number must therefore combine the two. This chapter builds that number two ways, shows where each one misleads, and ends with the accounting policies that quietly change both.
