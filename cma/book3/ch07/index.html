Direct Materials, Direct Labor and Overhead Budgets

موازنات المواد المباشرة والعمل المباشر والتكاليف الصناعية غير المباشرة

The production budget of Chapter 6 says how many cases Beverages will fill each quarter. It does not yet say how much concentrate to order, how many hours the operators will work, or what the plant will cost to run. This chapter answers those questions with three budgets built on the Book 2 standard cost card.
