Mix, Yield and Sales-Mix Variances; Variances in Service Companies

انحرافات المزيج والمردود ومزيج المبيعات؛ الانحرافات في شركات الخدمات

Chapters 11 and 12 analysed one product and one input at a time. Most divisions sell several products and blend several inputs. A division can sell exactly the budgeted number of units and still miss its profit, because it sold the wrong ones. This chapter measures that effect, on the selling side and on the production side.
