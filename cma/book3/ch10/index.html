Pro Forma Statements and Financing Needs

القوائم المالية التقديرية واحتياجات التمويل

Nine chapters of budgets end in three statements. The board does not read the materials purchases budget; it reads the profit the plan will earn, the assets it will need and the cash it will produce. This chapter builds those statements and answers the last question of the planning round: will Orontes need to raise money?
