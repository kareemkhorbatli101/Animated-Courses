Performance Against the Budget: Flexible Budgets and Sales Variances

قياس الأداء مقابل الموازنة: الموازنات المرنة وانحرافات المبيعات

Part II planned the year. Part III now asks a harder question: when the results arrive, what do they tell us? In June 2028, Beverages planned 75,000 cases and an operating income of $265,000. It sold 72,000 cases and earned $247,632. This chapter explains how much of that gap came from volume, and how much from everything else.
