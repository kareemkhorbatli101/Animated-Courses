Transfer Pricing

التسعير التحويلي

Levant Distribution delivers every case of juice that Beverages sells. Both are part of Orontes, so the price Levant charges Beverages is an internal matter: it moves profit from one division to the other and changes nothing for the company. Yet it changes how each manager behaves, and the wrong price can lead a manager to a decision that costs the company money.
