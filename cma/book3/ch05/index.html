Budget Systems II: Zero-Based, Rolling and Flexible Budgets

أنظمة الموازنة (2): الموازنة الصفرية والموازنة المستمرة والموازنة المرنة

Head-office costs at Orontes have grown every year for five years, and nobody can say exactly why. The juice market changes every month, but the annual budget is fixed in December. And when Beverages sells fewer cases than planned, the plant manager is blamed for costs that fell with volume. This chapter shows three budget systems that deal with these problems.
