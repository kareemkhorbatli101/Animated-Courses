The hand-written Workshop handouts for Book 1 Chapter 7.

These are kept because they are better writing than a generator produces: the
cost-layer cycle, the LIFO reserve trace and the two-year error walk were each
composed for the idea they teach. They are not built any more, because they
predate three rules that now hold for every sheet — no open questions, a
preview page that fills page one, and no stem that points at something it does
not print — and because their block signatures changed when those rules came
in.

The chapter they were written for is now generated, which is what makes seven
chapters comparable. Bringing any of this back means rewriting its items as
multiple choice and adding a preview page; the teaching in it is still sound.
