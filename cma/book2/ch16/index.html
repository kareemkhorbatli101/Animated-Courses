Continuous Improvement and the Cost of Quality

التحسين المستمر وتكاليف الجودة

In 2027 the Beverages division spent $400,000 on defects its customers found: returns, complaints and customers who stopped buying. It spent only $120,000 on stopping defects in the first place. This chapter measures the cost of quality and shows why that balance was wrong.
