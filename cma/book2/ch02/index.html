Cost Behavior and the Relevant Range

سلوك التكلفة والمدى الملائم

The Oils division expects to run its crushers for 38,400 machine hours in 2027. What if a good harvest pushes that higher, or a drought pulls it lower? Some costs will move with the hours and some will not. Managers must know which, before they can budget, price or control.
