THE ONE DIFFERENCE FROM BOOK 1: THE AUTHOR'S ID PREFIX
======================================================================================
Book 2 namespaces every id with a leading 2, inserted before the two-digit
chapter number. Nothing else about the scheme changes.

   figure   F07-01
   question SC7-1 / P7-01
   case     C7-1
   caption  Figure F07-01. Chapter 7 roadmap: goods available for sale split into cost of goods sold and end

   figure   F207-01
   question SC207-1 / P207-01
   case     C207-1
   caption  Figure F207-01. Chapter 7 roadmap: costs follow the product through departments.

CONSEQUENCE. `figure_tables` searches for `F%02d-\d\d` % n, so on Book 2 it
matches NOTHING and returns an empty dict - which a count reads as a clean zero,
not as a failure. Any Book 2 census must assert a POSITIVE count per chapter.
