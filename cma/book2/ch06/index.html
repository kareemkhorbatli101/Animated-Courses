Job Order Costing

Orontes's Tahini & Spreads division makes private-label tahini and halva for supermarkets such as Barada Wholesale. Each order has its own recipe, jar and label. The controller must know what each order costs, so that she can price the next one and value the inventory.

What you already know

In Book 1 you valued inventory at cost and moved cost to cost of goods sold when goods were sold. Chapter 1 of this book added raw materials, work in process and finished goods. Chapter 5 introduced normal costing: actual direct costs plus overhead applied at a predetermined rate. Job order costing puts these pieces together for one order at a time.
