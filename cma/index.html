HOW THESE BOOKS ARE BUILT

The book is the source of truth

Each chapter is read straight out of the author's manuscript, and the site is held against that reading word for word rather than against anybody's transcription of it. The figures on every page recompute from the chapter's own ledger on each load, so a number cannot drift from the book it came from.

2 of the four books are built: 2,063 exercises over 480 pages, with 210 drawn figures. Each book keeps its own saved progress in this browser, so resetting one leaves the others alone.
