Corporate Governance: the Board, the Officers and the Hierarchy

حوكمة الشركات: مجلس الإدارة والمسؤولون والتسلسل الهرمي

Chapter 1 ended with the control environment, and said that accountability is one of its elements. This chapter asks the obvious next question: accountable to whom? The answer is a chain that runs from the shareholders who own the company to the clerk who follows a procedure.
