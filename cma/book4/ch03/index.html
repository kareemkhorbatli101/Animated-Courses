Control Activities: Segregation of Duties and Safeguarding

أنشطة الرقابة: الفصل بين المهام وحماية الأصول

Chapter 1 named the five components and said that control activities are the actions that address a risk. This chapter is about those actions. They are the least glamorous part of internal control and the part that does most of the daily work.
